Sits on House Energy and Commerce Committee (subcommittees: Health; Energy; Commerce, Manufacturing, and Trade) and House Agriculture Committee. 8th District includes eastern King County suburbs, all of Pierce County's eastern portions, the Cascade Range crossing, and rural central Washington (Wenatchee, Ellensburg).

Who they are - **Verdict:** Schrier has real federal policy power through her Energy and Commerce seat (the Health subcommittee matters most for a doctor) and her Agriculture seat (it matters for the rural half of her district). She also leads the Democratic Doctors Caucus and the New Dems' Rural Revitalization Working Group, which gives her extra pull inside her party. Her district (WA-8) covers eastern Pierce County. Federal money flows from her committee work into Pierce County through health programs (CMS), farm research (USDA), and energy projects (DOE — the U.S. Department of Energy). She won re-election in 2024 by 8.2 points (54.0%–45.8%). She is safe but not unbeatable. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** As a Type 1 diabetic and doctor, her policy work on insulin prices, ACA protections for pre-existing conditions, and FDA rules hits her own life. Congress has no formal conflict-of-interest rule beyond financial-disclosure forms. Her health policy views match her own medical history, which is public. Her Agriculture seat helps the rural side of WA-8 (Wenatchee/Ellensburg). The suburban (Issaquah/Sammamish/eastern Pierce) and rural parts of the district sometimes want different things. That tension is built into her job. - **Other view:** A skeptic would say Schrier is a back-bench House member, not on track to be Speaker. Her committee seats matter but she is not a chair. Her caucus leadership is internal and does not decide floor votes. In 2022 she raised $9.6 million and spent $11 million — she was a top Republican target, and her 53.3%–46.4% win over Matt Larkin showed it. Her 2024 win by 8.2 points was easier, but the suburban-rural mix of WA-8 (it was a swing district for years under Republican Dave Reichert before Schrier flipped it in 2018) means her margin could shrink.
