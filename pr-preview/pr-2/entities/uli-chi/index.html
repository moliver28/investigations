## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why he is here:** Uli Chi is the board chair of Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (VMFH). VMFH was formed in January 2021 when Virginia Mason merged with CHI Franciscan. It is part of CommonSpirit Health. It runs the main Catholic hospital chain in Pierce County and Western Washington. As board chair, he is the top lay governance officer for a system that runs St. Joseph Medical Center (Tacoma), St. Anthony (Gig Harbor), St. Clare (Lakewood), St. Elizabeth (Enumclaw), and St. Francis (Federal Way). He decides on mission, ethics, capital projects, and regional strategy. - **Who is left out:** Ketul J. Patel runs CommonSpirit from Chicago — out of state. Julie Manas runs VMFH day-to-day as CommonSpirit Northwest Region President since 2024. She has her own profile or one coming under `vmfh`. Other VMFH board members are not on this map. Kevin McClatchy is not on VMFH; he runs Chatham AM. ## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Uli Chi is a mid-tier but key governance actor. As VMFH board chair he sits at the top of a healthcare system that shapes Pierce County's medical care, jobs, and real estate. His authority is governance authority, not ownership. But in a sector where the chair sets the agenda and approves deals, that is real power. His theological-academic network (Fuller, Regent, SPU) gives him extra cultural reach. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** (a) He is board chair of a Catholic hospital system while tied to evangelical Protestant institutions (Fuller, Regent). A defense lawyer would call this the kind of cross-confessional ecumenism CommonSpirit encourages. (b) He sits on Benaroya Research Institute plus Virginia Mason Institute plus VMFH. That creates a tight intra-Virginia-Mason-ecosystem board overlap. (c) VMFH covers Pierce, King, Kitsap, and Mason counties. Chi's reach is not limited to Pierce. - **Other reading:** A skeptic would call a regional hospital board chair a ceremonial figurehead in the age of national systems (CommonSpirit is over $30 billion).
