## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why included:** decision-making + well-connected + holds office. Division II of the Washington Court of Appeals is on the map because (a) it is the appeals court of last resort for most civil and criminal cases that start in Pierce County Superior Court (a court that handles major felonies, civil suits, and family cases); (b) Pierce County is one of the 13 counties it covers; (c) its rulings bind Pierce County Superior Court judges under stare decisis (the rule that courts must follow prior decisions); (d) it sits one step above Pierce County Superior Court in the Washington court system. (Sources 1, 2, 3, 4) - **Who was left out and why:** The Washington Supreme Court is state-level and has its own profile. Division I (Seattle) covers King, Snohomish, Skagit, Island, San Juan, and Whatcom counties. Division III is in Spokane. Pierce County Superior Court has its own profile. The Federal District Court for Western Washington has its own profile and only handles federal cases. The U.S. Ninth Circuit appeals court handles federal appeals. None of these are Division II. ## 1. Who they are - **Full legal name:** Washington Court of Appeals, Division II. - **Type:** State appeals court (intermediate). It is part of the unified Washington State Court System under Article IV of the Washington State Constitution. - **Governing law:** Washington Constitution Article IV (Judiciary); Washington Court of Appeals set up by constitutional amendment; state law RCW (Revised Code of Washington) 2.06. - **Headquarters:** 950 Broadway Plaza, Tacoma, WA 98402-4454. (Source 3) - **Phone:** (253) 593-2970. (Source 3) - **Parent:** Washington State Court System. The state Office of the Administrator for the Courts is the central office. - **Subsidiaries:** None. Division II is a single court with rotating 3-judge panels. - **Court roster (from the current WA Courts Directory):** - **Judges (7):** Chief Judge Bernard F. Veljacic; Acting Chief Judge Erik D. Price; Judge Meng Li Che; Judge Anne Cruser; Judge Rebecca Glasgow; Judge Linda Lee; Judge Bradley A. Maxa. - **Commissioners (2):** Aurora R. Bearse; Karl Triebel. - (Source 4) - **Constitutional basis:** Article IV § 30 of the Washington State Constitution lets the Legislature create intermediate appeals courts. Division II was created by statute in 1969-70 and seated in 1970. ## 3. Where their power comes from - **Power held:** - **Mandatory appellate jurisdiction:** Per WA Courts, "It is a non-discretionary appellate court — it must accept all appeals filed with it." (Source 2) - **Disposition authority:** "The Court of Appeals has authority to reverse (overrule), remand (send back to the lower court), modify, or affirm the decision of the lower court." (Source 2) - **Binding precedent on Pierce County:** Published opinions by Division II bind Pierce County Superior Court judges and Pierce County lower tribunals. Pierce County is in Division II's territory. - **No en banc review available.** (Source 1) - **Counties covered (Division II, 13 counties):** Pierce, Thurston, Mason, Kitsap, Jefferson, Grays Harbor, Clallam, Clark, Cowlitz, Lewis, Pacific, Skamania, and Wahkiakum. (Source 2) - **Selection / retention:** Nonpartisan election. Judges serve 6-year staggered terms. (Sources 1, 2) - **Panel structure:** "Panels of three judges hear each appeal," per the Washington Court of Appeals. (Source 1) - **Caseload discretion:** None. The court must accept all properly filed appeals. (Source 2) - **Key facts:** - **Tax status:** Government entity (not 501(c)(3), a federal tax-exempt charity). - **Roster (per WA Courts Directory):** Chief Judge Bernard F.
