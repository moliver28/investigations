PROCLAMAT IONS AND PRESENT AT IONS

Yout h Council Report .

The Youth Council reported on the March 13, 2010 Daddy and Daughter event and announced that Parks Appreciation Day will be held on
April 24, 2010. Reports were provided on the activities at each of the high schools.

Clover Park School Dist rict Board Report .

Clover Park School District (CPSD) Board Director Paul Wagemann reported on the Congressional delegation meeting that he attended in
Washington, D.C. to discuss impact aid funds. He then spoke about the need for schools on base to be brought up to building codes. He
commented about the progress on the school dress code, student behavior and demeanor at Clover Park High School. He reported that
students are receiving full scholarships at MIT and spoke about the ninth grade academy which started this year.

CIT Y COUNCIL COMMENT S

Councilmember Moss commented on the Relay for Life event she attended. She thanked Councilmembers for participating in the
Communities in Schools breakfast which yielded $25,000.

Councilmember Brandstetter commented about the Association of Washington Cities State grant application on Safe Routes to School
funding and other grants. He asked if the City should partner with the schools for submitting an application.

Councilmember Whalen commented on the Economic Development Board March meeting where Lakewood’s redevelopment efforts at
Tillicum were highlighted as one of the top ten economic development projects in the area. He complimented City staff for their work.

Mayor Richardson asked if a Councilmember would be able to attend the Public Safety Advisory Committee meeting this Thursday, April
8, 2010, at 5:15 p.m., in his absence. Councilmember Moss indicated that she will be able to attend.
