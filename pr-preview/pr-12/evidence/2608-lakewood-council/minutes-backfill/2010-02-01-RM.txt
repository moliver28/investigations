REPORT S BY T HE CIT Y MANAGER
City Manager Neiditz announced that the site visit from the assessors of the Police law enforcement accreditation program started their
review of Lakewood?s police practices over the weekend, and a response should be forthcoming in 60 days. He also announced that a
special memorial in honor of fallen police officers, hosted by the State Legislators, is scheduled for February 3, 2010, from 10:00a.m. ?
noon orchestrated by Senators Mike Carrell and Rosa Franklin.
City Manager Neiditz reported that the City is concerned about the bridge at the south end of Pacific Highway which requires
improvements. He explained that to repair the bridge and meet safety requirements, if one lane was closed, it would take four weeks to
complete or, if the entire bridge was closed, it would take two weeks to complete. He indicated that staff is recommending a full bridge
closure and provide ample notification to businesses and the public. He added that the improvements would commence the third week
of February.
City Manager Neiditz announced that the February 8, 2010 Study Session will be a joint meeting with the Citizens Transportation
Advisory Committee at the Multi-Purpose meeting at the Police Station.
City Manager Neiditz requested a 15-minute Executive Session regarding potential litigation followed by a closed session relative to
collective bargaining.

Lakewood City Council Minutes -6- February 1, 2010
BRIEFING BY T HE CIT Y MANAGER
City Manager Neiditz commended the Public Works staff for meeting with the neighborhood, Puget Sound Energy and others about their
work on constructing sewers.
City Manager Neiditz provided an update on a cease and desist order against CIAW and commended the Council for switching from CIAW
to WCIA insurance.
City Manager Neiditz explained that there does not appear to be much support for HB2873 on ?permitting local governments to limit
social card games? this year, but that there is commitment to work on this issue in 2011. He reported that HB2780 on the traffic safety
camera issue received testimony last week, and it appears that the bill does not seem to have much support.
CIT Y COUNCIL COMMENT S
Councilmember Brandstetter indicated that the Pacific Neighborhood Association has not been meeting due to Lisa Ikeda, former
President, moving out of Lakewood. He recommended that the City help engage in re-energizing that neighborhood association and help
foster some leadership in that association.
Councilmember Neary commented on the Citizens Transportation Advisory Committee and Landmarks Heritage Advisory Board

Councilmember Neary commented on the Citizens Transportation Advisory Committee and Landmarks Heritage Advisory Board
meetings he attended.
Councilmember Whalen commented on the Arts Commission meeting he attended. He then asked for a status on the $25,000 lodging
tax funds that was allocated for the Lakewood Theatre. He noted that the Arts Commission is interested in having a memorial flag in
remembrance of the fallen officers.
Councilmember Thomas suggested that the Council take advantage of meeting with Secretary Paula Hammond of the State
Transportation Committee as she offered to meet with Council to discuss transportation concerns in Lakewood.
Deputy Mayor Anderson commented on an Association of Washington Cities? report about electing part-time Municipal Court judges. He
then spoke about the City of University Place?s reduction of their police contract with Pierce County for police services and expressed
concern that through mutual aid calls, such cuts could impact Lakewood?s police and that the City should meet with University Place to
work out a solution in paying for such services.
Deputy Mayor Anderson announced that Partners for Parks is focusing their efforts on fundraising for restrooms at Ft. Steilacoom Park.

Lakewood City Council Minutes -7- February 1, 2010

Mayor Richardson asked for clarification about funding sewer improvements on Maple Avenue, and asked if reader boards will be posted
about the closures on Pacific Highway due to the bridge repairs.
Mayor Richardson asked for clarification about the $550,000 of federal funding coming to Washington State; and if it will be used for the
