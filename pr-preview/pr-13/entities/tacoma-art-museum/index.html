## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Why TAM is on the map: it is the largest art museum in Pierce County. The Tacoma Art Museum (TAM) holds $58.6 million in net assets. Its board president, Dona Ponepinto, also runs United Way of Pierce County. TAM's board ties into the county's civic network — United Way, MultiCare (a hospital system), Wellfound Behavioral Health (a mental health nonprofit), Workforce Development Council (a jobs agency), and Tacoma Community College. That overlap makes TAM a meeting place for Pierce County's civic leaders, not just a museum. - Other museums nearby: the Museum of Glass, Washington State History Museum, LeMay Family Collection, and Seattle Art Museum. They are nearby but are not the same organization.
