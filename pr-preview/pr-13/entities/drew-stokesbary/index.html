## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why he's on the map.** Drew Stokesbary is the state House Representative for Washington's 31st Legislative District, Position 1. That district covers parts of King and Pierce Counties, including Auburn, Bonney Lake, Buckley, Edgewood, and Sumner. More importantly, he has been the House Minority Leader since April 2023. That makes him the top Republican in the Washington House. His district includes a lot of Pierce County territory. - **Who is NOT on the map.** Josh Penner (R-31, House Position 2) is a district-mate but is not on the map at this tier. Phil Fortunato (R-31, Senate) is on the map separately.

Stokesbary (legal/formal name on state campaign-finance agency (PDC) filings). - **Born:** UNVERIFIED — public sources do not list a date. He is described as a "lifelong Washingtonian" (Source 5). - **Home:** Auburn, Washington (Source 2, 5). - **Family:** Wife Ashley Stokesbary; four sons; a border collie named Scout (Source 2, 5). - **Education:** B.S. in Economics and Mathematics from Duke University (he also studied History there); J.D. *cum laude* from the University of Notre Dame Law School (Source 2, 5, 7). - **Bar admissions:** Washington, Arkansas, and Texas. ## 3. Where their power comes from - **Elected office.** He has served in the Washington House of Representatives, 31st District, Position 1, since January 12, 2015. He is in his 6th term. His current term ends January 11, 2027. He is running for re-election in November 2026. He won the August 4, 2026 primary with 57.9% to Stephen Szczurko-Walton's 42.0% (Source 2). - **Leadership role.** He has been House Minority Leader since April 2023 — the top Republican job in the House. That makes him the chief strategist and spokesperson for the House Republican Caucus. He decides committee assignments for his members and runs the floor strategy (Source 2, 5). - **Past leadership jobs.** He was the Ranking Member of the House Appropriations Committee. He was Minority Floor Leader. He chaired the Select Committee on Pension Policy. He was assistant ranking Republican on House Appropriations (Source 5, 8). - **Current committee (2025–2026):** Rules Committee (Source 5). - **Budget.** As former Appropriations Ranking Member and current Minority Leader, Stokesbary has real influence over Republican budget strategy. The actual budget is controlled by the Democratic majority. - **Where his power reaches:** 31st Legislative District — Auburn, Bonney Lake, Buckley, Carbonado, Edgewood, Milton, South Prairie, Sumner, Wilkeson.

## 1. Who they are - **The bottom line.** Drew Stokesbary holds the most powerful position of any House Republican in Washington state. As House Minority Leader, he controls caucus strategy, committee assignments, and floor operations. He is the chief Republican voice in the House. His law firm work at Chalmers Adams and his co-founding of CADF give him reach beyond the legislature in the conservative legal world. His long committee experience — former Appropriations Ranking Member, Pension Policy Chair, and current Rules Committee — makes him one of the most policy-savvy Republicans in the building. His strong election numbers (58–64% in his district) show a secure base. - **Conflicts to watch.** As a practicing corporate and securities attorney while serving as House Minority Leader, his private legal clients (startups, emerging companies, investment funds) and his legislative role in business regulation and tax policy overlap. CADF, which he co-founded, sends legal advocacy letters to the very legislature he leads — a structural overlap between his nonprofit role and his legislative leadership. His bar admissions in multiple states and federal courts suggest a national legal practice that extends past Washington state. - **The counter-argument.** Stokesbary leads the minority party in a state where Democrats hold supermajorities in both chambers. His actual power to pass or block laws is limited by the Democratic majority's ability to govern without Republican votes. CADF's work could be seen as partisan advocacy rather than constitutional protection. His law firm is small and does not match the clout of partners at major firms. - **Confidence:** High — multiple primary sources (WA Legislature, House Republican Caucus official page, PDC, Chalmers Adams law firm website, CADF) corroborate all key claims.
