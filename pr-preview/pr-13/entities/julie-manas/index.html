## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why she is here:** Manas is the chief executive of the largest hospital network in Pierce County. Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (VMFH) runs St. Joseph, St. Francis, St. Anthony in Gig Harbor, St. Clare, St. Elizabeth, and the Franciscan Medical Pavilion complex. Those cover Tacoma, Federal Way, Lakewood, Gig Harbor, Burien, and Enumclaw. She signs off on building, hiring, and clinical programs that touch thousands of workers and roughly half of the county's hospital beds. That puts her inside Pierce's institutional decision-making circle. - **Who is NOT on this page:** Ketul J. Patel was her predecessor. He left for Wellstar Health System in Georgia. Jones is MultiCare's COO/Market President — MultiCare is the other big hospital group in Pierce County, on a separate page. Dr. David Carlson is MultiCare's Chief Physician Officer.

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Manas runs the day-to-day strategy and operations for the largest faith-based health system in Pierce County — VMFH — covering about 12 hospitals and 300 care sites across the Northwest Region. She has no elected or legal authority. But her choices on building, hiring, clinical programs, and partnerships in Tacoma, Lakewood, Gig Harbor, Federal Way, and Burien shape the county's health-care safety net, its biggest medical-workforce pipeline, and the buying power of a multi-state 501(c)(3). She started Jan 2, 2026. Her past work at Ascension Indiana and Quorum Health, plus eight years on the Catholic Health Association board (including a chair term), put her in the inner circle of U.S. Catholic health-care leadership. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** - Vendor/contractor capture: regional procurement flows up to CommonSpirit's Chicago HQ. Local influence is limited by national group-purchasing contracts. - Faith-based policy (CHA) may clash with staff and patient views on gender-affirming care, end-of-life directives, and contraceptive services. These have been contested in Pierce County before. - Defense-attorney view: a hospital executive is not a "Pierce leader" in the political sense, and adding her to the map blurs the decision-making line. - **Other view:** A skeptic would say Manas is a regional placeholder — she is part-time in Pierce County, and her real authority sits in Chicago at CommonSpirit HQ. The opposite reading (favored here) is that she is the single point of accountability for about half of Pierce County's hospital beds and a major employer. Her past CHA board chair role gives her long-lasting ties to U.S. Catholic health policy. - **Confidence:** Medium — role, predecessor/successor, and compensation authority confirmed via primary VMFH and Bizjournals sources; prior Ascension/Quorum tenure confirmed via VMFH press and South Sound Biz; DOB, residence, and family UNVERIFIED.
