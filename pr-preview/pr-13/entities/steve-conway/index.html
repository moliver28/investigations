## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why he is on the map:** Steve Conway is on this map because he holds office, makes decisions, and is well-connected. He is the senior Washington State Senator for the 29th Legislative District (Tacoma). He has served continuously for 33 years (State Representative 1993–2010, State Senator 2011–present). He is now the Senate President Pro Tempore (2025–). The President Pro Tempore is one of the top leaders in the State Senate. The other map ties are through Pierce Dems and the Pierce County Council. - **What that means:** He holds power from his long tenure. He is the longest-serving Pierce County legislator. He sits on the Ways & Means and Labor & Commerce committees. He has deep ties to labor unions. He is retiring at the end of 2026. - **Who is NOT on the map:** His wife Georgia Conway is not profiled. The two House members from the 29th District are not profiled here. Sharlett Mena, the Democrat running to replace him in 2026, is not profiled.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Power held:** Conway holds power from his office as Senate President Pro Tempore (2025–present). That is one of the top leadership jobs in the WA State Senate (source #1, #2). He sits on Labor & Commerce and Ways & Means committees (source #2). His power comes from seniority — 33 straight years in the legislature (1993–2026) make him the longest-serving Pierce County legislator and one of the most senior in the state. - **Government ties:** As President Pro Tempore, Conway runs Senate sessions when the Lieutenant Governor is absent. As a Ways & Means member, he shapes the state operating budget. On Labor & Commerce, he influences labor and consumer policy. - **What budget does he touch?** As a Ways & Means member, he helps shape the state operating budget (about $70 billion every two years). The President Pro Tempore role does not directly control the budget. - **Appointments:** As a senator, he votes to confirm gubernatorial appointments. The President Pro Tempore may have other procedural powers within the Senate. He does not have veto power. - **Where his power applies:** The 29th Legislative District (south Tacoma, Parkland, Spanaway). As a state senator, his bills apply statewide.

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Steve Conway holds one of the most powerful institutional jobs in Washington state government as Senate President Pro Tempore (2025–). His power is built on seniority — 33 straight years in the legislature (1993–2026). He has deep committee experience (Ways & Means, Labor & Commerce) and a long list of awards from labor unions, veterans groups, educators, and advocacy groups. His labor union background (UFCW Local 81 business agent) gives him a natural base with organized labor. His top donors reflect that (IBEW, SEIU 775, Carpenters, IAFF, WEA). His retirement on January 21, 2026 will end a 33-year career and leave a big gap for Pierce County in the state Senate. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** As a Ways & Means member, Conway influences the state budget that funds Pierce County. That is a structural feature of legislative appropriations, not a personal conflict. His labor union background and labor donations ($2,000 each from IBEW, SEIU, Carpenters, IAFF in 2022) could suggest a pro-labor bias in his Labor & Commerce work. The defense: labor support reflects his district (D-29 is working-class). Financial disclosures are required. All donations are public on the PDC site. Conway has always disclosed his labor background. - **Analysis of alternatives :** The other reading is that Conway's 0.032 score correctly reflects his declining relevance. His power has waned as newer senators like Trudeau and Nobles take on more active committee jobs. But the President Pro Tempore is chosen by the full Senate and carries institutional precedence. His 33 years on Ways & Means give him informal budget influence. His retirement has created a competitive open-seat race (Mena vs. Anderson), which shows his seat is valued. - **Confidence:** High.
