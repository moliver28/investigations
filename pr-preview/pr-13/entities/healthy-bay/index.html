## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** CHB is a 30-year-old Tacoma-based nonprofit (a 501(c)(3), a tax-exempt public-good group). It has about 6 staff and $801,000 in 2024 revenue. It does three rare things for its size. First, it runs the only environmental patrol boat in the South Sound. Second, it does legal work through a hired Staff Attorney and a "Legal Action & Corporate Accountability" program. Third, it sets the agenda through the "Dirty Dozen" report and a Community Pollution Dashboard. Together these let CHB push back on industrial-port actors in Pierce County — especially Weyerhaeuser and the Port of Tacoma. CHB also plays a real role in policy talks about industrial land-use reform and clean-water rules. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** (1) CHB's "hold polluters accountable" framing puts it in opposition to specific companies. Any claim CHB makes about a company should be checked. (2) CHB takes foundation and likely government money. Cause IQ shows "Greater Tacoma" grants ($40K). The full revenue mix is UNVERIFIED. (3) The Staff Attorney has a law degree (Stetson, Environmental focus) but her bar status and case record were not checked — UNVERIFIED. (4) The "Dirty Dozen" list is a public-advocacy tool, not a peer-reviewed study. Treat each named entity as advocacy-asserted rather than adjudicated. - **Analysis of alternatives :** A defense reading would say CHB is small. Its money is limited compared to the companies it targets. Its claims are advocacy, not regulatory findings. Any claim about a specific company should be checked against state and federal agency records (Ecology, the federal Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Region 10). The opposing reading would say CHB's mix of legal capacity, on-water monitoring, and 30-year history gives it real credibility that exceeds its small size. A hired Staff Attorney means its claims can land as real legal filings, not just press releases. For this investigation, CHB is best treated as a primary-source channel for environmental concerns about specific industrial actors. It has standing to file legal actions that can limit those actors' behavior. - **Confidence:** Medium.
