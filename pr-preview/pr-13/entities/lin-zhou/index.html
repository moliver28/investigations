## §0 why this entity is on the map Zhou leads one of two major Tacoma technical colleges. She also serves on state boards that shape policy on college readiness and adult education.

## §1 Identity **FACT** Dr. Lin Zhou, Ph.D., became president of Bates Technical College in 2018. She is the first female president in Bates history. She is also the first Chinese immigrant to lead a U.S. community college. **Source:** https://www.aacc.nche.edu/about-us/governance/2023-board-slate-profiles/lin-zhou (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** Bates hired Zhou in 2013 as the dean of continuing education, apprenticeship, and child studies. She then moved up. She served as dean of instruction, executive dean, and vice president of student success (2016) before her 2018 appointment. **Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/bates-president-lin-zhou-wins-chief-executive-officer-award (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** In April 2019, the College Spark Washington Board of Trustees appointed Zhou to a 14-member board. It brings together business, industry, and education leaders. **Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/bates-technical-colleges-president-lin-zhou-joins-college-spark-board (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Governor Jay Inslee appointed Zhou to the Adult Education Advisory Council. **Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/governor-jay-inslee-appoints-bates-technical-college-president-lin-zhou-to-adult-education-advisory-council (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Zhou received the 2023 Chief Executive Officer of the Year Award from the Washington Association of College Trustees (ACT). **Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/bates-president-lin-zhou-wins-chief-executive-officer-award (primary; reliability 0.95).

## §2 Power basis **FACT** As Bates president, Zhou leads a public technical college. It runs within the State Board for Community and Technical Colleges system. Bates was founded in 1940. Its name honors director LaVerne Hazen Bates (served 1944–1969). **Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bates_Technical_College (secondary; reliability 0.85)

**FACT** Bates owns KBTC-TV (PBS Tacoma). Zhou sits on its board. **Source:** https://www.kbtc.org/connect/board (primary; reliability 0.9).
