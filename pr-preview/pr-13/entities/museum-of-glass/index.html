## §0 why this entity is on the map Museum of Glass is on this map because it is the lasting project of the Russell family. George F. Russell Jr. (1932–2025) co-founded and bankrolled the museum. He built it as an anchor for the Thea Foss Waterway redevelopment. The museum is now a flagship of Tacoma's "Museum Row" cultural cluster.

Its 501(c)(3) tax status, its annual revenue of about $5 million, its capital expansion project, and its broad board ties make it a network node. It links Tacoma arts donors to waterfront redevelopment.

## §1 who they are legally **FACT** Museum of Glass is a registered 501(c)(3) charitable organization. Its federal tax ID number (EIN) is 91-1669422. Its tax exemption began in January 1996. **Source:** https://www.museumofglass.org/press-release-archive (primary; reliability 0.95); https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/911669422 (primary; reliability 0.95); https://www.charitynavigator.org/ein/911669422 (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** The museum sits at 1801 Dock Street, Tacoma, WA 98402. It opened in 2002 on a former Superfund site. Building the museum kicked off the Thea Foss Waterway cleanup and the surrounding museum district. Six museums now sit within walking distance. **Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/museum-of-glass (secondary; reliability 0.85); https://www.museumofglass.org/george-russell (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** The most recent IRS Form 990 (per ProPublica and Charity Navigator) shows the following. Contributions ranged from about $1.6 million to about $3.1 million in recent years. Program services brought in $1.3 million to $1.6 million. Investment income ranged from $17,000 to $296,000. Total annual revenue sits in the $3 million to $6 million range. **Source:** https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/911669422 (primary; reliability 0.9).

**FACT** The Board of Trustees is chaired by Kenneth Farmer, Jr., M.D. He is the Principal of Ken Farmer Advisors, LLC and a retired U.S. Army Major General. The Vice Chair is Walt Riehl. The Secretary is Todd Gilman. He is SVP, Deputy General Counsel, and Secretary of TrueBlue, Inc. Other trustees include Barry Anton, Rex Bates, Jonah Bergman, Joanna Carey Cleveland (UPS), Mary Carstensen, Clemencia Castro-Woolery, Curtis Cox, Calvin Gasaway, Brent Heilesen, Ian MacNeil, Marc Mangel, Megan Mead, Altaf Merchant (Milgard Endowed Dean, UW Tacoma), Katelyn Smith, and others. **Source:** https://museumofglass.org/board (primary; reliability 0.9).

## §2 Power basis **FACT** The museum runs a working Hot Shop. It is one of the largest and most active museum glass studios on the West Coast. That gives it cultural power. The museum curates national traveling shows, runs artist residencies, and shapes Tacoma's identity as a glass-arts city. Tacoma is the birthplace of Dale Chihuly. **Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/museum-of-glass (secondary; reliability 0.85); https://museumofglass.org/board (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** The museum receives Tacoma Creates funding. Tacoma Creates is a voter-approved city fund for arts, culture, and science. The museum also runs a 2026 capital campaign for expansion. Its mix of donations and program-service revenue shows blended public and private support. **Source:** https://www.museumofglass.org (primary; reliability 0.9); https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/911669422 (primary; reliability 0.9).

**FACT** George F. Russell Jr. (1933–2025) co-founded the museum with Phil Phibbs. He co-chaired the founding Board of Trustees with Jane Russell. The museum's Hot Shop is named the "Jane Russell Hot Shop" in her memory after her early death. **Source:** https://www.museumofglass.org/george-russell (primary; reliability 0.95).
