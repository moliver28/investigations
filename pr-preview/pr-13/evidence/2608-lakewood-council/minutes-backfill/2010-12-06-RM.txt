PROCLAMATIONS AND PRESENTATIONS
Youth Council Report.
The Youth Council reported on the Christmas Tree Lighting event, Caring for Kids “Stuff the Bus” event and leading a class on
Texting 101 at the Senior Center. A report was provided on the upcoming Martin Luther King Jr. celebration and activities at
the various high schools.
Presentation of Mountain View Police Memorial Plaque.
Mr. Ron Messenger, General Manager of Mountain View Funeral Home, presented a Police Memorial Plaque in honor of
Lakewood’s four fallen officers, Sgt. Renninger, Officers Griswold, Owens and Richards.

Clover Park School District Board Report.
Clover Park School District (CPSD) Board Director Carole Jacobs reported on the CPSD annual report called Learning Today
Leading Tomorrow. She then reported on the sports activities and athletes of the high schools. An update was provided on
the construction progress at Lakes High School. She then reported on the Operations Homefront donation that was received
and the Caring for Kids “stuff the bus” event and provided an update on the student test scores and student achievements.

Retail Analysis Report
Ms. Lisa Hill, Vice President of Community ID Division, Buxton Company, discussed the services they provide to municipalities
and businesses in understanding citizens’ retail needs.
Discussion ensued on how Buxton differentiates the data collected among surrounding communities/clients within the same
regional area; how does Buxton safeguard client information; what are the economic development staffing levels for cities the
size of Lakewood; and the vision and mission of Buxton.

Speaking before the Council were:
Curtis Littlegreen, Lakewood resident, thanked the Council for funding the Oasis Youth Center and spoke about his volunteer
experience at the Pierce County AIDS Foundation.
Daniel Fejerang, Spanaway resident, spoke about his experience with the Oasis Youth Center and programs provided at the
Center.
Joshua Parsons, Tacoma resident, spoke about his six months experience with the Oasis Youth Center and described his
personal situation with the Center.
Siggy Frank, Tacoma resident, spoke about his experience with the Oasis Youth Center for the past six years and expressed
his appreciation for the Center and how the Center helped him.
Dennis Haugen, Lakewood resident, indicated that the Buxton Company will not be helpful and that the problems that need to
be changed are fundamental changes to be made by the Legislature.

Debbie LeBeau, Lakewood resident, thanked the Council for supporting the Clover Park School District and encouraged the
Council’s support for child care funding for the early learning program of ECEAP and Headstart.
Janne Hutchins, Lakewood Area Shelter Association (LASA), spoke about seed money LASA received when Lakewood first
became a city and that small grants are important.
Kurt Sample, Lakewood resident, spoke about a youth robotics tournament he attended. He then spoke in support of human
services funding.

BRIEFING BY THE CITY MANAGER

City Manager Neiditz reported that the Joint Base Lewis-McChord Growth Coordination Plan group will meet next week as the
group moves into the implementation phase. He then reported on a meeting he had with General Lowenberg about the Camp
Murray gate location.

City Manager Neiditz announced that next week’s December 13, 2010 meeting will be a Special meeting and that the
December 20 and 27, 2010 Council meetings are cancelled.

City Manager Neiditz then announced the passing of Hal Young who was instrumental in the formation of Lakewood’s
cityhood.

CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS

Councilmember Moss commented on the Tillicum Neighborhood Association meeting she attended last week and the
concerns expressed by citizens about the Camp Murray gate relocation.

Councilmember Brandstetter commented on the Tillicum Action Committee meeting he attended and their discussion about
getting community input about a meeting with Amtrak. He then spoke about a representative of Congressman Smith’s office
who reported that all the dollars for Camp Murray had to be spent inside the boundaries of Camp Murray and that monies
outside the boundaries would need to come from the State. He then commented on the Christmas Tree lighting ceremony.

Councilmember Thomas asked that a thank you letter be sent to Mountain View Funeral Home for the memorial plaque. She
commented that there were not as many youth attending this year’s Christmas Tree lighting ceremony.

Councilmember Whalen spoke about a well done presentation by Ms. Hill from the Buxton Company. He suggested that the
Council hold a mini retreat to discuss economic development strategies and look at tourism related matters and infrastructure
improvements.

Councilmember Whalen commented on The News Tribune article about the annexation along Steele Street (Forza Coffee
Company) into Lakewood. City Manager Neiditz indicated that there has not been a formal request for an annexation.

Deputy Mayor Anderson commented on the Christmas Tree Lighting ceremony he attended.

CLOSED SESSION

Deputy Mayor Anderson announced that Council will recess into a closed session to discuss employee relations pursuant to
R.C.W. 42.30.140(4).

ADJOURNMENT

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned at 9:50 p.m.
