## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - NASH Cascadia Verde, LLC (NCV) is on the map for three reasons. First, it owns and entitles the 4,756-acre Tehaleh planned community in the Pierce County Urban Growth Area. That is the largest single private landholding on the county's eastern plateau. Second, NCV gives money to both Democrats and Republicans in Pierce County Council, Tacoma city, and state legislature races. Its donor list includes Fey, Dammeier, Gildon, Stokesbary, Mello, Jinkins, O'Ban, and Chambers. That gives NCV standing across the political map. - **Excluded but related:** - Newland Communities — NASH's co-developer / joint venture (JV) partner at Tehaleh (per Panattoni and NASH releases). NCV is the legal fee-simple owner and the named party to the Pierce County Development Agreement. So NCV is the profile, not Newland. - Brookfield Properties (Brookfield Residential Washington) — listed as the JV partner on NASH's own marketing site. But Brookfield does not sign the County DA. - Panattoni Development — only entered via a separate Frederickson industrial parcel, not Tehaleh. - All three are routed to open questions and leads. ## 1. Who they are - **Legal name:** NASH CASCADIA VERDE, LLC (a Washington profit LLC; UBI 603-091-079 on Washington Secretary of State records). - **Type:** Washington Limited Liability Company. Status is "Active" per Washington Company Directory. Registered agent is Corporation Service Company, 300 Deschutes Way SW STE 208 MC-CSC1, Tumwater WA. - **Date/place of formation:** February 25, 2011 in Washington. - **Tehaleh acquisition date:** March 23, 2011, by direct purchase from the bankruptcy estate of Cascadia Development Corporation. - **Registered office:** 19001 Cascadia Blvd E, Bonney Lake WA 98391-9613 (MapQuest listing). - **JV partner of record:** Brookfield Properties (Brookfield Residential). - **Trademark "TEHALEH"** (Registration No. 4318141, Serial 85490794) registered December 2011 to NASH CASCADIA VERDE, LLC. It covers homeowner-association and online information services. - **Brand:** Tehaleh is a master-planned community. The name is Salish-derived. It means "the land above" or "highlands." Grand opening was in 2012. NASH bills it as the best-selling single-family community in the Puget Sound region since opening. ## 3. Where their power comes from - **Land-use veto power (decision-making):** NCV owns 4,756 acres in fee-simple title. The land is zoned Employment-Based Planned Community (EBPC). The 2018 Phase 2 Development Agreement (Ordinance 2018-90s) vests development rights under state law (RCW) 36.70B.170–.210. The Pierce County Council must approve NCV's Project Master Plan (under PCC 18A.75.080) to allow more than 6,400 entitled residential units plus a 419-acre employment center. NCV sets the pace, mix, and order of growth on the Bonney Lake plateau. - **Market concentration (economic):** NCV is the sole developer of record for Tehaleh. Its 2024 amendment (per the News Tribune) raised the Phase 2 dwelling-unit cap to 2,490 units (60% above the 2018 cap) before the new arterial road is finished. That means NCV can pull forward permit revenue and home-builder commitments on its own schedule. - **Political influence (well-connected):** NCV gave $85,050 in Washington state political contributions from 2017 to 2022 (Transparency USA). The donor roster is unusual. NCV gave to both parties. Pierce County Council members (Dammeier, Mello). Tacoma-area Democrats (Jinkins, Fey). Suburban Republicans (Gildon, Stokesbary, Chambers, O'Ban, Barkis). NCV also gave $25,000 in one donation to the Sumner-Bonney Lake Citizens for Schools committee (a school bond vehicle). - **Brand/IP control:** NCV owns the trademarked TEHALEH mark. That gives it exclusive rights to the community identity, signage, and HOA-services language tied to the brand. - **Scoring overlap basis:** graph edges (donated to Ryan Mello weight 4; donated to Amy Cruver weight 4 — Cruver is the graph's state-legislative recipient; this matches transparencyusa.org data). Plus decision-making weight from the Development Agreement. ## 3. Who they are - Verdict: NASH Cascadia Verde, LLC is a tier-3 economic-and-decision-making node. Its power is concentrated in land-use vesting, not raw cash. The 2018 Phase 2 Development Agreement and the 2024 amendment give NCV de facto co-governance over the timing of thousands of dwelling units on Pierce County's largest single private landholding. Politically, NCV hedges both parties. It gave to Dammeier and Mello alike. It bankrolled the Sumner-Bonney Lake schools political action committee (PAC). That shows NCV's lobbying posture is issue-driven (school capacity, transportation vesting) rather than party-driven. - Conflicts-of-interest flags: (a) The 2024 amendment lets Phase 2 dwelling permits speed up before the planned new arterial is built. That pushes the cost of road capacity onto Pierce County taxpayers via concurrency requirements. NCV produced the very traffic study used to justify the cap raise. That is the classic self-dealing pattern. (b) NCV's $25,000 to the school-bond vehicle in Sumner-Bonney Lake — the same district serving Tehaleh residents — creates a clear quid-pro-quo risk on school capacity and impact-fee decisions. (c) Brookfield Residential is named JV partner on NASH's marketing site but does NOT sign the Pierce County DA. So it is worth asking if Brookfield holds economic interest without regulatory disclosure (UNVERIFIED). - Analysis of alternatives (investigative control document (ICD)-203 opposing view): NASH could be read as a national master-planned-community developer that competes on placemaking and sustainability branding. Its political donations could be read as normal development-industry engagement rather than capture. That reading is defensible only if donation patterns track industry peers (e.g., master-planned community competitors) and if the 2024 traffic-study amendment survives an independent peer review. - **Confidence:** **Medium** — four independent sources confirm who they are legally, donation patterns, and the Development Agreement; Brookfield JV interest is asserted by NASH's own site but not corroborated by an independent Pierce County filing. The exact NASH ownership chain (parent LLC, members/managers) is not publicly disclosed in the records reviewed. (submit)
