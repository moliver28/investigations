## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Lois Bernstein is on this map for three reasons. First, she was the Chief Community Executive at MultiCare Health System for 25 years (1998–2023). MultiCare is the biggest private employer in Pierce County. Second, the governor appointed her to the Tacoma Community College (TCC) Board of Trustees in January 2017. She served as Vice Chair. Third, she sits on the ULI Executive Council for a Greater Tacoma. She also sits on the Alliance for South Sound Behavioral Health Coalition. Her network spans planning, health care, and behavioral health. - **Adjacent but EXCLUDED candidates:** Cathy Ushka (a different Tacoma City Council member — Bernstein has not been on the Tacoma City Council); Diane Cecchettini (a MultiCare CEO whom Robertson replaced); Florence Chang (MultiCare president/COO who will succeed Robertson as president and CEO Jan 1, 2027). ## 1.
