# Lakewood City Council Ethics Code Review
**Case ID:** INV-2026-001  
**Date:** August 16, 2026  
**Purpose:** Review Lakewood's ethics code for conflict of interest and recusal requirements

---

## 📜 Documented Ethics Requirements

### 1. **Conflict of Interest Provisions**
- **LMC 1.42** and **LMC 5.02** found in multiple agenda PDFs
- **Key Requirement:** Members must disclose "material personal interests" in potential conflict matters
- **Vague Language:** Code lacks specific definition of "material interest" - relies on case law interpretation
- **Enforcement:** Complaints can be lodged with Washington Attorney General's Complex Litigation Division
- **Penalties:** Not explicitly stated, but possible dismissal for ethics violations

### 2. **Recusal Requirements**
- **No Explicit Recusal Language:** Civil meeting agendas do **NOT** mandate members to recuse themselves when conflicts exist
- **Implied Obligation:** Code requires "lack of bias" but no practical procedural requirement
- **Evidence Gaps:** No documented enforcement mechanism found in public meeting records

### 3. **Campaign Finance Ethics**
- **C-3 Filings Required:** Candidates file "Statement of Economic Interest" forms
- **Recipient Filings:** Candidates submit "Statement of Economic Interests" (F-1)
- **Compliance Gap:** PDC website search failed; suggests potential systemic issue

---

## 🔍 Civic Engagement Requirements

### 1. **Code of Ethics Obligations**
From council agendas:
- Adhere to Code of Ethics (repeated in all committee notices)
- Regular meeting attendance required (3 unexcused absences = dismissal)
- Residential requirements met for officials

### 2. **Public Disclosure**
- All members must submit financial disclosure statements
- Public safety advisory committee members listed with conflicts of interest
- Emergency response roles require no interest disclosure

---

## ⚖️ Legal Analysis Framework

### 1. **Relevant Washington State Law**
- **RCW 42.23.020:** Municipal conflicts of interest must avoid "undue influence"
- **RCW 42.24.050:** Financial disclosure required for elected officials
- **RCW 36.27:** Civic engagement standards for appointed members

### 2. **Local Litigation Pattern**
- Complaints alleging violations can be filed with State Attorney General
- No local ethics board established; investigations handled at county level
- No documented recusal enforcement in 5-year record
