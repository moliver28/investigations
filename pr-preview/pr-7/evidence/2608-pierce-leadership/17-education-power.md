## Overview

Pierce County has 14+ public school districts controlling hundreds of millions of dollars in annual operating budgets, construction contracts, and voter-approved bonds/levies. This file maps board membership, superintendents, budgets, construction awards, election results, and campaign finance across all major districts. School boards are a HIGH priority missing dimension per the gap analysis — they control significant public spending and construction contracting while operating with relatively low public scrutiny.

**Sources:** District websites, Ballotpedia, Washington State Public Disclosure Commission, Pierce County Auditor, OSPI, News Tribune.

## Campaign Finance Observations

Washington State school board races are **nonpartisan** with a top-two primary system. Key observations:

- **Tacoma:** Lisa Keating raised $20,000+ in her first run (per News Tribune, 2019 data) — significant for a school board race
- **Political affiliations visible:** Chelsea McElroy (Tacoma) is a Democratic precinct committee officer in LD 27; past Chair of Pierce County Young Democrats
- **Union connections:** McElroy (SEIU 925, active in bargaining); Janet Caldwell (Clover Park) is Bethel Education Association President — direct labor union leadership on a board
- **Endorsements:** Northwest Progress Coalition WA (NWPC) endorsed candidates in 2025 — includes progressive slate across Pierce County school boards
- **PDC Limits:** WA Public Disclosure Commission caps contributions; PDC database available at https://www.pdc.wa.gov
- **School board members do not receive a salary** — per diem only ($50/day, max $4,800/yr), so campaign finance is about influence, not direct financial benefit

### Notable Political Cross-Connections
- **T'wina Nobles** (UPSD board member) — WA State Senator for the 28th Legislative District (D); simultaneously holds school board and state legislature positions
- **Chelsea McElroy** (Tacoma board) — Vice Chair of City of Tacoma Board of Ethics; active in Democratic Party politics
- **Elizabeth Bonbright** (Tacoma board) — Former Chair of JMAC (Joint Municipal Action Committee) — the most powerful coordinating body in Tacoma/Pierce County
- **Mark Baumgarten** (Franklin Pierce) — 25+ years on board; former construction industry professional — direct industry insider
- **Alyssa Anderson Pearson** (Clover Park) — Contracts & Procurement Administrator for WA Military Department — government procurement expertise on board

## Power Structure Analysis

### Key Power Nodes

1. **Tacoma Public Schools** — Largest district ($665M budget, 28K students, 58 schools). Board President Korey Strozier and Superintendent Josh Garcia control the largest education budget in the county. $80.7M in capital outlay. Bonbright's JMAC chair role connects education to city/county/port/tribe power structure.

2. **Sumner-Bonney Lake** — Fast-growing district with major construction pipeline. Superintendent Laurie Dent (2024 WA State Supt of the Year) wields significant influence. Absher Construction relationship worth monitoring. $114M expansion bond + ongoing Tehaleh growth.

3. **University Place** — $295M bond (plus $60M state match = $355M) creates a massive construction program. Mary Lu Dickinson has served since 1995 — 30+ years of institutional memory. T'wina Nobles bridges school board and state senate.

4. **Bethel** — Second-largest south county district (21K students). Major new HS construction + middle school replacement. Dr. Brian Lowney new superintendent (2024). Board has military-connected members (Manning — retired USAF).

5. **Clover Park** — Serves Lakewood/JBLM. Superintendent Banner is WA State Supt of the Year (2025). Board includes union president (Caldwell) and procurement professional (Pearson).

### Red Flags / Research Gaps

- **Campaign finance data:** Requires deeper PDC database queries for all 14 districts. Most school board races fly under the radar with minimal reporting.
- **Construction contract award details:** Specific dollar amounts and competing bidders not consistently available — requires public records requests to individual districts.
- **Developer connections:** Sumner-Bonney Lake's Tehaleh land purchases and construction awards (Absher) warrant scrutiny given the rapid growth corridor.
- **Union influence:** Multiple board members with direct union ties (Caldwell/Bethel Education Association, McElroy/SEIU 925) — warrants analysis of collective bargaining decisions.
- **T'wina Nobles dual role:** School board member + state senator creates unique power concentration in University Place.
- **Emily Dickinson longevity:** 30 consecutive years on one school board is extraordinary — potential single-point-of-failure governance risk.
