## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Bruce Dammeier is on this map because he held the top elected job in Pierce County: Pierce County Executive from 2017 to 2024. The Executive runs the county's $1B+ budget, can veto Council laws, and hires the heads of about 30 county departments. He is a Republican. He left office in January 2025 after hitting his term limit. Democrat Ryan Mello took the seat. - People NOT on the map: Pat McCarthy (a former Executive before him), Roger Bush (a Council Chair during his term), and Joe Stortini (an even earlier Executive).

## 1. Who they are - **Full name:** Bruce Dammeier. - **Born:** Not confirmed in this pass. No date of birth in the records reviewed. - **School:** B.S. in Ocean Engineering from the U.S. Naval Academy, graduated with distinction. M.S. in Engineering from the University of Washington (UW). - **Military:** 8 years as a U.S. Navy Civil Engineer Corps Officer. He went on relief missions. - **Career:** Naval officer → ran a family printing business → community volunteer → WA House District 25 (2009–2013) → WA Senate District 25 (2013–2017, Senate Majority Caucus Vice Chair) → Pierce County Executive (2017–2024). - **Family:** Married to Lauren Dammeier. - **Home:** Pierce County native. He went to Curtis High School in the University Place area.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Elected executive power (from his office):** As Pierce County Executive from 2017 to 2024, Dammeier could veto Council laws. He hired the heads of about 30 county departments. He signed contracts and deals with other governments. He used the veto in public. His most public veto was Ordinance 2024-540s on large tent encampments in August 2024. - **Budget power (decisions on money):** He wrote and negotiated the county's two-year budget, worth more than $1B for operations. He also decided how to spend federal ARPA funds, homelessness programs, and the Building Together Initiative. - **Bridge role to nonprofits:** He sat on boards for community groups like Tacoma Goodwill and Mel Korum YMCA. As Executive, his seat on these boards linked county power to the local nonprofit world. - **Scoring basis:** Former Executive weight 3 + Pierce GOP member weight 3.
