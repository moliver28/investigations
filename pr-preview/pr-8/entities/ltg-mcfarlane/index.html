## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why he is on the map:** LTG McFarlane commands Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM), the largest Army base on the West Coast. He holds this power from holding office: he is the senior Army officer at JBLM. He makes decisions that shape a base worth about $14 billion a year to Washington State and about $3.9 billion a year to Pierce County (source #4, #5). The base supports over 85,000 jobs across Pierce and Thurston counties (source #5). JBLM is also a key launch point for military operations in the Indo-Pacific region (source #3). That mix of money, jobs, and military reach puts the base commander on this map. - **What he does:** He runs JBLM and I Corps. He oversees about 45,000 military and civilian workers on base. His decisions on base operations, training, and deployment tempo shape the local economy. He also meets with local civic groups, like the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber and the Rainier Club (source #6, #7). - **Who is NOT mapped:** Other JBLM leaders, like the 62nd Airlift Wing commander and the Madigan Army Medical Center commander.
