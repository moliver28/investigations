## KEY POWER DYNAMICS — Cities

1. **Tacoma dominates** — 220K of the county's ~960K. Mayor Ibsen + council are the most consequential municipal body.
2. **Puyallup has Jim Kastama** — Former state senator on city council = outsized political influence.
3. **Gig Harbor is the wealth corridor** — Council makeup matters for shoreline development and environmental regulation.
4. **Bonney Lake + Edgewood + Sumner** — Growing eastern suburbs; land use decisions are the power lever.
5. **Lakewood** is its own investigation (INV-2026-001).
6. **Most small towns operate with minimal resources** — dependent on county contracts, fire districts, water districts.
