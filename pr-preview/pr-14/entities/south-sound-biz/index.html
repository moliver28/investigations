## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Inclusion rule:** Reputational and media reach. South Sound Business is the only monthly magazine in the South Sound that covers only business topics. It serves Pierce, South King, Kitsap, and Thurston counties, plus Bainbridge Island. It is the main chronicler of the Pierce County business community. It covers the port, MultiCare (a hospital system), and Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM) contractors. It also covers regional banks and the local Chamber. The magazine calls itself the "#1 business communication media in the South Sound since 1985." Its editorial choices shape which businesses get steady regional attention. - **Adjacent but EXCLUDED candidates:** The News Tribune, the main daily paper, is owned separately. Seattle Business Magazine and 425 Business are run by Premier Media Group. KNKX is public radio. Tacoma Weekly is another local outlet. KING5 runs a South Sound bureau.

## 1. Who they are - **Publication title:** South Sound Business (current brand). Earlier called *South Sound Biz* from 1985 to 2021 under Business Examiner Media. - **Publisher:** Premier Media Group (PMG), based in Tacoma, WA. Mailing address: P.O. Box 1362, Tacoma, WA 98401-1362. Phone: (253) 588-5340. Web: https://www.southsoundbiz.com/. - **Acquisition history:** Launched in 1985 by Business Examiner Media, founded by Jeff and Jill Rounce. Acquired by Premier Media Group, founded by Josh and Michelle Dunn, around August 19, 2021. Rebranded from "South Sound Biz" to "South Sound Business" after the sale. - **Editorial staff (current, per About page):** Jeff Burlingame is Editor in Chief. He worked at Seattle University and the Gates Foundation. He won a Sigma Delta Chi award from the Society of Professional Journalists (SPJ). Joanna Kresge is Managing Editor. - **Recognition:** 2023 Silver Best of Show Excellence Award for Best Website. The award came from the Alliance of Area Business Publishers at its summer conference in Detroit.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Niche dominance:** The magazine calls itself the "only local media dedicated exclusively to business topics in the South Sound." No other outlet fills that niche since PMG bought Business Examiner Media. - **Geographic reach:** It covers 24 cities across Pierce, South King, Kitsap, and Thurston counties. It also covers Bainbridge Island. It is produced in Tacoma. It reaches leaders at CHI Franciscan, JBLM, MultiCare, Dillanos, Heritage Bank, and Umpqua. - **Award-winning:** 2023 Alliance of Area Business Publishers (AABP) Silver Best of Show for Best Website. - **Format:** Monthly print and digital, plus "Hallmark Events" like Women to Watch. These events are both marketing tools and access points for the region's business elite.

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** South Sound Business holds a structural monopoly on regional business-magazine coverage in the South Sound. No daily paper, TV station, or public radio outlet fills that niche. Its parent, Premier Media Group, also publishes the Eastside's *425 Business*. That makes the Dunn family the dominant regional business-magazine consolidator. The publication's editorial choices shape the reputation of Pierce County's business elite. Spotlight on Business Awards, Women to Watch, and "Lessons from a Leader" features act as informal reputation currency. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** Editor-in-chief profiles, "Lessons from a Leader" features, and Spotlight on Business Awards are advertising-adjacent formats. The line between editorial and advertorial blurs when advertisers are also subjects. The defense: the publication discloses paid partnerships and runs a separate "advertising" section. - **Analysis of alternatives:** A defense-friendly reading is that the magazine is a normal regional business-to-business (B2B) publication. It serves the information needs of South Sound executives. A critical reading is that — combined with sibling titles 425, 425 Business, South Sound, and West Sound — the Dunn family controls an outsized share of regional business journalism. Josh Dunn sits on the UW Milgard School of Business board. He also sits on the Association of Washington Business board. That gives the company direct policy and education access. - **Confidence:** High — multiple PMG-controlled primary sources plus Chamber directory and acquisition-announcement corroborate.
