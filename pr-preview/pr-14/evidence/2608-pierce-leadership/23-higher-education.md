## Overview

Pierce County is home to **four degree-granting institutions of higher education**, each led by a president or chancellor who wields institutional, economic, and political power. This file maps leadership, governing boards, budgets, enrollment, key partnerships, expansion plans, and political/economic influence for each.

**Why This Matters (from supplementary gap analysis, Dimension 4):**
- **PLU–MultiCare partnership** (announced Feb 2024) is a major corporate-education alliance that was entirely unmapped.
- **TCC Board of Trustees** includes Dona Ponepinto (United Way of Pierce County CEO / Tacoma Art Museum chair / 7+ board interlocks) — another node in her network.
- **UW Tacoma** is driving downtown Tacoma's physical redevelopment — the campus expansion is a key driver of the "Tacoma Revival" narrative.
- **Presidents are appointed by state-level bodies** (UW Regents, Governor's office for community college trustees) — a form of external control over local institutions.

**Sources:** Institution websites, UW Board of Regents, WA Governor's office, SBCTC, Pierce County, MultiCare Newsroom, News Tribune, Puget Sound Business Journal, The Urbanist, Tacoma Ledger.

### PLU–MultiCare Partnership for Health Innovation (KEY)

**Announced:** February 7, 2024. Unites **PLU + MultiCare Health System + WSU Elson S. Floyd College of Medicine**.

| Element | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **MultiCare Medical Center at PLU** | New outpatient care center on PLU campus, southeast corner (~15 acres); serves ~200,000 people in Central Pierce County |
| **Funding** | Funded by MultiCare; announced by MultiCare CEO Bill Robertson |
| **Facility scope** | Up to 75,000 sq ft medical office building + parking; height limit increase 100→140 ft; includes PACE (all-inclusive care for elderly) program |
| **WSU role** | Placing medical students throughout Central Pierce County; students live on PLU campus; access to PLU/MultiCare clinical facilities; expanding graduate medical education to retain residents in Pierce County |
| **Land use** | Pierce County Major Amendment to PLU's Major Institution Master Plan (MIMP) — submitted 2026; presented to Parkland-Spanaway-Midland Land Use Advisory Commission March 4, 2026 |

**Why it matters:** This is a major corporate-education-health alliance that ties PLU (and its Board of Regents, which includes Greater Tacoma Community Foundation CEO Kathi Littmann) directly to MultiCare's expansion in Central Pierce County. It also brings WSU's medical school into Pierce County — a significant new health-education power center in Parkland-Spanaway, a medically underserved area.

### Political / Economic Influence

- **2025 legislative session:** A last-minute funding cut to the **Washington College Bound Scholarship** for students at independent not-for-profit universities (like PLU) was set to take effect 2027-28 — a direct state-policy vulnerability PLU's president publicly flagged.

## Cross-Cutting Power Observations

1. **MultiCare is the connective tissue.** MultiCare interlocks appear at TCC (Lois Bernstein on board), PLU (Partnership for Health Innovation + Medical Center), and Bates (Phlebotomy partnership). MultiCare CEO Bill Robertson is a central figure. This reinforces the MultiCare deep-dive (file 08).

2. **Dona Ponepinto's network extends into higher ed.** As TCC Board Chair, the United Way CEO controls a seat on the county's largest community college board — adding to her 7+ interlocks (foundations, arts, nonprofits).

3. **External appointment = external control.** All three public institutions (UW Tacoma, TCC, Bates) are governed by boards appointed by the WA Governor (UW Regents for UW Tacoma). This is a structural channel for state-level influence over local institutions.

4. **Downtown Tacoma redevelopment is anchored by UW Tacoma.** The $115M housing project + master plan to double enrollment to 10,000 is a primary driver of the "Tacoma Revival" narrative and downtown physical transformation.

5. **PLU–MultiCare–WSU is a new health-education power center** in Parkland-Spanaway, bringing WSU's medical school into Pierce County and building a MultiCare medical center on PLU's campus — a major land-use and economic development event in Central Pierce County.

6. **Tribal/county interlocks:** Andrew Strobel (TCC board) is simultaneously a senior advisor to Pierce County Executive Ryan Mello and former Puyallup Tribe planning director; UW Regents Chair Leonard Forsman is Suquamish Tribal Chairman. The Puyallup Tribe is UW Tacoma's official tribal sponsor.
