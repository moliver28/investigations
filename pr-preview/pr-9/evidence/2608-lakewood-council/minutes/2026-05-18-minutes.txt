ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION
Review of the Downtown Transportation Feasibility Study: Draft
Public Participation Plan.
Angie Silva, Assistant Director, Planning and Public Works provided a
high-level overview of the draft Public Participation Plan (PPP) which
outlines an engagement strategy for the Lakewood Downtown
Transportation Feasibility Study. She reviewed the study area, project
scope, key deliverables, engagement strategies and tentative project
schedule. Discussion ensued.
Review of the Automated Traffic Safety Camera Program.
Heidi Ann Wachter, City Attorney provided an overview of the feedback
provided by the Public Safety Advisory Committee (PSAC) related to
proposed parks and school locations for automated traffic safety
cameras. She shared graphs comparing collision data and traffic counts
for each location. Discussion ensued and the City Council requested a
future meeting to discuss and decide on the total number of cameras
and locations based on public safety.
