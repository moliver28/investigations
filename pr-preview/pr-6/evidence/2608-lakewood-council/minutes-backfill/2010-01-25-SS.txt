IT EMS FOR DISCUSSION:

Review of t he proposed CDBG Economic Development program.

Economic Development Manager Chambers-Grady reviewed the proposed CDBG economic development program to provide small
business loans to create jobs for low-income individuals.

Suzanne Tessaro, Operations Manager of Community Capital Development (CCD) and Linda Jadwin, Director of the South Sound
Women?s Business Center (SSWBC), provided background information about the agencies and workshops they provide to
entrepreneurs.

Discussion ensued about how the CCD and SSWBC work with banks and credit unions for providing loans; how do their loan program
compliment the Small Business Administration loan program; how is the $75,000 CDBG and $75,000 CCD funds going to be handled as
money is repaid and how are the funds accounted for; what is the minimum amount of time should the City contract with CCD and
SSWBC; will the administrative fee for service be constant over a five-year contract, as proposed; where does the $75,000 matching
funds come from; must businesses be located in Lakewood; what happens if a business starts in Lakewood and then moves out of
Lakewood; is there any privacy component to which businesses received loans; what businesses have benefited from small business
loans by the SSWBC.

City Council Study Session Minutes -2- January 25, 2010

Further discussion ensued on the high cost of administration by providing $25,000 of the $100,000 allocation (25 percent) for
administering $75,000 of CDBG loans in addition to 20 percent of the CDBG allocation for the administration of the total CDBG program; is
this loan proposal the best utilization of CDBG funds for economic development; would infrastructure improvements such as sewer
connections to Tillicum business in creating jobs be eligible as an economic development activity; what businesses are not able to obtain
loans due to their inability to meet regulatory leveraging requirements; and noting that the CDBG Citizens Advisory Board (CDBG/CAB)
meeting minutes indicates that the CDBG/CAB is asking that the Board and the Council have a clear understanding of the program
provisions that would fall within the CDBG requirement parameters and identify what constitutes the City parameters.
