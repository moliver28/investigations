PUBLIC COMMENTS
The City Council received written comments in advance of the meeting
from Michelle Le.

Roger Neal, Steilacoom resident , presented the City with a Certificate of
Recognition from AARP for driver safety.

Jason Gerwen, Parks and Recreation Advisory Board , spoke in support of

the H-Barn Restoration Project with leed certification and a sustainable
design.

Joel Vranna, Eagle Point Homeowners Association President , spoke
about the water quality on American Lake.

Joel Kangiser , Lakewood resident , spoke about water quality on
American Lake and in support of assessment changes.

Paul Brice, Lakewood resident , spoke in support of retail cannabis in the
City.

Christina Manetti, Lakewood resident, spoke in opposition to the
Portland Loo and Edgewater Park redevelopment project.

Christina Manetti, Garry Oak Coalition, spoke about the clear cutting on

112th Street at Interlaaken Drive and in support of the City taking action to
preserve and protect the tree canopy.

James Dunlop, Lakewood resident , spoke about Edgewater Park,

turning the property into a parking lot and questioned why the City is
redeveloping the park.

Terry Wise , spoke about the contract for abatement of 5105 Solberg

Drive SW and the Lakewood Municipal Code related to the impervious
footprint of the property.

Michelle Le , Lakewood resident , spoke about her experience in

residential real estate and the contract for abatement of property at
5105 Solberg Drive SW.

Tichomir Dunlop, Lakewood resident , spoke about the clear cutting on

112th Street at Interlaaken Drive and in support of the City taking action to
prevent clear cutting of trees.

Joseph Ramos, Apartment Association of Greater Los Angeles , spoke in
opposition to Ordinance No. 849 related to rental licensing and
inspection requirements.

CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
Councilmember Lauricella shared that he attended the Public Safety
Advisory Committee meeting where there was discussion regarding
the automatic traffic safety camera program and safety on motorized
scooters or e-bikes. He shared that he looks forward to Parks
Appreciation Day and the City Council Retreat.
Councilmember Lindholm requested that the City Council issue a
proclamation on Human Trafficking. He thanked Mayor Bocchi for
attending the Lakewood Rotary meeting.
Councilmember Talbo shared that she attended the Planning
Commission meeting where there was discussion regarding the
formation of the NECC Ad Hoc Committee. She thanked AARP for
hosting classes in Lakewood and shared that she looks forward to
the Wards Lake Park Grand Opening event.
Councilmember Brandstetter shared that he will attend the Tillicum
Woodbrook Neighborhood Association meeting and meetings with
South Sound 911.
Councilmember Pearson shared that last week he attended the
Community Healthcare Lunch and Laughter event, the Parks
and Recreation Advisory Board meeting and the Ad Hoc Planning
Committee meeting for the H-Barn Restoration Project. He
requested a Council discussion regarding the Resolution expressing
commitment to Unified Regional Approach to Homelessness.
Deputy Mayor Belle shared that she attended the Youth Council
meeting where Elijah StaAna was presented with the Lakewood First
Lions scholarship and she thanked the Council for their discussions this
evening.
Mayor Bocchi shared that he attended the Lakewood Rotary meeting
and spoke about individuals picking up garbage in Kiwanis Park.
