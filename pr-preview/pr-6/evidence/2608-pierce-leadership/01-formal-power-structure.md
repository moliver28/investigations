## 1. COUNTY EXECUTIVE

### Ryan N. Mello (Democrat) — County Executive since Jan 2025
- **Power Basis:** Elected countywide (52-48% in 2024, narrowly defeating Kelly Chambers)
- **Background:** Born 1978/79 in Kailua, HI. BA from University of Puget Sound (2001). President of student government (ASUPS) 2000-2001. AmeriCorps alum. Staffer for State Rep Hans Dunshee.
- **Political Rise:** Metro Parks Tacoma Commissioner (2005, defeated incumbent) → Tacoma City Council (2010-2019, appointed then twice elected) → Pierce County Council District 4 (2021-2025) → County Executive (2025-present)
- **Nonprofit/Civic:** Former Pierce County Director of Cascade Land Conservancy (2006), United Way of Pierce County staff (2001-2003)
- **Domains of Power:** Government (executive), Economic (budget control — $3.5B biennial budget), Political (Democratic party leader, statewide profile as potentially first openly-gay county executive in WA)
- **Key Powers:** Veto, budget proposal, executive appointments, land use decisions
- **Forward Together Agenda:** Housing, juvenile justice reform, public safety, inclusivity
- **Notes:** First openly gay county executive in Washington. Narrow victory margin suggests potential vulnerability in 2028. Former environmental/conservancy background.

### Bruce Dammeier (Republican) — County Executive 2017-2024 (term-limited)
- **Power Basis:** Retired but still influential. Former two-term executive
- **Background:** M.S. Engineering UW, B.S. Ocean Engineering US Naval Academy. Civil Engineer Corps Officer. Small business owner/printing company director.
- **Rise:** Puyallup School Board → State House (2009-2013) → State Senate (2013-2017) → County Executive
- **Current Influence:** Still embedded in Republican donor network + Puyallup GOP establishment

## 3. COUNTY-WIDE ELECTED OFFICIALS

### Sheriff — Keith Swank (since Jan 2025)
- **Background:** 33 years Seattle PD (patrol officer → captain). Army veteran (Ranger, Airborne, HALO). B.S. Psychology UW. Born Feb 1968 (Silver Spring, MD). Graduated Everett Area High School (PA).
- **Style/Controversy:** Already clashed with County Executive Mello over authority. Sued by county over legal representation. Feuded with Prosecutor Robnett. WSPA considered expulsion over legislative testimony.
- **Power Basis:** Elected countywide. Controls law enforcement, jail, licensing. "Constitutional sheriff" alignment disputed.
- **Power Score:** High autonomy — WA sheriffs are elected independently of county executive, giving them substantial independence.

### Prosecuting Attorney — Mary Robnett (since 2019)
- **Background:** Joined PA's office 1994. Chief Criminal Deputy. Special Assault Unit (10 yrs). Prosecuted prominent cases (Zina Linnik homicide, Parkland Rapist, Edgewood Craigslist murders). Previously in AG's Sexually Violent Predators unit. University of Puget Sound law school (with honors). Started career as emergency dispatcher.
- **Style:** Non-partisan framing. Focus on de-politicizing office. Strong law enforcement community support.
- **Power Basis:** Controls all felony prosecutions in county. Civil division advises county government. Family support division.

### Auditor — Linda Farmer
- **Power:** Elections administration, financial oversight, recording
- **Salary:** $167,019 + 2% raise (2026)

### Assessor-Treasurer — Marty Campbell
- **Background:** Previously County Council District 5 (left seat for this role)
- **Power:** Property valuations, tax collection. Directly impacts every property owner in county.
- **Salary:** $167,019 + 2% raise (2026)

## 7. INSTITUTIONAL & ECONOMIC POWER CENTERS

### Joint Base Lewis-McChord
- **Impact:** 40,000+ military + civilian personnel. Largest employer in Pierce County. $5B+ annual economic impact.
- **Command Power:** I Corps commanding general, JBLM garrison commander
- **Influence:** Land use buffer zones around base, environmental agreements, transportation infrastructure

### MultiCare Health System
- **HQ:** Tacoma. 13 hospitals, 300+ care sites. $7.2B revenue (FY2025).
- **CEO:** William "Bill" Robertson (retiring Dec 2026 → Florence Chang Jan 2027)
- **Board:** Community volunteer board. Key appointments shape region's largest private employer.
- **Influence:** Certificate of need, expansion decisions, charity care, lobbying

### Puyallup Tribe of Indians
- **Economic Power:** Emerald Queen Casino, land holdings, development
- **Sovereignty:** Tribal-state gaming compact, land-into-trust, jurisdictional issues
- **Political Power:** Significant campaign contributions, lobbying on gaming/housing

### CHI Franciscan / Virginia Mason
- Major hospital presence in Tacoma. Catholic health system with ethical/religious directives
