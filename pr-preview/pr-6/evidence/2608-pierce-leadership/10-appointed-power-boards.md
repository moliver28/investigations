## 2. Government Relations (County Lobbying)

Pierce County contracts with a **State Legislative Representative Consultant** who acts as the county's lobbyist in Olympia. The consultant reports to the **Director of Policy** under the Executive.

**Key point:** The county spends taxpayer money on lobbying state government. The consultant and their contract value should be identified — this is a transparency gap.

[Source: https://www.piercecountywa.gov/5753/2053-State-Legislative-Rep-Services]

[Source: https://www.piercecountywa.gov/5944/Planning-Commission]

### Citizens' Salary Commission for Elected Officials
- Sets salaries for County Executive, Assessor-Treasurer, Sheriff, and Auditor
- Recommended 2% raises for 2026-2027
- Direct financial impact on elected officials

### Ethics Commission
- Reviews complaints under Pierce County Ethics Code
- Power to investigate County Executive, Council, and staff

### Transportation Advisory Commission (TAC)
- Policy guidance to Transportation Benefit District, Executive, and Council
- Recommends transportation improvement projects and financing proposals
- Controls tens of millions in infrastructure spending

### South Sound Housing Affordability Partners (SSHAP)
- **Executive Board includes:** Ryan Mello (County Exec), Robyn Denson (Council), mayors from Auburn, Puyallup, Sumner, Fife, Gig Harbor, DuPont; council members from Edgewood, Lakewood (Mike Brandstetter), Tacoma (Sandesh Sadalge), Fircrest, Steilacoom
- **Key power:** Allocates affordable housing funding across the county
- [Source: SSHAP Board Meeting Packet April 2026]

### Civil Service Commission
- Personnel policies for competitive testing and hiring
- Controls hiring/appeals process for county positions

### LEOFF 1 Administration Board
- Police and fire pension administration for Pierce County (excluding Tacoma and Puyallup)

### Arts Commission (9 members)
- Allocates cultural funding county-wide
