## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Linda Farmer is the Pierce County Auditor. She runs every election in Pierce County. That makes her a key power holder. - She was elected countywide in 2022 in a nonpartisan race. She is independent from the County Council and the County Executive on election matters. - She also runs the Recording and Licensing office (deeds, marriage licenses, vehicle titles, passports). She manages a $28M budget and about 65 full-time staff plus 200 to 300 part-time election workers. - She runs Pierce County elections for about 600,000 voters. That includes vote-by-mail, ballot design, 51 drop boxes, and signature checks. - Her reach goes beyond Pierce County. She co-chairs the VoteWA steering committee (the state's voter-registration portal). She co-chairs the legislative committee for the Washington State Association of County Auditors (WSACA). She sits on the state Voting Systems Review Board. These seats let her shape how Washington runs its elections. - She also serves as the Administrator for the Pierce County Opioid Abatement Council. In that role, she manages how opioid-settlement money is spent. ## 1. Who they are - **Full name:** Linda Farmer, APR, CERA (the credentials are listed after her name on official pages). - **Born:** Not verified in public records. She is from Longview, WA. - **Home:** Lakewood, WA, where she has lived for more than 20 years. - **Family:** Married to Jimmie Farmer, who works in IT and plays music. She started as a newspaper reporter and editor. ## 3. Where their power comes from - **The office itself:** The Auditor is a constitutional county officer. Voters across the county elect her to a four-year term. She is independent from the Council and Executive on election matters. State law (Revised Code of Washington, or RCW, 36.22) and the Pierce County Charter define her role. - **Running elections for 600,000 voters:** She oversees vote-by-mail, ballot design, 51 drop boxes, signature checks, and buying voting machines. Her office has won national Clearie Awards for cybersecurity and signature work. - **Recording and licensing:** Her office records deeds, liens, and other legal documents. It issues marriage licenses, vehicle and boat titles, and accepts passport applications. - **Statewide power:** She co-chairs the VoteWA Executive Steering Committee (the state's voter portal). She co-chairs the WSACA Legislative Committee. She serves on the Washington State Voting Systems Review Board. In 2025 she won the WSACA Heavy Hitter Award for work on 29 fiscal notes and over 70 legislative proposals. - **Opioid settlement role:** She is the Administrator of the Pierce County Opioid Abatement Council. This council decides how to spend opioid-settlement funds in the county. - **Budget and staff:** Her office runs a $28M two-year budget and has 65.5 full-time staff plus 200 to 300 part-time election workers. - **What she cannot do:** The Auditor has no veto over laws. She does not vote on the Council. Her main check on election results is serving on the canvassing board. ## 4.
