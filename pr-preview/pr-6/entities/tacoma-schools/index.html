## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Inclusion rule:** Tacoma Public Schools (TPS) is on this map because it holds office and makes decisions. It is the largest school district in Pierce County. It has 28,272 students across 69 schools (2023-24). It runs a large budget (with a $30M shortfall for 2025-26) and hires hundreds of staff. Its elected board sets education policy for tens of thousands of families. The superintendent, Josh Garcia, is one of the highest-paid public officials in Tacoma at $342,038 (per OpenPayrolls). The district's school-bond link with Pierce County Council (graph edge weight 4) and its partnership with MultiCare Health (weight 3) spread its reach into county finance and healthcare. - **Adjacent but EXCLUDED candidates:** Puyallup School District is the second-largest in the county and is a separate entity.
