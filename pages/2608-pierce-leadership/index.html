Pierce County Leadership Investigation

Case Summary

A systematic investigation into the power structure of Pierce County, Washington — mapping who holds power across government, economic institutions, military, tribes, healthcare, legal systems, and media. The goal is to identify the most influential actors, trace the origins and mechanics of their power, and document what they do with it. This is a broad-structure investigation designed to surface patterns of concentrated influence, conflicts of interest, and institutional capture.

Key Questions

  1. Who are the most powerful individuals and organizations in Pierce County?
  2. What is the basis of their power? (Elected office, wealth, institutional role, inherited position, military rank, etc.)
  3. What do they do with their power? (Policy outcomes, contract awards, land use decisions, influence campaigns)
  4. Which actors span multiple power domains? (Government + corporate + nonprofit + media)
  5. What mechanisms connect power across domains? (Campaign donations, board interlocks, family ties, joint ventures)
  6. Are there patterns of self-dealing, regulatory capture, or conflicts of interest at the county level?
