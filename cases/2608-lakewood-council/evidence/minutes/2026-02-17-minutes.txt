ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION
Unified Regional Approach to Addressing Homelessness.
Planning Manager Tiffany Speir introduced Liza Burell, Uncommon
Bridges. Burell provided an overview of the purpose to agree on and
provide a unified response to homelessness in Pierce County, she
reviewed the project vision, guiding principles, project structure and
reviewed the best national practices around effective regional
approaches. She then reviewed problems for individuals, service
providers and jurisdictions without a unified approach, coordinated
activities around data standards, sharing and a universal process of
assessment and entry and the implementation timeline. Discussion
ensued.

Establishment of a Natural Environment and Climate Change (NECC)
Program Advisory Community Board.
Planning Manager Tiffany Speir shared that feedback is requested
regarding the Natural Environment and Climate Change (NECC)
Program noting that options include adoption of an Ordinance to
establish a stand-alone NECC Advisory Board or amending the Planning
Commission work plan to allow the Commission to serve as the
community advisory board to the NECC Program. After discussion,
refining of goals around the establishment of and Advisory Board will be
reviewed with the City Council again at a future meeting.
