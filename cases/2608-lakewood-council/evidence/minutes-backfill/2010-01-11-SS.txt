IT EMS FOR DISCUSSION:

Review of t he Shoreline Mast er Program Public Part icipat ion Plan.

Assistant City Manager Bugher provided background information about the process for developing the Shoreline Master Program Plan. He
then reviewed the schedule for completion based upon the experience of the Washington State Department of Ecology. He noted that
completion of the Plan is scheduled for June 2012. He indicated that the Council may want to consider the matter about street ends as a
separate component from the Shoreline Plan.

Senior Planner Amrine then reviewed the proposed Shoreline Master Program Public Participation Plan as recommended by the Planning
Advisory Board.

Discussion ensued on the use of focus groups and how will City Committees (i.e. Parks and Recreation Board) be able to provide input;
how will input from neighboring jurisdictions with common shorelines such as Chambers Creek be integrated with the City?s plan; will
notification be provided to residents that may be more than 200 feet from the affected shoreline if the closest property was greater
than 200 feet from the shoreline; Lakewood being responsible for the shoreline planning on the south side of Chambers Creek and
University Place being responsible for the north side of Chambers Creek; what is the definition of reaching the ?underprivileged and hard
to reach? residents; why was Gravelly Lake Drive, Steilacoom Lake and Waughop Lake grouped together; and whether the focus group
meetings will be facilitated by the consultants.
