Speaking before the Council were:

Dennis Haugen, Lakewood resident, commented that obtaining public comment on the Pt. Defiance Bypass project will not be successful
because there is no management accountability.

Alan Hart, Lakewood resident, indicated that neighbors are actively following the Pt. Defiance Bypass project and they are concerned
with noise, vibration and public safety. He expressed concern about a high speed corridor and if ridership goes up, Lakewood could
increase the number of rail lines and the City could be split into two. He also expressed concern about the Clover Creek crossing and
potential road closure.
