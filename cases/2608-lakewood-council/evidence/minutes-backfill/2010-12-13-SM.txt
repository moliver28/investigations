Lakewood City Council Special Meeting Minutes

-2-

December 13, 2010

BRIEFING BY THE CITY MANAGER
City Manager Neiditz reported that in regards to the sewer franchise, the City and County staff are working on a final form for
the franchise fee. He then commented on a $150,000 agreement with Pierce County for the maintenance of Ft. Steilacoom
Park that will be brought before the Council for consideration in early January.
City Manager Neiditz reported that the Ft. Steilacoom Park restroom project is moving forward.
City Manager Neiditz commented that Parks Director Mary Dodsworth spent a week and a half in Haiti to do medical mission

work.
City Manager Neiditz reported on a tour he attended with Sound Transit to see the rail work that has been done in Tacoma.
City Manager Neiditz then commented on the Joint Council and Legislators meeting today.
City Manager Neiditz announced that the Council meetings of December 20 and 27, 2010 are cancelled.
City Manager Neiditz announced that the 4th Quarterly Employee Annual Event will be held on December 16, 2010, at 8:00
a.m., in the Council Chambers. He invited the Council to attend.
COUNCIL COMMENTS
Councilmember Thomas commented that the Arts Commission and Keep Lakewood Beautiful could work together on
developing some scenic art work on some of the buildings along the route of the Sound Transit rail tour taken today.
Councilmember Brandstetter commented on his experience on the Council this past year.
Councilmember Whalen reflected on the past year as a Councilmember and the many good things that have been
accomplished.
Deputy Mayor Anderson thanked City staff and Councilmembers for their work.
Mayor Richardson stated that the City’s Christmas tree was not lit. He commented that the City was awarded $2.7 million from
the Transportation Improvement Board for construction projects on South Tacoma Way and a $3 million grant for a Bridgeport
Way project.
