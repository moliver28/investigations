December 1, 2025

This is the date set for a public hearing on amendments to the
Lakewood Municipal Code related to the expansion of childcare
locations.
The City Council received written testimony in advance of the meeting
from Tricia Parsons.
Speaking before Council were:

Ebrahim Mirjalili, Lakewood resident, spoke in opposition to the
expansion of childcare locations.

James Dunlop, Lakewood resident, spoke about unintended
consequences of expanding childcare center locations.

Christina Manetti, Lakewood resident, spoke in support of strict
environmental and noise regulations to protect residents and balance
childcare needs with neighborhood livability.
Christina Manetti, Garry Oak Coalition, spoke in opposition to
unmitigated expansion of childcare facilities into residential
neighborhoods due to environmental concerns.

There being no further testimony, the public hearing was declared
closed at 8:47 p.m.
