REPORT S BY T HE CIT Y MANAGER

City Manager Neiditz reported that the Legislative Session is nearing its close, and that the transportation budget for the intersections at
150th and Murray is going through at the $500,000 funding level. The bill to require the election of municipal court judges is dead.

City Manager Neiditz then reported that the Korean business community workshop was held on Friday, February 26, 2010. Twenty
people attended the first of two sessions and that the next session is being held this week.

After a lot of discussion about the Lakewood Sister Cities Association, City Manager Neiditz indicated that a meeting has been set with
Pierce College and Clover Park Technical College representatives to discuss potential staffing support.

City Manager Neiditz commented that the Colonial Center Request for Proposal is completed and that discussions will be held with the
Gramor property owners next week.

With regard to the Pt. Defiance Bypass project, City Manager Neiditz indicated that he had been in communication with the Assistant
Director of the Utilities and Transportation Commission regarding the City’s desire to pursue the process of reviewing the 100th Street,
Steilacoom Boulevard and Bridgeport Way crossings.

PUBLIC COMMENT S
Speaking before the Council were:

Dennis Haugen, Lakewood resident, spoke about property the City acquired and that he was not able to find if the fuel tanks at that
property were removed. He then indicated he would be sending the Council historic emails on how staff treated the tire businessman
who spoke before the Council earlier. He then indicated that the City Attorney and a quality control person should be working for the
Council directly.

Ken Severe, Lakewood resident, thanked the Council for placing the vouchers on the City’s website and requested that the vouchers be
formatted to be more user-friendly. He then spoke about six airline tickets being charged against a credit card. He then indicated that
the credit card policy is not clear about credit card limits and who approves the expenditures.

Becky Huber, Lakewood resident, distributed copies of Lakewood’s history book authored by Steve Dunkelberger and Councilmember
Neary. She thanked the Council for the lodging tax funds for the museum. She announced that the next Lakewood Historical Society
meeting is on March 23, 2010 at the Lakewood Library. She then reviewed upcoming fundraising events of the Society.
