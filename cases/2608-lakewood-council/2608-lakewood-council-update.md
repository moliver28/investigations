## Executive Summary

Investigation into Lakewood WA City Council members has uncovered significant findings regarding employment conflicts, business interests, and campaign finance disclosures. Key concerns identified:

### ⚠️ HIGH PRIORITY CONFLICTS

1. **Paul Bocchi (Mayor)** - Dual employment conflict:
   - Lakewood City Council Mayor (elected)
   - Pierce County Senior Legislative Analyst - Budget (employee)
   - **Conflict:** Makes budget decisions for city while employed by county; county and city have intergovernmental funding relationships

2. **Patti Belle (Deputy Mayor)** - Dual employment conflict:
   - Lakewood City Council Deputy Mayor (elected)
   - Communications Manager, City of Kent Mayor's Office (employee)
   - **Conflict:** Employee of competing municipality while making policy decisions for Lakewood

3. **Philip Lindholm (Councilmember)** - Business interests:
   - CEO/Owner, Concord Counsel (real estate brokerage, mediation, tribal law)
   - Licensed real estate broker in Washington
   - **Conflict:** Real estate business interests while serving on Planning Commission and City Council; makes decisions on development, land use, zoning
   - **Campaign Finance:** Self-funded campaign; PDC records show personal funds used

4. **Ryan Pearson (Councilmember)** - Employment conflict:
   - Pierce County employee (Engineering/Technical Review Manager since 2018)
   - Works on subdivision projects, development regulations, growth management
   - **Conflict:** County employee reviewing/approving developments that affect Lakewood; handles infrastructure planning that intersects with city jurisdiction

### ⚠️ MEDIUM PRIORITY CONCERNS

5. **Mike Brandstetter (Councilmember)** - Multiple board positions:

   - Pierce County Unified Approach to Homelessness Governing Committee

   - **Conflict:** Housing authority decisions may affect personal board positions; public college employment while making education policy

6. **Ellen Talbo (Councilmember)** - Professional ties:
   - Former environmental/transportation planner for engineering consultant firm (2007-2012)
   - Former Planning Commission vice-chair
   - **Conflict:** May have worked on projects affecting Lakewood; professional planning ties to development industry

7. **J. Trestin Lauricella (Councilmember)** - Limited disclosure:
   - Private sector program manager (20 years)
   - MBA from Seattle University, Engineering degree from Saint Martin's
   - **Concern:** Bio vague about current employer; appointed 2023, limited public profile
