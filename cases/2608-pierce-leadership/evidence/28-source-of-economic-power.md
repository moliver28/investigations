# The Source of Economic Power: Government-Created Wealth in Pierce County

**Investigation:** INV-2026-002 (Pierce County Leadership)
**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Author:** Lead investigator
**Status:** Analysis — the structural origin of the power mapped in files 12, 14, 19

---

## The Core Finding

**The "private" economic power in Pierce County is overwhelmingly government-created.** The business elite that dominates the county does not derive its power from independent capital accumulation — it derives it from a series of government grants, appropriations, tax exemptions, and sovereignty grants. The elite then uses that government-created wealth to influence the government that created it. This is structural capture: a feedback loop where public resources become private power, which then shapes public policy.

This reframes the entire investigation. The question is not "who are the powerful businesses?" but **"what public resources did the government give them, and what did they give back?"**

## The Evidence, Entity by Entity

### 1. JBLM — 100% federal appropriations

| Source | Amount (2022) |
|--------|---------------|
| JBLM Payroll | $3,240,000,000 |
| Basic Allowance for Housing | $1,300,000,000 |
| Retiree Pay | $1,270,000,000 |
| Disability Pay | $1,480,000,000 |
| Purchased Healthcare | $113,000,000 |
| School Impact Aid | $12,310,000 |
| Contracts paid | $581,100,000 |
| **TOTAL** | **~$8,000,000,000** |

**Source:** SSMCP REIA 2023 (ssmcp.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/REIA-2023-Pierce-Thurston-as-of-09272023.pdf)

**The structural fact:** JBLM's $12.1B economic impact (UW Tacoma study) is **entirely federal money** — defense appropriations, military payroll, retiree and disability benefits, and defense contracts. There is no "private sector" here. The county's largest employer is a federal government installation. Its power is not economic independence — it is **dependence on a federal appropriation that could be cut or moved at any time.** The county's entire political strategy (SSMCP, lobbying Congress, protecting the base) is the behavior of a client state, not a partner.

**What this means for power:** JBLM's power over the county is real but structurally fragile. It is the power of a landlord who can leave. The county's deference to JBLM (land-use buffers, transportation, housing) is the deference of a tenant.

### 2. MultiCare — a tax-exempt nonprofit funded by government-regulated reimbursement

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Revenue (FY2025) | ~$7.2 billion |
| Tax status | 501(c)(3) nonprofit |
| Program services revenue | $4,795,052,090 (FY2024 990; earlier figure $1,377,489,080 was FY2011) |
| Contributions | $108,924,556 (2.2% of revenue; FY2024 990; earlier $21,876,174 / 1.6% was FY2011) |
| Federal awards | $13.4M from 2 agencies |
| Tax-exempt since | 1991 |

**Sources:** ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/911352172); Fitch Ratings via chiefhealthcareexecutive.com

**The structural fact:** MultiCare is a **nonprofit** — it pays no federal income tax, no state income tax, no property tax, and issues tax-exempt bonds. Its revenue comes from **Medicare and Medicaid (government programs) plus commercial insurance (a government-regulated market).** Nationally, nonprofit hospitals received a **$37.4 billion tax benefit in 2021** (income tax $11.5B, sales tax $9.1B, property tax $7.8B, state income $3.7B, contributions $3.2B, bond financing $2.1B) — per PMC11428023 and CRS IF13192.

**What this means for power:** MultiCare's $7.2B "private" empire is built on:
1. **Tax exemption** — the public forgoes billions in revenue so MultiCare can operate.
2. **Government reimbursement** — Medicare/Medicaid are the largest payers.
3. **Regulated market** — commercial insurance rates are set in a government-regulated system.

The "largest private employer in Pierce County" is, structurally, a **government-subsidized, government-regulated nonprofit** that pays no taxes. Its power over the county is the power of an entity that receives public money while being accountable to no electorate.

### 3. The Puyallup Tribe — sovereignty and land returned by the government

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Treaty | Medicine Creek, Dec 26, 1854 |
| Land ceded | 2.5 million acres |
| Compensation | $32,500 + 3 reservations of 1,280 acres each |
| 1990 Settlement | $162 million + 900 acres returned |
| Sovereignty | Federally recognized, treaty rights |

**Sources:** HistoryLink File 5254; Smithsonian NMAI; puyalluptribe-nsn.gov

**The structural fact:** The Tribe's power rests on **sovereignty granted and recognized by the federal government**, and on **land that the government took in 1854 and partially returned in 1990.** The gaming revenue (Emerald Queen Casino) flows from a **tribal-state gaming compact** — a government-to-government agreement. The Tribe's $1.9M lobbying spend is the behavior of an entity defending a government-granted status.

**What this means for power:** The Tribe is the most structurally independent actor in the county (it can opt out of county jurisdiction), but that independence is itself a **government grant**. The Tribe's power is real, but it is the power of a sovereign whose sovereignty was recognized — and could theoretically be altered — by the federal government.

### 4. Weyerhaeuser — a fortune built on a federal land grant

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Origin | 1864 Northern Pacific Railroad Land Grant Act |
| Purchase | 900,000 acres from Northern Pacific for $6/acre ($5.4M) |
| Source of land | Federal land grant to the railroad (free) |
| Date | Jan 3, 1900 |
| Today | 10.4 million acres, largest private timberland owner in U.S. |

**Sources:** HistoryLink File 22983; streamlinermemories.info; ecobooks.com "Railroads and Clearcuts" (Jensen & Draffan)

**The structural fact:** The single most important fact in Pierce County economic history: **the timber fortune that built Tacoma came from a federal land grant.** Congress gave the Northern Pacific Railroad millions of acres of public land in 1864. The railroad went bankrupt twice, and in 1900 sold 900,000 acres to Frederick Weyerhaeuser for $6/acre — land the government had given away. Weyerhaeuser's descendants and successors (including Green Diamond/Simpson-Reed) still control vast acreage. **The "old money" of Pierce County is, at its root, privatized public land.**

**What this means for power:** The timber dynasties' wealth is a **rentier claim on public resources** — land the government gave away, then timber the companies harvested, then real estate they developed. Their political power (Chamber, lobbying, campaign donations) is the power of beneficiaries defending a historical government giveaway.

### 5. Port of Tacoma — a public entity funded by property tax

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Status | Independent municipal corporation (RCW Title 53) |
| Tax levy (2023) | $25.3M, rising to $28.5M by 2027 |
| Levy use | GO debt, environmental, transportation (not operations) |
| Revenue | NWSA cargo, leases, tax levy |

**Sources:** portoftacoma.com/about/financial-information; Port 2023 Budget (s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com)

**The structural fact:** The Port is a **public entity** — an independent municipal corporation created by the county, funded partly by a **property tax levy** on county residents. Its "economic power" ($76B cargo, 41,000 jobs) is the power of a public agency. The commissioners are elected. The Port is not private capital — it is **public capital managed by elected officials.**

**What this means for power:** The Port's power is legitimate public power, but it is also a **concentration of public resources** that the business elite (Chamber, developers, maritime interests) seeks to influence. The Port is a prize, not a private actor.

## The Synthesis: Structural Capture

### The pattern across all six

| Entity | Source of power | Government role |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| JBLM | $12.1B impact | 100% federal appropriations |
| MultiCare | $7.2B revenue | Tax-exempt + Medicare/Medicaid + regulated market |
| Puyallup Tribe | Sovereignty + gaming | Federal recognition + gaming compact + land return |
| Weyerhaeuser | 10.4M acres | Federal land grant (1864) |
| Port of Tacoma | $76B cargo | Public municipal corp + property tax levy |
| Chamber | $3M budget | Tax-exempt (c)(6) |

**The feedback loop:**
1. **Government creates wealth** — land grants, appropriations, tax exemptions, sovereignty, public levies.
2. **Private actors convert it to power** — timber fortunes, hospital empires, tribal gaming, business coalitions.
3. **That power influences government** — lobbying, campaign donations, Chamber Candidate Academy, board interlocks, revolving door.
4. **Government returns the favor** — more tax breaks, more contracts, more favorable regulation, more land-into-trust, more deference.

This is **structural capture** — not corruption in the criminal sense, but a system where the public's resources are systematically converted into private power that then controls the public's decisions.

### The uncomfortable conclusion

**The "private sector" in Pierce County is largely a government-created rentier class.** The business elite's power is not evidence of a vibrant independent economy — it is evidence of a **public-private partnership in which the public provides the capital (land, money, tax exemption, sovereignty) and the private actors keep the returns.** The Chamber's "growth machine" ideology serves this arrangement: growth justifies more land conversion, more development, more tax revenue, more government spending — all of which flows back to the same elite.

### What this means for the investigation

1. **The composite power score should weight "government-dependence" as a vulnerability, not just a strength.** JBLM, MultiCare, and the Tribe are powerful, but their power is contingent on government grants that could change.

2. **The real power question is not "who holds economic power" but "who controls the government's allocation of public resources."** That's the County Council (budget, land use), the Executive (contracts, appointments), the Port (levy, leases), and the state/federal delegations (appropriations, grants).

3. **The most powerful actors are those who can convert public resources into private power AND back again** — the interlocks (Wiborg, Ponepinto, Lawver) who sit on both the government-facing and private-facing boards.

4. **The "growth machine" is not a metaphor — it is a literal machine for converting public land and tax revenue into private wealth.** The Tideflats, the timberlands, the hospital system, the casino — all are public resources (or government-granted privileges) generating private returns.
