## Overview

- **Federally recognized tribe** — Medicine Creek Treaty of 1854
- **Enrolled members:** ~4,000
- **Reservation:** ~73,935 km² (28,546 sq mi) — checkerboard pattern across Pierce County
- **Government:** Tribal Council (sk̓ʷapad ʔə tiiɫ siʔiʔab)
- **Economic Engine:** Emerald Queen Casino, real estate development, various enterprises

The Puyallup Tribe is one of the **most economically powerful entities in Pierce County** — operating the largest casino in the region, holding substantial land parcels (including in downtown Tacoma), and exercising sovereign authority that intersects with local, county, and state governments.

## Economic Enterprises

### Emerald Queen Casino (EQC)
- **Operations:** Large casino and hotel complex on I-5 at Fife, plus smaller venue in Tacoma
- **Economic Impact:** Major employer, significant revenue source for tribal government
- **Regulatory:** Governed by Tribal-State Gaming Compact with WA State

### Other Enterprises
- Real estate development arm (mixed-use, residential, commercial)
- Trust land acquisitions (land-into-trust process with DOI)

## Political Power & Sovereignty

### Key Jurisdictional Issues:
1. **Gaming compact** — Negotiated with WA State; any amendments flow through Olympia
2. **Land-into-trust** — Tribe seeks to place land under federal trust status (removes local tax base, brings under tribal jurisdiction). Often contested by Pierce County and municipalities.
3. **Taxation** — Tribal enterprises on trust land are generally exempt from state/county taxes
4. **Environmental regulation** — Tribe has authority over its reservation lands, including Puyallup River watershed
5. **Housing** — Tribal housing authority operates across reservation checkerboard

### Campaign Contributions
- Puyallup Tribe is a **consistent campaign donor** in Pierce County and WA state races
- Tribal contributions typically go through tribal enterprise entities or directly from tribal council members
- **WA PDC records needed** to quantify giving patterns

### Cross-Government Relationships
- **Pierce County:** Ongoing negotiations over land use, taxation, and services
- **City of Tacoma:** Joint planning on Tideflats, downtown development, Port
- **Port of Tacoma:** Environmental and economic cooperation on Tideflats
- **WA State:** Gaming compact, transportation, natural resources
