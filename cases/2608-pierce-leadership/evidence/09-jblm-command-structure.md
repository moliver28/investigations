## 6. JBLM's Formal and Informal Relationships with Pierce County Government

### 6.1 South Sound Military and Communities Partnership (SSMCP)
- **The primary vehicle** for formal collaboration between JBLM and local governments
- Membership: 50+ members including cities, counties, tribes, nonprofits, corporations, and JBLM
- **Memorandum of Agreement (MOA)** — a formal written understanding between multiple jurisdictions to cooperate on agreed projects
- **Governance Structure:**
  - Executive Leadership Team (ELT) — provides strategic guidance
  - Steering Committee — reps from Nisqually Indian Tribe, Thurston County, City of DuPont, Puget Sound Energy, City of Tacoma, City of University Place, City of Yelm
  - Working Groups: Healthcare, Social Services, Education, Transportation, Business & Economic Development
  - Elected Officials Council (EOC) — forum for JBLM leadership, council members, mayors, and state legislators
- **Program Manager:** Maria Tobin (253-983-7804, mtobin@cityoflakewood.us)
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/ssmcp-memorandum-of-agreement
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/steering-committee
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/elected-officials-council
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/ssmcp-leadership/

### 6.2 Joint Land Use Study (JLUS) — 2015
- Collaborative process among federal, regional, local governments, tribes, and public
- Designed to balance military operational demands with community land use plans, economic development, and environmental sustainability
- Study area: communities within ~2 miles of JBLM boundary
- **Key Issues Addressed:** Urban growth encroachment, aircraft safety zones, noise compatibility, threatened/endangered species, transportation, unauthorized use of training lands
- **Participating Agencies:** Pierce County, Thurston County, City of Lakewood, DuPont, Tacoma, Steilacoom, Lacey, Yelm, Roy, Rainier, Nisqually Indian Tribe, among others
- Not binding — participants asked to make "good faith efforts" to implement recommendations
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/joint-land-use-study

### 6.3 JBLM North Clear Zone
- Active SSMCP project addressing land use compatibility around McChord Field's north clear zone
- Involves Pierce County planning processes
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/jblm-north-clear-zone/

### 6.4 Growth Coordination Plan (2022)
- Framework of implementable action items to improve quality of life in JBLM region while balancing military readiness
- Builds on 2010 GCP
- Addresses off-base housing (71% of active duty live off-base), infrastructure, transportation
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/2022-jblm-growth-coordination-plan/

### 6.5 Transportation Coordination
- I-5 JBLM corridor improvements coordinated with WSDOT
- JLUS incorporates regional transportation work by WSDOT
- SSMCP advocates for: widening DuPont/Steilacoom Road from Integrity Gate to Exit 119, addressing at-grade rail crossing near JBLM main gate (Exit 120), SR 510 Yelm Loop completion
- **Source:** SSMCP home page / legislative advocacy section

### 6.6 Environmental Coordination
- JBLM and partners work on prairie habitat protection/restoration (threatened and endangered species)
- Air Installations Compatible Use Zone (AICUZ) study for noise footprint and accident potential zones
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/joint-land-use-study

### 6.7 Direct Pierce County Engagement
- Pierce County Councilmember (District 6) represents the JBLM area — currently elected official serves as liaison
- **JBLM Community Relations** office supports relationships between JBLM and surrounding communities
- **JBLM Hall of Fame** recognizes community leaders — notable inductees include Pierce County Councilmember Dick Muri (past)
- **Pierce Military & Business Alliance (PMBA)** — nonprofit supporting morale, welfare, readiness; coordinates Operation Turkey Drop and other community events
- **Source:** https://home.army.mil/lewis-mcchord/my-Joint-Base-Lewis-Mcchord/all-services/public-affairs/community-relations
- **Source:** https://home.army.mil/lewis-mcchord/about/civilian-hall-fame
- **Source:** https://blog.piercecountywa.gov/janihitchen/2025/05/20/military-impacts-on-pierce-county

## 7. Economic Impact Figures

### Most Authoritative Numbers (in order of recency/preference)

| Figure | Source | Year | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **$12.1 billion** | UW Tacoma Center for Business Analytics (per City of Lakewood) | 2023 (study period) | Pierce + Thurston counties combined total economic impact |
| **~$8 billion direct impact** | SSMCP Regional Economic Impact Analysis (per SSMCP.org) | 2023 | Direct economic impact (payroll $3.24B, BAH $1.3B, retiree pay $1.27B, disability $1.48B, contracts $581M, etc.) |
| **$9.0 billion** economic output + $7.6M labor income | PSRC Draft Regional Economic Strategy | 2024 (data year) | Central Puget Sound region — JBLM directly providing ~57,000 jobs |
| **$8.3–$9.2 billion** | UW Tacoma CBA / SSMCP (original 2018 study) | 2017-2018 data | South Sound (17 communities in Pierce + Thurston) |
| **"Previous studies found economic impact approached $15.0B"** | SSMCP 2024 Business Survey Summary Report | 2024 (citing earlier studies) | South Sound region (broader multiplier effects) |

### Key Employment Numbers
- **~57,000 direct jobs** (PSRC 2024 data)
- **85,400+** total jobs (direct + spin-off) in Pierce + Thurston counties
- **4th largest employer in Washington State**; #1 employer in Pierce County
- **Nearly 130,000** total population serviced (52,000+ active duty/DoD civilians + 45,000 dependents + 32,000 retirees)
- **$487 million** in state and local taxes contributed
- **~100,000 FTEs** statewide earning ~$9B in wages, producing ~$14B in gross output, generating $2.25B+ in government revenue

### Most Authoritative Source
The **2023 JBLM Regional Economic Impact Report** (SSMCP / UW Tacoma Center for Business Analytics) — https://www.ssmcp.org/5986-2/ — with the full report PDF at:
https://www.ssmcp.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/REIA-2023-Pierce-Thurston-as-of-09272023.pdf

The widely cited **$12.1 billion figure** (City of Lakewood) appears to include direct + indirect + induced multiplier effects across Pierce and Thurston counties.

## 8. Community Engagement — How Base Leadership Interfaces with County/City Government

### 8.1 Formal Channels

**SSMCP Elected Officials Council (EOC)**
- The primary formal forum connecting JBLM leadership with local/state elected officials
- Meets periodically (May and November events typical)
- Brings together JBLM command leadership, city council members, mayors, and state legislators
- Recent examples: May 2026 EOC with presentation materials available; Nov 2025 EOC
- Registration via Eventbrite; open to SSMCP members
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/elected-officials-council
- **Source:** https://www.eventbrite.com/e/ssmcp-elected-officials-council-nov-2026-tickets-1991800383565

**SSMCP Executive Leadership Team**
- Strategic guidance body with JBLM representation
- Current SSMCP ELT includes: David Fullmer (JBLM Headquarters), Don Anderson (Pierce County), Tho Kraus (Chair, City of Lakewood), Scott Spence (Vice-Chair, City of Lacey)
- **Source:** https://www.ssmcp.org/home

**Joint Base Partnership Council**
- Formal council where the I Corps CG (Army senior commander) and 62nd AW Commander (Air Force senior component commander) coordinate with tenant unit leaders

### 8.2 Informal/Liaison Channels

**JBLM Community Relations Office**
- Part of the Joint Base Public Affairs Office
- Supports and sustains relationships between service members/leaders and citizens of neighboring communities
- Coordinates tours, speaking engagements, events
- **Source:** https://home.army.mil/lewis-mcchord/my-Joint-Base-Lewis-Mcchord/all-services/public-affairs/community-relations

**Pierce Military & Business Alliance (PMBA)**
- 501(c)3 nonprofit supporting JBLM morale, welfare, readiness, and retention
- Coordinates community events (Operation Turkey Drop, etc.)
- Listed under Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber
- **Source:** https://www.facebook.com/Piercemilitaryandbusinessalliance
- **Source:** https://business.tacomachamber.org/list/member/pierce-military-and-business-alliance-1114357

**"Community Connector" Events**
- 7th Infantry Division hosts periodic community connector events (e.g., "Olympia Leaders, 7ID Tour Around JBLM in Spring Community Connector Event" — 2022)
- JBLM hosts annual Armed Forces Day, Freedom Fest, and other public-facing events
- I Corps Freedom Fest Unit Run (2026) with installation-wide participation

**Pierce County Council District 6**
- District 6 encompasses JBLM — the sitting council member serves as a direct political liaison
- Councilmember Jani Hitchen (as of 2025) has blogged about military impacts, housing, and childcare issues affecting JBLM personnel
- **Source:** https://blog.piercecountywa.gov/janihitchen/2025/05/20/military-impacts-on-pierce-county

**School Liaison Program**
- JBLM School Liaison Officers coordinate between military families and local school districts (Clover Park, Steilacoom, and other affected districts)
- **Source:** https://jblm.armymwr.com/programs/school-support-services

**Civic Leader Tours and Media Engagement**
- 62nd Airlift Wing Public Affairs handles media queries and civic leader engagement (253-982-5638)
- 62d AW leadership and Airmen pictured with civic leaders on McChord flightline (May 19, 2026) — demonstrating direct community engagement at the wing level
- **Source:** https://www.mcchord.af.mil/Units/62nd-Airlift-Wing-Public-Affairs
