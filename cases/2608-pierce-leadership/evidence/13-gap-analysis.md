## Executive Summary

The existing investigation (evidence/01–12) produced a robust **formal power structure map** — elected officials, campaign finance, lobbying, institutional actors, media ownership, and a NetworkX power matrix ranking 25 actors across 6 domains. This is a strong foundation. However, formal power mapping systematically misses **10 dimensions of informal, structural, and hidden power** that are critical to understanding who really governs Pierce County.

This gap analysis identifies 10 missing dimensions, scores each for investigative priority, provides specific names and organizations uncovered through web research, and proposes actionable next steps to fill each gap.

**Priority Scale:**
- **CRITICAL** — Material gap that undermines the core thesis if unfilled
- **HIGH** — Significant missing power center that changes the network map
- **MEDIUM** — Important context; enriches but doesn't invalidate existing analysis
- **LOW** — Useful background; can be deferred

## Dimension 1: HIDDEN / INFORMAL POWER — The Unelected Power Structure

### What We Have
- Named elected officials (County Executive, Council, Sheriff, Prosecutor)
- Mapped campaign donors and lobbying firms
- Identified John Wiborg (MultiCare Chair / Stellar Industrial CEO) as an interlock node
- Named Gordon Thomas Honeywell as the dominant lobbying firm

### What's Missing

**The Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber of Commerce** — The single most important unelected power structure in the county. The Chamber:
- Has a 25-member Board of Directors representing the county's largest employers
- Board Chairs rotate: **Chyna Willman** (FY2025–2026), **Kierra Phifer** (prior), **AJ Gordon** of Banner Bank (prior)
- Has a $4.5M budget and 15 full-time staff
- Interim President/CEO is **Tom Pierson** (returned after Andrea Reay left for WA Hospitality Association, Sept 2025)
- Runs: Candidate Academy, Business Leadership Academy, Manufacturing Industrial Council for the South Sound, Spaceworks Tacoma
- Operates at 950 Pacific Ave, Suite 300, Tacoma

**Key Finding:** The Chamber is a **shadow political party for business interests** — it runs a Candidate Academy that trains and vets candidates, endorses, and lobbies across all levels. **No elected official in Pierce County can win without Chamber support or active opposition.** This institution was completely absent from the power matrix.

**Private Social Clubs (Old Tacoma Elite):**
- **Tacoma Yacht Club** (est. 1889) — 26 founding members of Tacoma's elite. Located on Belvedere Cove. Generations of old Tacoma families meet here.
- **Tacoma Country & Golf Club** (est. 1894) — Founded by Scotsmen employed by Balfour, Guthrie & Co. The historic venue for Tacoma's business aristocracy. Course moved to current location 1905.
- **Tacoma Lodge of Freemasons** — Grand Lodge of Washington headquartered at 4970 Bridgeport Way W, University Place. Historic Masonic Temple in downtown Tacoma. Historically the venue where business, legal, and political elites formed private networks.
- **University of Puget Sound** — Private liberal arts college. President Isiaah Crawford. The institution where the Tacoma elite are educated and where board interlocks concentrate.

**Major Developers (Land Use Power):**
- **Southport Real Estate** — $116,800 in lobbying expenditures (2024). Major Tideflats/waterfront development interests.
- **Rush Development Company** — $2,400 donor to Kelly Chambers. Gig Harbor-based.

## Dimension 2: CRIMINAL POWER — Organized Crime, Gangs, and Corruption

### What We Have
- Named Sheriff Swank and Prosecutor Robnett as formal legal actors
- Mapped law enforcement budgets ($406M combined)
- Noted Sheriff Swank's conflict with County Executive

### What's Missing

**Historical Organized Crime (The "Enterprise"):**
- **The 1978 FBI RICO Indictment** — This is the most significant organized crime prosecution in Pierce County history, yet completely absent from our analysis:
  - 15 men indicted by federal grand jury (Dec 8, 1978)
  - **Pierce County Sheriff George V. Janovich** was among those charged — the sitting sheriff was part of a racketeering conspiracy
  - Led by Tacoma mobster **John Joseph Carbone** (1919–1998) and his son **Joseph M. Carbone**
  - The "Enterprise" used assault, arson, extortion, bribery, and attempted murder to control Pierce County's topless-dancing tavern business
  - Also engaged in insurance fraud, gambling, and prostitution
  - Sheriff Janovich solicited bribes to allow prostitution and gambling in the county
  - RICO conviction: Janovich sentenced to 12 years in federal prison
  - 150+ witnesses testified; 77 tape recordings entered as evidence
  - Source: HistoryLink.org File #9728; FBI records; U.S. District Court, WDWA

**Contemporary Gang Influence:**
- **Knoccout Crips** — Federal indictment (Aug 2025, 15 defendants). Fentanyl trafficking conspiracy using female couriers at Sea-Tac Airport. Doused bags in perfume to mask drug odors. Multi-state operation covering WA, OR, CA. OCDETF operation.
- **Black Gangster Disciples** — Federal wiretap investigation (Feb 2026, 14 arrested). Counterfeit oxycodone, Xanax, methamphetamine, fentanyl, cocaine distribution. Lead defendant Kevin Salgado, 27, of Puyallup.
- **Hilltop Crips** — Documented street gang in Tacoma's Hilltop neighborhood. Confirmed in Pierce County Sheriff blotter. Involved in shootings and drug trafficking.
- **5 Pierce County homicides linked to Tacoma gang members** (News Tribune, 2025) — String of homicides prompted large-scale federal takedown.

**JBLM-to-Street Drug Pipeline:**
- NCIS and Army CID participated in the Feb 2026 gang raids — indicating military connection
- 18-month federal investigation found drugs flowing through Sea-Tac and JBLM region
- This dimension is a known national pattern (military personnel trafficking weapons/drugs) but unmapped in our analysis

**Public Corruption:**
- **Prosecutor Mark Lindquist** — Lost 2018 election to Mary Robnett (63-37%) after whistleblower complaints documented toxic work environment. External investigation commissioned.
- **Pierce County Court Leader Resignation** (2026) — "Failed to act" investigations preceded resignation (News Tribune, July 2026)
- **Judge Grant L. Anderson** — CJC finding: received undisclosed payments from estate assets for which he was fiduciary, failed to report on disclosure filings
- **Judge Mark S. Deming** (District Court) — CJC public hearing: improper relationship with probation department employee
- **Judge A. Eugene Hammermaster** — CJC finding: patterns of disrespectful conduct, violations of procedural rights
- **Tacoma Bank Failure Indictment** (2011) — Four indicted for conspiracy resulting in failure of Tacoma bank
