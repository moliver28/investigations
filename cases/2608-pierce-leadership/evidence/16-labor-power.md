## 2. ILWU LOCAL 23 — PORT OF TACOMA DOCKWORKERS

### Overview
- **Union:** International Longshore and Warehouse Union (ILWU)
- **Local:** 23
- **Location:** Fife / Port of Tacoma, WA

### Strike / Labor Action History
1. **1908 strike:** Employers refused to recognize Longshoremen's Protective Club; Local 23 joined Seattle locals; led to creation of Longshoremen's Union of the Pacific
2. **1916 strike:** Tacoma longshoremen struck and lost; lost control of dispatching
3. **1971 strike (134 days):** ILWU nationwide strike over containerization; 96.4% strike vote; longest coastwise longshore strike in U.S. history
4. **2002 employer lockout:** PMA locked out West Coast workers; ILWU Local 23 President Roger Boesflug negotiated "richest contract ever" — 58% pension increase, $3/hr wage increase; Local 23 voted 521-20 to approve
5. **2024 WWL Wallenius strike (multiple):** Feb 7, 2024 — mass strike at Vehicle Processing Center coinciding with ship offload; Aug 2, 2024 — 1-day ULP strike over illegal firing; Dec 2024 — 4-day ULP strike over firing of bargaining committee member
6. **2025 Wallenius contract victory:** Historic first contract covering 150 workers at WWL Tacoma VPC; ILWU "heat & hammer" model; longshore, truck drivers, and railroad workers honored picket lines

### Political Power
- Endorsed Nick Brown for WA Attorney General (2024)
- ILWU international involvement in supply chain organizing (SCLT program)
- Wallenius contract won with international solidarity from MUA (Australia), Zenkowan (Japan), ILA (East Coast)
