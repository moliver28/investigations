## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Current power (Assessor-Treasurer, started January 1, 2025):**
 - **Property-tax valuation:** Sets the assessed value of all real and personal property in Pierce County. (The county has a very high parcel count.) State law RCW 84.40 covers assessments. RCW 84.69 covers senior and disabled exemptions.
 - **Property-tax billing and collection:** Sends out tax bills. Collects more than $2 billion a year in property taxes. That money funds Pierce County government, cities, fire districts, school districts, ports, and other special districts.
 - **Treasurer duties (combined office):** Holds and invests county funds. Processes warrants. Warrants handles bank relationships and debt service. RCW 36.29 covers the treasurer role.
 - **Levy administration:** Certifies levy amounts for taxing districts. This step is key to any successful ballot measure by a school district, fire district, port, or similar.
- **Past power (Pierce County Council District 5, 2021 to December 31, 2024):**
 - **Council Vice Chair:** Ran the meeting when the Council Chair was absent. Could shape the agenda.
 - **Vote on the $3.5 billion biennial budget:** Approved spending bills, including the Assessor-Treasurer's own budget.
 - **2020-21 biennial budget:** Led the Council's work on that budget cycle, per an official Facebook post.
- **Past power (Tacoma City Council, 8 years):** Made municipal decisions on zoning, contracts, and budget inside Tacoma city limits.
- **How his roles cross over:** His government power (countywide tax and assessment work) crosses with economic power (the property-tax base funds all Pierce County taxing districts) and political power (levy certification shapes ballot-measure success). The Assessor-Treasurer is one of three Pierce County countywide-elected offices. The other two are the Auditor and the Prosecutor.
- **Key facts:**
 - **Office:** Pierce County Assessor-Treasurer. Required by state law.
 - **Budget:** Runs the Assessor-Treasurer's own department budget. The County appropriates it. He does not control how the collected funds are spent.
 - **Veto power:** None.
 - **Reach:** Pierce County. Tax collection flows downstream to hundreds of taxing districts.
