## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why included:** holds office. Marilyn Strickland is the U.S. Representative for WA-10. Her district covers parts of Pierce and Thurston Counties, including east Tacoma and Olympia. She is a federal officeholder. She has pull over federal money, infrastructure, and military policy. That policy hits Pierce County because JBLM (Joint Base Lewis-McChord, the major Army base on the Pierce-Thurston line) sits there. (Sources 1, 7)
- **Who was left out and why:** Denny Heck is her predecessor and now serves as Washington State Treasurer. Victoria Woodards is the current Tacoma Mayor. Strickland once held that mayoral seat. Both have their own profiles and are not the subject here.

## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Marilyn Strickland. Korean name: 김순자.
- **Date of birth / birthplace:** September 25, 1962, Seoul, South Korea. (Sources 1, 7)
- **Current home:** Tacoma, Washington. (Source 3)
- **Education:** Mount Tahoma High School, class of 1980. B.A. in Sociology from the University of Washington in 1984. M.B.A. from Clark Atlanta University in 1992. (Sources 1, 7)
- **Family / spouse:** Married. She lives in Tacoma with her husband. His name is not in the official bios. (Source 8)
- **Career before Congress:** Marketing executive at Starbucks. Helped launch Tacoma's Click! broadband (a city fiber network). Tacoma City Council from 2008 to 2009. Mayor of Tacoma from 2010 to 2017. President and CEO of the Seattle Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce from 2018 to January 31, 2020. (Sources 1, 5, 6)
- **Firsts:** First African-American to represent Washington State at the federal level. One of the first three Korean-American women in Congress. (Source 5)

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Elected office:** U.S. Representative for WA-10 since January 3, 2021. That covers the 117th through 119th Congress. She was re-elected in 2022 and 2024. (Sources 1, 7)
- **Committee seats:** House Transportation and Infrastructure, and House Armed Services, both for the 117th through 119th Congress. Her subcommittees: Coast Guard and Maritime Transportation. Highways and Transit. Military Personnel. Readiness. (Source 4)
- **Caucus leadership:** Secretary of the Congressional Black Caucus. Whip of the New Democrat Coalition. Chair of the New Democrat Coalition National Security Task Force. Executive Board Member of the Congressional Asian Pacific American Caucus. She is also co-chair of the Korea Caucus, the Global Investment in America Caucus, the Grid Innovation Caucus, the Foreign Trade Zone Caucus, the Friends of Indonesia Caucus, and the Congressional Caucus on Black Innovation. (Sources 4, 8)
- **Federal funding muscle:** She pulled about $120M for WA-10 from the IIJA, the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act. That covers broadband, clean water, transit, and roads. She won a 14.5% pay raise for junior enlisted service members through the Armed Services Committee. (Source 5)
- **Domains she spans:** federal government. Infrastructure and transportation. Defense — JBLM is in her district. Healthcare — she voted for the IRA, the Inflation Reduction Act. Business — she is the former Chamber CEO.

## 5. How they touch government
- **Campaign money:** For the 2025–2026 cycle, she raised $1.51M. That is $839K from individuals and $667K from PACs (political action committees). She had $677,498 cash on hand as of July 15, 2026. Her FEC (Federal Election Commission) committee is Strickland for Washington, ID C00732826. (Sources 2, 9)
- **Past ethics sanction:** In May 2010, the Tacoma Board of Ethics sanctioned Strickland. She took frequent flyer miles from a local businessman for an official trip to Asia. She accepted the sanction and paid back the miles. (Source 1)
- **Chamber opposition to head tax:** As Seattle Metro Chamber CEO, she opposed the Seattle head tax. That was a 2018 proposal to tax each employee to fund housing and homelessness services. (Source 1)
- **Federal money she brought home:** About $120M in IIJA earmarks for WA-10. She voted for the Inflation Reduction Act. (Source 5)
- **State campaign-finance agency (PDC):** No Washington State PDC filings found as a candidate. She is a federal officeholder only. UNVERIFIED for any state-level donations.

## 1. Who they are
- **Verdict:** Strickland controls federal dollars and federal policy that hit Pierce County. She sits on two powerful House committees: Transportation and Infrastructure, and Armed Services. Her district contains JBLM. That makes her one of the most important federal figures for military and infrastructure money in the South Sound. She rose from local government (Tacoma mayor) to business leadership (Seattle Chamber) to Congress. She built a centrist Democratic coalition along the way.
- **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** 2010 ethics sanction for taking frequent flyer miles. It was minor and resolved. Her former Chamber CEO role creates a possible perception of pro-business bias, but no current money conflict found. Her opposition to the Seattle head tax as Chamber CEO is on the public record.
- **Analysis of alternatives:** An opposing view would say Strickland's power is limited by House minority status. Democrats are in the minority in the 119th Congress. That limits her real legislative output. Her committee seats may bring less power than claimed, since the majority party sets the agenda. Her Chamber CEO role could be seen mainly as a lobbying and advocacy position rather than independent power.
- **Confidence:** High. The sources are official House bios, FEC filings, and congressional records, and her career path is well-documented.
