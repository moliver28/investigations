## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why he is here:** Uli Chi is the board chair of Virginia Mason Franciscan Health (VMFH). VMFH was formed in January 2021 when Virginia Mason merged with CHI Franciscan. It is part of CommonSpirit Health. It runs the main Catholic hospital chain in Pierce County and Western Washington. As board chair, he is the top lay governance officer for a system that runs St. Joseph Medical Center (Tacoma), St. Anthony (Gig Harbor), St. Clare (Lakewood), St. Elizabeth (Enumclaw), and St. Francis (Federal Way). He decides on mission, ethics, capital projects, and regional strategy.
- **Who is left out:** Ketul J. Patel runs CommonSpirit from Chicago — out of state. Julie Manas runs VMFH day-to-day as CommonSpirit Northwest Region President since 2024. She has her own profile or one coming under `vmfh`. Other VMFH board members are not on this map. Kevin McClatchy is not on VMFH; he runs Chatham AM.
