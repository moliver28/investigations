## §0 why this entity is on the map
Bates Technical College is on the map because it is one of two main technical colleges in Tacoma (alongside TCC). It feeds Pierce County's apprenticeship and skilled-trades pipeline. It also runs KBTC-TV PBS, which gives it a media footprint in the network.

## §1 who they are legally
**FACT** Bates Technical College is a public technical college in Tacoma, Washington. It was founded in 1940.
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bates_Technical_College (secondary; reliability 0.85)

**FACT** In 1940, a trade school program started in the basement of Hawthorne Elementary School. In the 1941–42 school year, it was named Tacoma Vocational School. In 1944, LaVerne Hazen Bates (L. H. Bates) became the school's director. In 1947, the school was renamed Tacoma Vocational-Technical Institute. After L. H. Bates retired in 1969, the Tacoma School Board renamed it the "L. H. Bates Vocational Technical Institute."
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bates_Technical_College (secondary; reliability 0.85).

**FACT** In 1991, Washington State pulled the vocational schools out of local school districts. They then came under the State Board for Community and Technical Colleges.
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bates_Technical_College (secondary; reliability 0.85).

**FACT** In 1992, the college took over KTPS-TV, a PBS station in Tacoma, from the Tacoma School District. It renamed the station KBTC-TV on October 12.
**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bates_Technical_College (secondary; reliability 0.85).

## §2 Governance
**FACT** A five-member Board of Trustees runs Bates Technical College. The Governor of Washington appoints the trustees. They set policy, hire the president, approve the budget, and award faculty tenure.
**Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/about-bates/college-governance (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** Board Chair Florence Chang took the role in August 2019. She is also President of MultiCare Health System. This makes her a direct board link between Bates and Pierce County's biggest healthcare system.
**Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/about-bates/college-governance (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Trustee Christina Blocker was appointed by Governor Jay Inslee in 2016. She is the first African American woman to serve on the Bates board. She is also President of the National African American Caucus of Community College Trustees.
**Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/about-bates/college-governance (primary; reliability 0.95).
