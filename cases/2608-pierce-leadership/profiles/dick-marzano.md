## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Dick Marzano is one of five elected Port of Tacoma commissioners. In 2026 he became commission president. That makes him co-chair of the Northwest Seaport Alliance (NWSA) Managing Members. This group controls marine cargo terminals across Puget Sound.
