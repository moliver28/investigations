## 3. Where their power comes from
- **How he got authority:** Appointed CEO of a Public Transportation Benefit Area (PTBA), a special kind of transit district set up under state law (RCW 36.57A). He is not elected. A nine-member Board of Commissioners hires him — eight elected officials and one non-voting labor union representative. He runs day-to-day operations, labor talks, contracts (Sound Transit express bus), and capital grants.
- **Budget and contracts:** $154 million a year operating budget (per the United Way bio; the $345 million graph figure likely includes multi-year capital spending). Pierce Transit operates Sound Transit's ST Express bus service between Pierce and King counties. That accounts for about 40% of its bus-operator jobs.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Office:** Pierce Transit CEO (appointed).
  - **Budget:** About $154 million operating; about 960 full-time employees.
  - **Veto / appointment power:** None. The Board hires and fires the CEO.
  - **Where he operates:** A 292-square-mile PTBA in Pierce County, plus Sound Transit contract work into King County.
