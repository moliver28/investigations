## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Weyerhaeuser Company (NYSE: WY)
- **Type:** Real estate investment trust (REIT). Publicly traded on the NYSE under the ticker WY. The company became a REIT in 2010. It paid shareholders a special $5.6 billion dividend as part of that change.
- **SEC CIK number:** 0000106535
- **Headquarters:** Seattle, WA (moved from Federal Way, WA)
- **Founded:** 1900, by Frederick Weyerhaeuser. The company started in a Tacoma field office. It began by buying 900,000 acres of Washington timberland from the Northern Pacific Railway.
- **Ownership:** Publicly traded. Held by large investors. No single shareholder controls the company.
- **Governing law:** Delaware General Corporation Law. Operates as a REIT under IRC §856 of the federal tax code.
