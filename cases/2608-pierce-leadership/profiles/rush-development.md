## 1. Who they are
- **Exact legal name:** Rush Development Company Inc (per Better Business Bureau and Washington State Department of Labor and Industries (L&I) records).
- **Entity type:** For-profit corporation. It is a general contractor and a real estate developer.
- **Parent/subsidiaries:** A division of The Rush Companies, a partnership of six companies with shared ownership. Sister companies are Rush Capital, Rush Commercial, Rush Properties, Edison47, and Rush Residential.
- **Ownership chain:** Owned by founder Gordon Rush (CEO/President) and Matt Smith (President/Manager). Larry Melsness is also an owner of Rush Properties, per a 2017 DJC news story.
- **Governing law:** Washington State corporate law. It is licensed as a general contractor under Washington L&I (UBI 603126275 for Rush Residential Inc).
- **Tax ID (EIN)/registration:** UBI 603126275 (Rush Residential Inc, WA L&I). Rush Development Company Inc is registered in Washington. Full EIN was UNVERIFIED in public sources.
- **Headquarters:** Gig Harbor, WA. The office is at the Park Plaza building. Rush built it in 2003 as a speculative project. Rush has used it as its HQ since then, with about 55 employees at that location.
- **Founded:** 1987. The first project was a speculative home in Tacoma's North End.
