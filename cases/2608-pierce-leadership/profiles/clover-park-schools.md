## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why it is on the map.** Clover Park School District (CPSD) is one of Pierce County's largest K-12 public school districts. It serves about 12,395 students across 33 schools. Its schools sit in Lakewood, Tillicum, Woodbrook, and parts of unincorporated Pierce County (a county not part of any city). It also borders Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM). The district runs on a $262 million budget for the 2024-25 school year (FY2024-25). It has the legal power to levy property taxes. It is a Purple Star Award District, which means it serves many military-connected students whose families move with JBLM's deployment cycle. It has won statewide awards for its board and its superintendent.
- **Who is left out.** Tacoma Public Schools, Bethel, Puyallup, University Place, Sumner-Bonney Lake, and Peninsula are separate districts and are mapped on their own pages. JBLM itself is mapped separately. The city of Lakewood is mapped on its own. Madigan Army Medical Center is part of JBLM and is mapped under military and defense.

## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Clover Park School District (CPSD).
- **Type:** A public K-12 school district under Washington State law. It is a government body, not a nonprofit. The IRS does not treat it as a 501(c)(3) (federal tax-exempt charity).
- **Governing law:** RCW 28A (Washington's Revised Code of Washington for common schools) and the Pierce County Charter.
- **EIN (tax ID number):** Not verified. School districts are government bodies and do not file IRS Form 990.
- **Location:** Head office in Lakewood, Washington. Schools spread across Lakewood, Tillicum, Woodbrook, parts of unincorporated Pierce County, and a small part of Tacoma near JBLM.
- **Schools:** 33 schools (2023-24).
- **Students:** 12,395 (2023-24).
- **How it is run:** A 5-member elected Board of Directors governs it. No single person owns it.
- **Subsidiaries:** None found.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Power held:**
  - **Legal power (RCW 28A):** CPSD enforces compulsory school attendance, sets curriculum, manages buildings, and bargains with employee unions.
  - **Tax power:** The Board can put property-tax levies on the ballot. Pierce County collects the money through the Assessor-Treasurer's office.
  - **Workforce:** About 1,100 staff (a third-party estimate from August 2026). That includes teachers, support staff, and 19 district leaders plus 54.5 school leaders (2023-24 NCES, the National Center for Education Statistics).
  - **Buildings budget:** $6.38 million in capital spending (FY2021-22). Bond measures need voter approval.
  - **Buying power:** CPSD signs contracts for buses, food, technology, curriculum, and building upkeep.
  - **Community role:** It partners with the Lakewood Multicultural Coalition and runs a youth council. It gives community leadership awards.
- **Where its power crosses over:** CPSD sits at the meeting point of schools, government, the local economy, and military-civilian life.
- **Key facts:**
  - **Tax status:** Government body, not a 501(c)(3).
  - **Money mix (FY2021-22 NCES):** Federal $49.588M (20%); State $161.046M (66%); Local $33.565M (14%). Total: $244.199M.
  - **Current money (FY2024-25):** Revenue $262,088,238. Expenses $274,886,723. The district spent more than it took in. The Board approved a $12.6 million cut for FY2026-27 to fix the gap.
  - **Board (2025-26):** Darrin Lowry (District 4, until 2029); Dana Kelleher (District 3, until 2029); Janet Caldwell (District 5, until 2027); Krissy Kim (District 1, until 2027); Bryan Thomas (District 2, until 2027).
  - **Government share:** About 86% of revenue came from federal and state sources combined (FY2021-22).
- **Money trouble:** A $12.6 million cut for FY2026-27 was approved in February 2026 to keep the district solvent.

## 1. Who they are
