## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Jani Hitchen is the Chair of the Pierce County Council (District 6). The Council is the law-making body for Pierce County, which has 921,130 people. She is on this map because she holds the top legislative office in the county. She also chairs the Tacoma-Pierce County Board of Health, which sets health policy for the county.
- People who are NOT on the map: Annette Swillie is a Council aide, not a member. Loujanna Rohrer lost the 2024 race to Hitchen, so she has no current office. Danny Hankins lost the primary.

## 1. Who they are
- **Name:** Jani G. Hitchen (per the state's campaign-finance agency, the PDC).
- **Born:** Grew up in Olympia, WA. Exact date of birth is UNVERIFIED.
- **Home:** Parkland, WA (part of unincorporated Pierce County). She has lived there for almost 35 years.
- **Family:** Her father died when she was young. Her mother raised her alone in Olympia. Her mother later sat on the Olympia School Board. Hitchen's spouse and children are not listed in public records. UNVERIFIED.
- **School:** Bachelor's in Education from Pacific Lutheran University (PLU), finished December 1996. Master's in Educational Technology from the University of Washington Tacoma (UW Tacoma).
- **Career:** She taught at Lochburn Middle School in Clover Park School District for 10 years. In 2008, she moved to Bethel School District. Over 14 years there she taught science at three schools. She ended her teaching career in January 2021 to take a Council seat.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Elected office:** She is the Councilmember for District 6. She was first elected in November 2020 and re-elected on November 5, 2024. District 6 covers about 100,000 people in Parkland, Steilacoom, Lakewood, DuPont, Anderson Island, Ketron Island, McNeil Island, and Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM).
- **Council Chair:** Her fellow Council members voted her Chair for 2025 and again for 2026. The Chair runs meetings and sets the agenda. It is the top elected job in Pierce County after the County Executive.
- **Committee roles:** She chairs the Rules & Operations Committee, the Health & Human Services Committee, and the outside Performance Audit Committee. She also sits on the Public Safety Committee and the Flood Control Zone District Board.
- **Board of Health:** She joined the Tacoma-Pierce County Board of Health as an alternate in 2021. She became a full member in 2023. She became Vice Chair in January 2023 and Chair in June 2024.
- **Party:** Democratic.
