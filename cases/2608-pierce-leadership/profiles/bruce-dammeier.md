## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Bruce Dammeier is on this map because he held the top elected job in Pierce County: Pierce County Executive from 2017 to 2024. The Executive runs the county's $1B+ budget, can veto Council laws, and hires the heads of about 30 county departments. He is a Republican. He left office in January 2025 after hitting his term limit. Democrat Ryan Mello took the seat.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Elected executive power (from his office):** As Pierce County Executive from 2017 to 2024, Dammeier could veto Council laws. He hired the heads of about 30 county departments. He signed contracts and deals with other governments. He used the veto in public. His most public veto was Ordinance 2024-540s on large tent encampments in August 2024.

- **Bridge role to nonprofits:** He sat on boards for community groups like Tacoma Goodwill and Mel Korum YMCA. As Executive, his seat on these boards linked county power to the local nonprofit world.
