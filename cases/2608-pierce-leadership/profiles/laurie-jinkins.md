## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Laurie A. Jinkins (source #2).
- **Date of birth / birthplace:** Born August 1964, Iowa (source #2).
- **Current residence:** Tacoma, WA (source #1).

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Elected office:** WA House of Representatives, District 27, Position 1. She took office January 10, 2011. Her current term ends January 11, 2027 (source #3).
- **Leadership role:** Speaker of the WA House of Representatives. She took that role on January 13, 2020. She was the first woman, the first lesbian, and the first Pierce County representative to hold the speakership since 1995 (source #1, #2, #7).
- **Power domains:**
 - Legislative agenda control: As Speaker, she decides the floor calendar, committee assignments, and bill referrals. She controls the flow of all legislation, including the state operating and capital budgets (source #5, #7).
 - Public health: As a senior advisor at the Tacoma-Pierce County Health Department, she connects legislative health-care policy with county-level public health work (source #1).
 - Budget and appropriations: She previously served on House Appropriations and Capital Budget committees (source #5).
 - Civil rights and judiciary: She previously chaired the Civil Rights and Judiciary Committee (source #1).
- **Key facts:**
 - Offices and authority: Speaker of the House (statewide legislative control) and State Representative for the 27th LD.
 - Budget control: As Speaker, she indirectly controls the chamber's version of the biennial state operating budget. It was about $70B for the 2023-25 biennium. She previously served on Appropriations and Capital Budget committees (source #5).
 - Powers: She appoints committee chairs and members, refers bills to committees, and controls the floor calendar.
 - Jurisdictions: Washington State (legislative), 27th LD (electoral), Pierce County (residential and professional).
