## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Adam Smith is on this map for two reasons: he holds office, and he makes big decisions. He has been the U.S. Representative for Washington's 9th Congressional District since 1997. That is 15 terms in office.
- As Ranking Member (and former Chair) of the House Armed Services Committee, he shapes national defense policy and Pentagon spending. This affects Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM), the largest military base in Pierce County and one of the largest on the West Coast.
- His district covers parts of Tacoma, SeaTac, Tukwila, and other South King/North Pierce County communities. As Dean of the Washington House delegation, he carries extra weight.
- People near him who are not on the map: Washington's two U.S. Senators (Patty Murray, Maria Cantwell) have their own nodes. Other WA House members are not on the map. Smith's wife Sara Bickle-Eldridge (a UW/Seattle University Law graduate) is not on the map unless she holds her own power.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Primary power basis:** Elected federal office. U.S. Representative, WA-9, since 1997 (15 terms). As the longest-serving WA House member (Dean of delegation), he has deep seniority and know-how.
- **Committee power:** Ranking Member of the House Armed Services Committee (HASC). That makes him the top Democrat on the committee that handles military policy, the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA), and oversight of the Department of Defense. This is one of the most powerful committee spots in Congress. He was Chair during the 116th and 117th Congresses (2019–2023) when Democrats held the majority. He has served on HASC since 1997.
- **Defense and foreign policy reach:** Former chair of the Terrorism, Unconventional Threats and Capabilities subcommittee. Former chair of the Air and Land Forces subcommittee. Previously served on the House Foreign Affairs Committee and the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence. Founded the bipartisan Congressional Caucus for Effective Foreign Assistance (CCEFA). Has traveled to the Middle East, North Africa, and other conflict zones.
- **Community Project Funding:** Smith secured $6,316,000 in community project funds for WA-09 in a House-passed package (Jan 2026). He also secured $24.6M for Puget Sound military projects in an earlier spending package. As Ranking Member of HASC, he has "traditionally been restrained in making requests of the Defense Appropriations Subcommittee."
- **District covered:** WA-9 includes parts of Tacoma, SeaTac, Tukwila, Renton, Kent, Bellevue, and other South King/North Pierce County communities. The district covers SeaTac Airport and parts of the Puget Sound industrial corridor.
- **Domain overlaps:** Government (congressman, HASC ranking member), Defense/Military (armed services policy, JBLM advocacy), Economic (transportation, trade, tech), Foreign Policy (national security, foreign aid reform).
