## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Bill Robertson is on this map because he runs MultiCare Health System. MultiCare is the largest private employer in Pierce County. It is also the largest not-for-profit, locally owned health system in Washington State. He has been President and CEO since May 1, 2014.
- Under his leadership, MultiCare grew from a Tacoma-focused hospital group into the largest community-owned nonprofit health system in the region.
- He also chaired the Washington State Hospital Association (WSHA) board from 2020 to 2021. WSHA lobbies in Olympia on behalf of member hospitals.
- He plans to retire on December 31, 2026. Florence Chang will take over as President and CEO on January 1, 2027.
