## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why she's on the map:** Maria Cantwell is the US Senator for Washington State. Pierce County is part of her state. She is the Ranking Member of the Senate Commerce, Science, and Transportation Committee. "Ranking Member" is the top minority-party member on a Senate committee — the lead Democrat when Republicans chair the panel. Her role gives her decision-making power over federal policy on transportation, technology, aviation, maritime, and telecom. All of these touch Pierce County directly: Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM), the Port of Tacoma, and freight rail. Her position lets her direct federal spending (earmarks) to Washington projects.
- **Other people nearby:** Rep. Derek Kilmer (D-6, former) and Rep. Emily Randall (D-6, current) cover the Pierce County district at the federal level. Governor Bob Ferguson is a separate statewide officeholder.
