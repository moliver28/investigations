## 5. How they touch government
- **Cedar Flats project (Pierce County):** Pierce County put $7 million from the Maureen Howard Affordable Housing Fund into Cedar Flats. It is a 276-unit affordable housing complex in the South Hill / Frederickson area. The project serves households earning less than 60% of Area Median Income (AMI) — the local middle income set by the federal government. The max income is $72,480 for a family of four. It includes larger units for families and reserves a portion for people with disabilities. The County Council approved $12.2 million for four housing projects (including Cedar Flats) at its September 2, 2025 meeting. The total Cedar Flats cost is nearly $109 million.
