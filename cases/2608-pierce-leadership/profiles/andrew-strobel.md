- **Why he is on the map:** Andrew Strobel is a key connector in Pierce County. He works in three places at once. He is the Tribal and Regional Affairs Advisor to the County Executive (Ryan Mello). ## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Appointed job:** Strategic Advisor of Tribal and Regional Affairs in County Executive Ryan Mello's office. This is an at-will job. That means he serves at the pleasure of the Executive. He advises Mello on regional policy and transit. He also acts as the main contact between the county and tribal governments. He works with the Puget Sound Regional Council (PSRC), the Pierce County Regional Council, and Sound Transit.
- **Picked by the governor:** Washington Governor Bob Ferguson chose Strobel for the TCC Board of Trustees. His five-year term started January 28, 2026. He took the seat that Pat Shuman used to hold.
- **Old job:** Planning and Land Use Director at the Puyallup Tribe, from April 2010 to January 15, 2025. That is almost 15 years. He moved up from Associate Planner to Director. He helped pass the tribe's full land use plan. He led the Tacoma Tideflats Subarea Plan, a seven-year project. He helped open the Emerald Queen Casino in 2020. He worked on road safety on tribal land.
- **Volunteer and civic jobs:** Vice-Chair of the Tacoma Planning Commission until at least June 2023. Chair of the Transportation Committee of the Central Council of Tlingit and Haida (term 2026–2028). He sat on the PSRC Growth Management Policy Board for the Puyallup Tribe (July 2024). He sat on the PSRC Transportation Policy Board for the Puyallup Tribe (December 2024).
- **How his roles overlap:**
 - **Government and tribal:** Main bridge between Pierce County and the Medicine Creek Treaty Tribes (Puyallup, Nisqually, Squaxin Island, Muckleshoot).
 - **Institutional and government:** TCC Board (education) plus Pierce County Executive staff (government).
 - **Government and transit:** Advises Mello on transit and works with Sound Transit and PSRC.
