## §0 why this entity is on the map
Museum of Glass is on this map because it is the lasting project of the Russell family. George F. Russell Jr. (1932–2025) co-founded and bankrolled the museum. He built it as an anchor for the Thea Foss Waterway redevelopment. The museum is now a flagship of Tacoma's "Museum Row" cultural cluster.

Its 501(c)(3) tax status, its annual revenue of about $5 million, its capital expansion project, and its broad board ties make it a network node. It links Tacoma arts donors to waterfront redevelopment.

## §2 Power basis
**FACT** The museum runs a working Hot Shop. It is one of the largest and most active museum glass studios on the West Coast. That gives it cultural power. The museum curates national traveling shows, runs artist residencies, and shapes Tacoma's identity as a glass-arts city. Tacoma is the birthplace of Dale Chihuly.
**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/company/museum-of-glass (secondary; reliability 0.85); https://museumofglass.org/board (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** The museum receives Tacoma Creates funding. Tacoma Creates is a voter-approved city fund for arts, culture, and science. The museum also runs a 2026 capital campaign for expansion. Its mix of donations and program-service revenue shows blended public and private support.
**Source:** https://www.museumofglass.org (primary; reliability 0.9); https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/911669422 (primary; reliability 0.9).

**FACT** George F. Russell Jr. (1933–2025) co-founded the museum with Phil Phibbs. He co-chaired the founding Board of Trustees with Jane Russell. The museum's Hot Shop is named the "Jane Russell Hot Shop" in her memory after her early death.
**Source:** https://www.museumofglass.org/george-russell (primary; reliability 0.95).
