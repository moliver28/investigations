## §0 why this entity is on the map
Strozier is on this map because he is the elected President of the Tacoma Public Schools (TPS) Board of Directors. He holds Position 3 on the five-member board.

The board runs Tacoma Public Schools. TPS is the second-largest school district in Pierce County. About 28,272 students attended TPS during the 2024 school year.

Strozier worked for TPS from 2008 to 2017. Those nine years inside the district give him strong ties to TPS staff. He now also holds an elected board seat.

## §7 who they are connected to
Strozier is a recent insider turned board leader. His nine years as TPS staff (2008–2017) gave him strong ties inside the district. His current board role gives him standing outside it.
