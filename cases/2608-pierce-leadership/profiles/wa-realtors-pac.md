## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why it is on the map.** The Washington Realtors Political Action Committee holds power from the PAC role and from the decisions it makes. It is one of the top 10 PACs by spending in Washington State (the state's campaign-finance agency (PDC) data, source #1). It picks which candidates to fund and endorse. The graph also shows ties to the Chamber of Commerce and Pierce County Council. The PAC acts as a pipeline between the real estate industry and elected officials.
- **Who is left out:** The National Association of Realtors PAC (RPAC) is the federal-level parent PAC. It works at the congressional level, so it is not in this Pierce County-focused graph. Local REALTOR® associations (like Seattle-King County and Tacoma-Pierce County) run their own local PACs, but they are not listed here. The Washington REALTORS® trade association itself (the 501(c)(6) parent) is a related but separate group from the PAC.

## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Washington Realtors Political Action Committee.
- **Type:** A Political Action Committee (PAC) registered with the Washington State Public Disclosure Commission (PDC). A PAC is a group that raises and spends money to influence elections.
- **Parent organization:** Washington REALTORS® (a statewide not-for-profit trade group with about 20,000+ members, source #3).
- **National affiliation:** National Association of Realtors (NAR) / REALTORS® Political Action Committee (RPAC). The state PAC is part of the national RPAC network.

- **Phone:** (360) 943-3100 (parent org); (360) 628-8129 (PDC filing contact).

- **CEO of parent org:** Lori Adler (source #3).
- **Tax ID (EIN)/UBI:** UNVERIFIED — not directly available from PDC filings.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Power held:** The PAC holds power to make decisions by choosing which candidates to endorse and fund. It is one of the top 10 PACs by spending in Washington State, with about $1,005,504.24 in spending in the current PDC reporting cycle (source #1). Its parent group, Washington REALTORS®, has 20,000+ members statewide (source #3), giving the PAC money and name recognition.
- **Political domain overlap:** The PAC only works in politics. It endorses and funds candidates for state, local, and legislative offices. Its "REALTOR Party" idea is nonpartisan. The PAC backs candidates who defend REALTOR® policy goals, no matter the party (source #4).
- **Funded and endorsed:** Candidates for statewide, legislative, judicial, and local offices across Washington. Nationally, RPAC had a 97% win rate in 2024 federal elections, with money split 50/50 between Democrats and Republicans (source #5). State-level endorsements for Pierce County candidates are unverified here (would need a PDC contribution search by recipient).
- **PAC spending:** About $1,005,504.24 in the current PDC cycle (source #1). Nationally, RPAC raised $45,845,025 in 2024 including state and local funds (source #5).
- **Party machinery control:** None. The PAC is nonpartisan and does not control any party. It adds to party activity by funding pro-REALTOR candidates across party lines.
- **Candidate recruitment and training:** Washington REALTORS® runs RPAC Trustee-led candidate interviews and endorsements (source #6). No formal candidate training programs found.

## 1. Who they are
- **The bottom line.** The WA Realtors PAC runs a large statewide campaign-funding pipeline (about $1M+ per cycle). It moves real estate industry money to pro-REALTOR candidates across party lines. Its power is money and connections. It uses the 20,000+ member Washington REALTORS® base and the national RPAC system ($45.8M raised in 2024) to shape election outcomes. The nonpartisan strategy (50/50 D/R split nationally) gives it access no matter which party holds power. The Pierce County link runs through the "donated" edge to County Council (weight 4) and "aligned" with Chamber (weight 3). That puts the PAC in the middle as a funding bridge between the real estate industry and local government.
- **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** The PAC's nonpartisan funding strategy means it often backs candidates who later vote on real estate, land-use, housing, and property tax bills. This is a normal feature of PACs, not specific to WA Realtors PAC. No evidence of explicit vote-buying was found. Defense counter: PAC contributions are public and capped by law; candidates get money from many sources.
- **Analysis of alternatives:** The opposing reading: the PAC's about $1M in spending is modest compared to total Washington campaign spending (the top PAC, Washington Coalition for Consumer Choice, spent about $4.8M). The REALTOR® brand's political pull may be overstated. Under this view, the PAC is a mid-tier actor whose endorsements matter most in down-ballot local races where dollars have a bigger impact. But the national RPAC's 97% win rate and $45.8M total raise suggest the state PAC gets real infrastructure and volunteer support beyond its own spending.
- **Confidence:** Medium. PDC expenditure data is authoritative (reliability 0.95), and the parent organization's structure and RPAC principles are confirmed by primary sources. However, specific Pierce County recipient data, individual lobbyist registrations for Washington REALTORS®, and exact EIN/UBI numbers were not retrieved in this session. The $1M expenditure figure is from PDC's aggregate disclosure page and may span multiple election cycles.
