## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why we added Tucci & Sons:** They are one of the biggest heavy-road builders in Pierce County. They hold many state and federal road contracts. They also donate to political campaigns and sit in local business groups. So they help shape how Pierce County builds its roads (source 1, 4, 9).
- **Other companies we did not add:** Active Construction, Inc. also bids on road work. They are not on the map unless we find stronger proof of power.

## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Tucci & Sons LLC (also Tucci & Sons, Inc.). Their Washington state business license number is 278 021 315 (source 3).
- **Type:** LLC. A family-owned construction company.
- **Address:** 4224 Waller Road E, Tacoma, WA 98443 (source 1, 3).
- **Owners:** Michael J. Tucci started the firm in 1950. It is now in its third generation of family owners. Mike Tucci is president. Tim Tucci is secretary and treasurer (source 6). Tim Tucci is also the main contact at the Asphalt Pavement Association of Washington (source 9).
- **State rules:** They follow state contractor rules. The Washington Department of Labor and Industries oversees those rules under state law RCW 18.27.
- **Federal ID:** Their SAM.gov Unique Entity ID is K5N5ZMTJJEM8. Their CAGE code is 0HXL1. Their DUNS number is 003825122 (source 8).

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Market share:** They run two asphalt plants in Pierce County. Each plant makes 400 or more tons of asphalt per hour. The newer Lakewood plant alone makes 150,000 to 200,000 tons a year (source 5, 6). They also recycle 100,000 or more tons of old concrete and asphalt each year at the McChord plant (source 5).
- **Government contracts:** They win state contracts with the Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT). They were the lowest bidder on the SR 507/510/702 roundabout contract, called Contract XE3689, awarded March 3, 2026 (source 2). They hold federal contracts with the U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) and the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA). Their two main federal awards add up to about $47.1 million. That includes the Stevens Canyon Road repair job in Mount Rainier National Park, where their bid was $8.9 million and the total award was $38.8 million (source 7, 8). They also work as a subcontractor on 4 other federal awards with Manson Construction Co. (source 8). They bid on a City of Tacoma crosswalk job on McKinley Avenue (source 4).
- **Revenue:** About $15 million a year (source 10 — secondary, not yet confirmed by primary filings).
- **Jobs:** About 71 workers (source 10 — secondary). Their LinkedIn page lists 51–200 workers (source 11 — secondary).
- **Property:** Their main yard is at 4224 Waller Rd E, Tacoma. They also run asphalt plants in Lakewood and at McChord (source 5, 6).

## 5. How they touch government
- **WSDOT contracts:** They bid on and win state highway jobs. Their most recent win is the SR 510/SR 702/SR 507 Crab Bundle 1A roundabout contract, awarded March 3, 2026 (source 2).
- **Federal DOT/FHWA:** They hold 2 main federal awards worth about $47.1 million. They also work as a subcontractor on 4 other awards (source 7, 8). One of those contracts led to a legal fight, case CBCA 4779. They asked for an extra $81,320.45 because the site conditions were different. The Civilian Board of Contract Appeals denied the claim (source 7).
- **City of Tacoma:** They bid on the McKinley Avenue Crosswalk Improvements job, contract PW20-0252F (source 4).
- **WA Labor & Industries:** They hold an active contractor license (source 3).

- **Campaign donations:** They gave money to Kelly Chambers, a political candidate. The state Public Disclosure Commission (PDC) filing for this donation has not been checked in this research pass.
- **Union work:** They highlight their union workforce and apprenticeship programs (source 1).

## 1. Who they are
- **Verdict:** Tucci & Sons has real market power in Pierce County. They are one of the largest heavy-road builders and asphalt makers here. Their power comes from three generations of family ownership, two high-capacity asphalt plants, and a steady flow of state and federal contracts. Their two main federal awards alone total about $47.1 million. Their ties to county land-use decisions and the Chamber of Commerce give them influence in the local development coalition. Their donation to Kelly Chambers shows they engage in politics. The full scope of their political donations still needs PDC verification.
- **Conflicts of interest:** They are both a major WSDOT contractor and a political donor. That puts them at the meeting point of road spending and political influence. The CBCA 4779 case shows they will sue over federal contract disputes. Their McChord plant is on or near Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM), a military base. That raises questions about land use near a military site. No evidence of bid-rigging or corrupt deals was found.
- **The other view:** Tucci & Sons may simply be a strong local contractor that wins bids by offering low prices and good work. The WSDOT bid check report shows they were the lowest bidder among several competitors. Active Construction, Inc. was the second-lowest bidder. Their political donations may be normal civic engagement, not influence-buying. Their multi-generational roots and union workforce suggest they are part of the community, not extracting from it.
- **Confidence:** Medium — primary sources confirm their contracts and market position. But revenue and employee numbers come from secondary websites. The full scope of their political donations was not checked in this pass.
