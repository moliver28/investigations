## 1. Who they are
- **Name:** The News Tribune. It is published by The McClatchy Company.
- **Type of business:** A privately held newspaper. McClatchy is a Delaware corporation.
- **Who owns it:** The News Tribune → McClatchy Media Company → Chatham Asset Management (a hedge fund in New Jersey). McClatchy went bankrupt on Feb 13, 2020. Chatham bought the company in a court-approved sale that closed Sep 4, 2020, for $312 million.
- **Old owners:** McClatchy bought the News Tribune from the Baker family in 1986 for about $112 million. The Bakers had owned it since 1912.
- **Tax status:** A private company. It pays taxes. It is not a nonprofit.
- **Where it is:** 1950 South State Street, Tacoma, WA 98405.
- **When it started:** 1880 (as The Tacoma Ledger). The current name came from a 1918 merger.
