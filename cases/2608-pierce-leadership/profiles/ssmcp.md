## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why it is here:** South Sound Military and Communities Partnership is a formal intergovernmental group. It works between Joint Base Lewis-McChord and 50+ nearby cities, counties, tribes, and groups. JBLM is the largest employer in Pierce County. The Elected Officials Council brings together top leaders from the county, the city of Lakewood, and JBLM command. It is a clear exercise of cross-jurisdictional power.
- **Who is left out:** The Office of Local Defense Community Cooperation is a federal funder of SSMCP. It is not a local power node. JBLM has its own profile. Member cities like Lakewood, Lacey, and DuPont may get their own profiles too.

## 1. Who they are
- **Exact legal name:** South Sound Military & Communities Partnership (SSMCP).
- **Entity type:** Intergovernmental partnership — not a separate legal body. The memorandum of agreement (MOA) says SSMCP is a regional partnership. It can fund staff but does not directly employ anyone. The City of Lakewood acts as the fiduciary agent. It provides legal advice and administrative services.
- **Formed:** 2011, by memorandum of agreement. The current MOA is dated 2018.
- **Governing law:** The MOA among member jurisdictions. The Elected Officials Council (EOC) follows Washington's Open Public Meetings Act (Revised Code of Washington (RCW) 42.30).
- **Staff:** Two full-time paid staff with military backgrounds, funded by SSMCP. Program Manager: Maria Tobin (253-983-7804, mtobin@cityoflakewood.us). Past Program Director: Bill Adamson (retired Colonel).

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **What power does SSMCP hold?** SSMCP works on land use, housing, transport, childcare, and economic development across Pierce and Thurston Counties. It has no legal authority. It shapes policy through the MOA, the Elected Officials Council, working groups, and studies. Its influence grows with JBLM's huge economic footprint.
- **Budget it controls:** SSMCP is funded by member dues and grants. Grants come from OLDCC. OLDCC is the federal Office of Local Defense Community Cooperation. That office used to be called the Office of Economic Adjustment. Exact budget numbers are not verified.
- **Appointment or veto powers:** None. The EOC makes policy suggestions and coordinates legislative strategies.
- **Area covered:** Pierce and Thurston Counties. This includes Lakewood, Lacey, Tacoma, DuPont, University Place, Yelm, Olympia, and nearby areas.

## 5. How they touch government
- **Pierce County:** Executive Leadership member. The Pierce County Executive serves as an EOC co-chair. Ryan Mello is the current Pierce County Executive. He sits on the EOC.
- **City of Lakewood:** Fiduciary agent and Executive Leadership member. It provides legal and administrative services. SSMCP staff are Lakewood city employees.
- **JBLM:** Ex officio Executive Leadership member. JBLM commanders take part in EOC meetings. These commanders include Col. Phil Lamb, Col. Dave Fazenbaker, and Lt. Gen. Xavier Brunson.
- **Office of Local Defense Community Cooperation:** Federal grant funder. Known as OLDCC.
- **Legislative advocacy:** SSMCP runs a Legislative Agenda. It pushes for state and federal funding for JBLM-community projects. Key efforts include the Defense Community Compatibility Account. They also include the North Clear Zone. They include the I-5 Nisqually River Delta Project.
- **Requests for Proposals:** SSMCP puts out RFPs for professional services. In 2025 it asked for bids on the JBLM Growth Implementation II plan. That plan covers three things: a Transportation Corridor Readiness Study, a Communications Interoperability Plan, and an Energy Grid Readiness Plan.

## 1. Who they are
- Verdict: SSMCP is the main coordinating body between JBLM and 50+ nearby civilian jurisdictions. JBLM is Pierce County's largest employer. Its statewide impact is $9 billion. SSMCP's power comes from its structure, not from law. The Elected Officials Council meets twice a year. It is the region's formal forum for military-community policy coordination. It is co-chaired by the Pierce County Executive, the Lakewood Mayor, and the Thurston County Commission Chair. SSMCP shapes regional land-use strategy. It also shapes infrastructure planning, housing policy, and legislative advocacy. Lakewood is the fiduciary agent. That means SSMCP staff are effectively Lakewood employees. Amazon's sponsorship of EOC events shows corporate interest in SSMCP's work.
- Conflicts-of-interest flags: SSMCP has a dual mission. It supports both JBLM military readiness and community interests. These can clash. Military land-use needs like the North Clear Zone and noise zones may conflict with civilian development. The EOC's one-vote-per-government structure may favor larger places. Corporate sponsorship by Amazon of EOC events raises questions about influence. SSMCP-commissioned economic impact studies may inflate JBLM's perceived importance.
- Analysis of alternatives : The opposing reading is that SSMCP is a voluntary coordination body. It has no binding authority. It cannot force member jurisdictions to adopt any policy. Its suggestions are advisory. Members keep local control. The "$9 billion economic impact" figure comes from an economic model. That model, called input-output analysis, may overstate JBLM's real marginal impact. SSMCP's actual policy outputs beyond studies and forums may be modest.
- **Confidence:** High — SSMCP's structure, membership, economic impact data, and how they touch government are extensively documented in primary sources including its own MOA, official website, U.S. Army publications, and Pierce County government records. The organization's coordinating role is well-established and verifiable.
