## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Alice Phillips is on this map because she runs labor in Pierce County. She is the elected President of the Pierce County Central Labor Council, AFL-CIO (PCCLC). That council is a federation of about 114 unions. Those unions cover about 44,000 workers.
- As President, she runs the union's endorsement process. Her union's political action committee (COPE) picks which Democrats to back. That endorsement matters a lot in close primaries.
- She is also a Labor Trustee on the Clover Park Technical College Board. The governor gave her that seat in 2021.
- **Adjacent but EXCLUDED:** Nathe Lawver (PCCLC Secretary-Treasurer). Vance Lelli (former PCCLC President). Adam Brinkman (current IBEW 483 Business Manager). All are nearby but not the same person on this map.

## 1. Who they are
- **Legal name:** Alice Phillips.
- **Pronouns:** she/her (per PCCLC bio).
- **Education:** Clover Park High School. Clover Park Technical College, 1979.
- **Military:** US Navy. She rose to Hull Technician First Class. She won the Navy Medal of Achievement.
- **Family / residence:** South Sound resident. Her spouse's name is not in public records.
- **Career path:** US Navy → City of Tacoma electrical worker (10 years) → IBEW Local 483 (the local electricians' union). She moved up: shop steward, then Executive Board, then Vice President, then President, then Organizer, then Union Rep, then Assistant Business Manager, then Business Manager for 30 years. Now she is PCCLC President. She was the first woman in that role.
- **Sanctions / criminal / civil:** None found.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Domain = political.** Her power comes from running the biggest labor group in the county. That group gives endorsements in every election. The union's political action committee (COPE) endorsement is a strong signal in Democratic primaries.
- **Statutory:** None. This is an elected union role, not a public office.
- **Influence mechanisms:**
  - COPE endorsements: Phillips is the public face of PCCLC's vetting process. That process has 40+ hours of candidate questionnaires and interviews. In 2024, PCCLC backed Ryan Mello for County Executive, Marty Campbell for Auditor, Davida Haygood for Council District 2, and several Superior Court picks.
  - **Clover Park Technical College Board of Trustees** (Labor Trustee, appointed by the governor). This seat ties labor to workforce training.
  - Public advocacy: Phillips pushed the 2026 unanimous endorsement of Pierce Transit's sales-tax hike from 0.6% to 0.9%. She has spoken at labor rallies.
- **key facts answers:**
  - Who is funded and endorsed: about 44,000 workers' dues, through 114 affiliates, pay for PCCLC's political work.
  - PAC spending: PCCLC has its own COPE. The exact dollar amounts were not in public records.
  - Party-machinery control: She is the de facto gatekeeper for Democratic primary endorsements in Pierce County.
  - Candidate recruitment: Through the IBEW pipeline and PCCLC delegates.
