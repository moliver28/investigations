## 1. Who they are
- **Exact legal name:** Columbia Banking System, Inc. (the holding company). Columbia Bank was the bank before the merger. Umpqua Bank is the bank brand after March 2023.
- **Type of business:** A Washington-state bank holding company. Its bank is insured by the FDIC (Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation). It is registered with the the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), ticker COLB. The holding company is a Washington corporation. The bank was a Washington state-chartered bank.
- **History:** Founded in Longview WA in 1973. Headquarters moved to Tacoma (1301 "A" Street) in 1993. In October 2021, Columbia announced a merger of equals with Portland-based Umpqua Holdings. The deal closed Feb 28, 2023. Columbia was the named survivor, but Umpqua was the accounting acquirer. The combined bank took the Umpqua Bank brand. The holding company kept the name Columbia Banking System, Inc. and kept Tacoma as the holding-company home.
- **Leadership:** Clint Stein is President, CEO, and Chairman (since the 2023 merger). Christopher Merrywell and Torran Nixon are Co-Presidents. Ivan Seda is the CFO. William Weyerhaeuser was the former Chairman. He stepped down after the merger.
- **Locations:** 376 branches (2026) across 8 states. The bank's headquarters moved to Portland, OR in 2023. The holding company's headquarters is still in Tacoma, WA.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Market power:** Columbia Banking System is the holding company for a top-30 U.S. bank after the Umpqua merger. It has about 376 branches. It controls business lending, deposits, and treasury services across the Pacific Northwest and Mountain West.
- **Civic ties:** William Weyerhaeuser's chairmanship connected Columbia's lending to one of America's oldest timber families (Weyerhaeuser Co.). Weyerhaeuser also served on the Greater Tacoma Community Foundation board. This is the main way Columbia enters the power map.
- **Establishment ties:** As a Tacoma Chamber member and long-time Tacoma bank, Columbia is tied to the local establishment: GTCF, the Chamber, Weyerhaeuser Co., and MultiCare's supply-chain financing.
- **Why it scores:** GTCF board tie (weight 2), Weyerhaeuser chairman tie (weight 3), Chamber of Commerce membership (weight 3).
