- **Verdict:** Kelly Chambers' power has shifted. Her Republican caucus leadership roles (Vice Chair, Assistant Whip, Ranking Member of Appropriations) gave her institutional influence within the minority party. Her loss in the 2024 Pierce County Executive race (by 2.7%) showed she could compete countywide but could not win the top executive office. Her SBA appointment is a jump to federal administrative power. She now controls SBA program delivery across a 4-state region. That may be more powerful in practice than her old legislative role. Her donor base is heavy on business (PACs, developers, auto dealers, construction). That matches her pro-business record and her small-business-owner identity.

Her own businesses (Visiting Angels franchise and Lomcevak Cellars winery) are small businesses. They could in theory use SBA programs. That is a possible conflict. Federal ethics rules usually require her to step aside from matters involving her own businesses. Her wide network of business donors from her legislative career (10 or more business donors in the graph) could also raise appearance-of-conflict issues in her SBA role. This is especially true if those donors later seek SBA help. No actual conflict has been identified.
