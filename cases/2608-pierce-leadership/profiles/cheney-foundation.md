## §0 why this entity is on the map
The Cheney Foundation is one of the largest private family foundations in Pierce County. It gives grants across youth, education, community development, and human services. These areas overlap with many public agencies and nonprofits in the network.

## §1 who they are legally
**FACT** The Ben B. Cheney Foundation Inc. was incorporated in 1955 in Washington. It is a family foundation.
**Source:** https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile?key=CHEN001 (primary; reliability 0.85)

**FACT** Foundation EIN: 91-6053760. Location: 3110 Ruston Way Ste A, Tacoma, WA 98402-5308.
**Source:** https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile?key=CHEN001 (primary; reliability 0.85).

**FACT** Ben B. Cheney founded the Cheney Lumber Company in 1936. He worked in the lumber industry his whole life. After he died in 1971, his estate set up the Foundation. It began giving grants in 1975.
**Source:** https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile?key=CHEN001 (primary; reliability 0.85).
