## §0 why this entity is on the map

## §1 Identity
**FACT** Dr. Lin Zhou, Ph.D., became president of Bates Technical College in 2018. She is the first female president in Bates history. She is also the first Chinese immigrant to lead a U.S. community college.
**Source:** https://www.aacc.nche.edu/about-us/governance/2023-board-slate-profiles/lin-zhou (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** Bates hired Zhou in 2013 as the dean of continuing education, apprenticeship, and child studies. She then moved up. She served as dean of instruction, executive dean, and vice president of student success (2016) before her 2018 appointment.
**Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/bates-president-lin-zhou-wins-chief-executive-officer-award (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** In April 2019, the College Spark Washington Board of Trustees appointed Zhou to a 14-member board. It brings together business, industry, and education leaders.
**Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/bates-technical-colleges-president-lin-zhou-joins-college-spark-board (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Governor Jay Inslee appointed Zhou to the Adult Education Advisory Council.
**Source:** https://www.batestech.edu/governor-jay-inslee-appoints-bates-technical-college-president-lin-zhou-to-adult-education-advisory-council (primary; reliability 0.95).
