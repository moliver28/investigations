## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Sheila Edwards Lange is the top leader (Chancellor) of the University of Washington Tacoma (UW Tacoma). UW Tacoma is one of three UW campuses. It is the only one in Pierce County. Lange has been Chancellor since September 16, 2021.
- Lange holds power because she runs the campus. She makes decisions on which academic programs open or close. She hires senior faculty. She leads the campus master plan (the long-range plan for buildings and land). She runs fundraising and major programs, like the Dressel Scholars program.
- UW Tacoma is a key player in the South Sound economy. It trains local workers. Lange sits on the board of the Tacoma-Pierce County Economic Development Board (EDB). That puts her inside the county's economic decision-making.
- **Adjacent but not on the map:**

## 1. Who they are
- **Verdict:** Lange is the top leader of UW Tacoma — the only public-research-university campus in Pierce County. Her power comes from the office she holds, not from any law. She sets academic priorities. She hires senior faculty and leaders. She leads the Campus Master Plan. She runs the Dressel Scholars program and a strong Pell-Grant advocacy role with U.S. Senator Patty Murray. She is the most well-connected chancellor in UW Tacoma history. She sits on the boards of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco (Seattle Branch), Pugh Capital Management, AAA Washington, Seattle Art Museum, Washington Campus Compact, and the Tacoma-Pierce EDB. The Puyallup Tribe holds the formal "Tribal Sponsor" title at UW Tacoma. Lange is the steward of that tie. That gives her a unique link between the Tribe and the university.
- **Possible conflicts of interest:**
 - Her seat on the Federal Reserve Bank board (Seattle Branch) is unpaid but well-recognized. It can look like the banking sector has a hand in higher-ed leadership.
 - Her seat on Pugh Capital Management's board — a private asset-management firm — can look like the finance sector is too close.
 - Her past national presidency of WEPAN and her say over vendors and curricula could be read as advocacy capture.
 - Defense counter-argument: a public-university chancellor is not a "Pierce leader" in the political sense. Adding her to the map may dilute the boundary.
- **Other ways to read this:** A skeptic would say Lange's decision-making power is heavily limited by UW Regents, UW-system rules, and the state legislature's budget choices. The opposing reading (favored here) is that even with those limits, she controls a campus master plan, an EDB board seat, a Federal Reserve seat, and a Pell-Grant lobbying channel. That kind of network rivals many elected officials in Pierce County.
- **Confidence:** High — identity, career path, boards, salary, and tribal-sponsor relationship verified across primary UW and News Tribune sources; 2021 appointment date verified by UW News release.
