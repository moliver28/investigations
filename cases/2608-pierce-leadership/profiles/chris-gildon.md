## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Chris Gildon is on the map because he holds office and makes decisions. He is the sitting State Senator for the 25th Legislative District. That district covers Puyallup, South Hill, Summit, and Waller in Pierce County. As Senate Republican lead on the Ways and Means Committee (the operating budget committee), he plays a big role in shaping the state's operating budget. That budget directly affects Pierce County funding. He also co-chairs the bipartisan Pierce County legislative delegation. That delegation includes members from both parties and both chambers.
- **Adjacent figures not profiled here.** Cyndy Jacobsen (R-25) is the current House member who succeeded Gildon. She is not at this tier. Joyce McDonald (R-25) was the House member Gildon succeeded. She is a former officeholder.

## 1. Who they are
- **Verdict:** Gildon's power comes from his role as Senate Republican Operating Budget Leader. It is the top budget job for the minority party in the Washington Senate. The Republican caucus is in the minority, with 19 of 49 seats. Even so, his budget role, his co-chair role on the Pierce County delegation, and his many board seats (WSIPP, Economic and Revenue Forecast Council, Legislative Ethics Board) give him real influence over state spending that affects Pierce County. His military record and real-estate work add weight on veterans' affairs and housing policy.
- **Conflict flags:** As a commercial real-estate broker on the Senate Housing Committee, his private work could overlap with his legislative role on housing. The 2020 PDC complaint (over-limit contribution) was cleared without a violation finding, but it shows campaign-finance issues. Recent corporate PAC gifts (T-Mobile, WA Food Industry) while serving as budget leader raise standard access-influence questions.
- **Counter-reading:** Gildon's real power is limited by the Republican minority. The Democratic majority controls the budget process. His role is advisory and oppositional rather than final. His committee leadership is "Republican Leader," not chair. The Pierce County delegation co-chair role is mostly coordinative.
