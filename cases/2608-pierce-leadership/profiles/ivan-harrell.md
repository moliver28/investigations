## §0 why this entity is on the map
Harrell is in the network because he is the 11th President of Tacoma Community College (TCC). TCC is one of the largest public community colleges in Pierce County. The president runs TCC's strategy, budget, and outside advocacy. Harrell also sits on the American Association of Community Colleges (AACC) board. That gives him a say in national community-college policy.
