## 1. Who they are
- **Trade name:** Toyota of Puyallup.

## 1. Who they are
- **Verdict:** Toyota of Puyallup is a single-store, family-run Toyota franchise. It is a high-volume store and it is a known Republican donor in Pierce County politics. The graph edge to Kelly Chambers (weight 3) is confirmed by a verified $1,000 federal donation on July 12, 2020. The same store gave federal money to Hans Zeiger (R, 25th LD), Rob McKenna (R, former WA AG), and Reagan Dunn (R, King County). Its standalone political weight is low (composite 0.001). But the pattern is clear: this is one of the two main River Rd dealers backing the 25th LD and Pierce County Republican donor pool. The story: Glenn Sawyer got the franchise in 1970. His family ran the store for decades. His daughter Kerry Sawyer Odeman took over and grew it to "#1 volume" status. It has won the President's Award 26 times. It has kept giving to federal candidates.
- **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** No direct city-contract conflict shows up in public records. The store only gives to Republican candidates in the records reviewed. That is consistent, but not a legal problem.
- **Analysis of alternatives:** The other reading is that Toyota of Puyallup is just a successful family business. Its political donations are routine civic work. A single $1,000 federal donation is well within normal small-business political activity. That reading is fair. The argument for including it is the pattern. The same store gave to the entire 25th LD Republican group: Zeiger, Chambers, and McKenna-era Pierce Republicans. That fits a dealer-class donor group, not isolated civic donations.
- **Confidence:** High — corporate-identity facts and the political-contribution claim to Kelly Chambers are corroborated by primary-source FEC filings via OpenSecrets, with corporate self-disclosure as secondary corroboration; the cross-corroboration across 5 independent sources is sufficient for a tier3 deep profile.
