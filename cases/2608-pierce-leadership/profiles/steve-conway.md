## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Power held:** Conway holds power from his office as Senate President Pro Tempore (2025–present). That is one of the top leadership jobs in the WA State Senate (source #1, #2). He sits on Labor & Commerce and Ways & Means committees (source #2). His power comes from seniority — 33 straight years in the legislature (1993–2026) make him the longest-serving Pierce County legislator and one of the most senior in the state.
- **Government ties:** As President Pro Tempore, Conway runs Senate sessions when the Lieutenant Governor is absent. As a Ways & Means member, he shapes the state operating budget. On Labor & Commerce, he influences labor and consumer policy.
- **What budget does he touch?** As a Ways & Means member, he helps shape the state operating budget (about $70 billion every two years). The President Pro Tempore role does not directly control the budget.
- **Appointments:** As a senator, he votes to confirm gubernatorial appointments. The President Pro Tempore may have other procedural powers within the Senate. He does not have veto power.
- **Where his power applies:** The 29th Legislative District (south Tacoma, Parkland, Spanaway). As a state senator, his bills apply statewide.
