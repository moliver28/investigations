## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- Miles Sand & Gravel is a family-owned construction materials supplier in Pierce County. The company sells concrete, gravel, and rock for roads, bridges, and buildings.
- Miles is on the map for two reasons. (1) It has real market power in concrete and aggregates across Western Washington. (2) It is well-connected: the company has given money to Pierce County elected officials (Kelly Chambers, Amy Cruver, Paul Herrera) and is a member of the Chamber of Commerce.
- Not on the map separately: Port Orchard Sand & Gravel (a related company in Kitsap/Mason/Jefferson counties) and Concrete Nor'West (acquired in the 1970s and absorbed into Miles).

## 1. Who they are
- **Full name:** Miles Sand & Gravel Company (also called "Miles Sand & Gravel" and the "Miles group of companies").
- **Type:** A private, family-owned business, now in its 5th generation. It is a for-profit company.
- **Where it operates:** Headquartered in Puyallup, WA. Active in 9 or more Western Washington counties.
- **Who founded it:** Walter Miles and his father started the company in the early 1940s. They bought a gravel pit in Auburn, WA. Walter died in 1964. His wife Idamae Miles took over management. Their son Frank Miles ran the company next. Frank grew the company from 6 employees to 900. He died December 8, 2019.
- **Who runs it now:** Tim and Lisa Kittilsby (Frank's daughter and son-in-law) are in charge, per a 2011 source. The Mount Vernon location has Ryan Lemos as Owner/Principal/Partner.
- **Governing law:** Washington State corporate law (RCW Title 23B). The company also follows state mining and environmental rules.

## 3. Where their power comes from
- **Market power:** Miles has plants and quarries in 9 or more Western Washington counties. The service area runs from the Canadian border down to Chehalis. About 500 employees work for the company. It has operated for more than 80 years.
- **Political influence:** Miles has donated to three Pierce County officials: Kelly Chambers (former state rep, 2024 county executive candidate), Amy Cruver (Pierce County Council, District 3), and Paul Herrera (Pierce County Council, District 2). It has given $4,890 to the Washington Aggregate and Concrete Association PAC. It gave $450 to the Island County Republican Party Central Committee.
- **Rules it deals with:** As a mining and aggregate company, Miles needs permits for gravel mining, concrete production, and trucking. The company works with the WA Department of Ecology, Pierce County land-use rules, and local zoning. Its website talks about "environmental responsibility" and "safe work environments."
- **Business stakes:** Miles sells the concrete and rock that go into Pierce County's roads, bridges, and buildings. County infrastructure spending, impact fees, and development rules all affect demand for its products. The company has a direct stake in pro-development county policy.
