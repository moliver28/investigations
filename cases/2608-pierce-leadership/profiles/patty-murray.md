## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Inclusion rule:** decision-making and from holding office. Murray is the senior U.S. Senator from Washington State. She serves as Vice Chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee. That committee is the most powerful funding committee in Congress. She controls Congressionally Directed Spending (CDS) for Washington State. That includes Pierce County projects.

She secured more than $121 million in CDS for WA projects in FY2026 alone. She also brought in more than $5 billion in direct state funding (source #1, #2).

Her former role as President Pro Tempore (2023–2025) placed her third in line for the U.S. presidency. That was the highest federal position held by anyone in this investigation's scope.
- **Adjacent but EXCLUDED candidates:** Sen. Maria Cantwell is Washington's junior senator. She is on the map separately. Gov. Bob Ferguson handles state-level executive power. Rep. Emily Randall (D-6) handles district-level Pierce County representation.
