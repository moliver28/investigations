## 0. Why this person or group is on the map
- **Why he is on the map:** LTG McFarlane commands Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM), the largest Army base on the West Coast. He holds this power from holding office: he is the senior Army officer at JBLM. He makes decisions that shape a base worth about $14 billion a year to Washington State and about $3.9 billion a year to Pierce County (source #4, #5). That mix of money, jobs, and military reach puts the base commander on this map.

He also meets with local civic groups, like the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber and the Rainier Club (source #6, #7).
