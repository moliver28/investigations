## 5. How they touch government
- **Campaign donations:** The Puyallup Tribe (not Sterud personally) donates to political candidates. The graph shows `puyallup-tribe → rosie-ayala (donated, weight 3)`. The Tribe has given $600,000+ to 47 local groups from 2023 Emerald Queen Casino revenue. That is charitable, not political. It also gave $200,000+ to 19 local groups.
- **Lobbying:** Sterud personally testified before the Washington State Senate Transportation Committee on Feb 7. The bill would help tribes reclaim land. He and EQC CEO Yale Rowe also testified for the college sports wagering bill that Gov. Ferguson signed. Those are direct legislative actions.
- **Gaming compact:** The Tribe operates under a tribal-state gaming compact with Washington State. The original compact was signed by Gov. Lowry on May 28, 1996. Seven amendments followed through 2025. The current restated compact was signed by Gov. Ferguson on Sept. 12, 2025. The 7th amendment restates and supersedes all prior amendments. The Puyallup Tribe is one of three tribes (with Muckleshoot and Tulalip) allowed up to 4,000 player terminals (most tribes get 2,500).
- **Federal recognition:** The Puyallup Tribe is a federally recognized Indian Tribe (Bureau of Indian Affairs). It has a self-governance compact with the U.S. Department of the Interior. The Tribal-State Gaming Compact was deemed approved by law and published in the Federal Register (91 FR 3213, Jan 26, 2026).
