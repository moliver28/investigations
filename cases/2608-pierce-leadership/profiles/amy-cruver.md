This is an inference from her words and donor list, not proof.

Her donors may back her because she shares their values, not because they bought her. Her 63% win suggests broad voter agreement. Her limited committee role (member, not chair, on three committees) shows she is a reliable Republican vote in a Council that flipped Democratic in 2024.
