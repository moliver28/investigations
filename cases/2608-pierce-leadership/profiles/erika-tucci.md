## §0 why this entity is on the map
Erika Tucci runs one of the largest private foundations in Pierce County, the Ben B. Cheney Foundation. She also chairs a governor's commission. Both roles give her real power over civic agenda-setting and grantmaking in the South Sound. She used to work at Tacoma Public Utilities. That gives her a bridge into the public-sector utilities world.

## §1 Identity
**FACT** Erika Tucci is the Executive Director of the Ben B. Cheney Foundation. She was appointed in November 2023. She took over from Brad Cheney, who served more than 20 years. She is the first woman to lead the foundation.
**Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** Before becoming Executive Director, Tucci served as the Foundation's Senior Program Officer. She has been in that role since 2019.
**Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Tucci previously led community affairs at Tacoma Public Utilities. There, she started a leadership program that encouraged staff to volunteer.
