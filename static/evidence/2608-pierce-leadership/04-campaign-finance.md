## 5. Key Observations

1. **County Executive was the most expensive race:** Combined candidate spending of ~$1.05M plus ~$500K+ in independent/party expenditures.

2. **Party money dominated Chambers' fundraising:** 32% of her total ($175,500) came from the Pierce County Republican Party alone.

3. **Mello had broader individual donor base:** 716 itemized donors vs. 585 for Chambers, with many union and progressive PACs contributing the $2,400 maximum.

4. **Sheriff's race was closer in spending:** Swank raised $121K, Jackson raised $135K, but Swank received significant IE support from the WA State Republican Party (~$19.6K in-kind postage) while Jackson got IE support from Fuse Votes (~$10.7K).

5. **Council races showed steep fundraising disparities:** In the most expensive council race, Loujanna Rohrer raised $215K (heavily self-funded + party) but lost to Jani Hitchen who raised $138K. Most other council winners raised between $37K-$57K.

6. **Together for Pierce County** was the largest IE spender at $293,755, producing mailers supporting Mello and opposing Chambers.
