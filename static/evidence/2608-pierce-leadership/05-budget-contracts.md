## 2026-2027 Biennial Budget: $3.5 Billion

Adopted Nov 25, 2025. Signed by Executive Ryan Mello Dec 16, 2025.
Covers Jan 1, 2026 – Dec 31, 2027.

### By Fund Type

| Fund | Amount | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Special Revenue | $1,115.6M | Roads, human services grants, etc. |
| General Fund | $967.4M | Core operations — up 2.2% |
| Enterprise | $561.5M | Sewer, airports, ferries |
| Capital Projects | $353.7M | Infrastructure |
| Internal Service | $339.1M | Fleet, IT, facilities |
| TPCHD (Health Dept) | $102.9M | Down $24M from prior — 22 staff cut |
| Debt Service | $25.5M | — |

### General Fund: Largest Departments

| Department | 2026-27 Budget | % of GF |
|-----------|----------------|---------|
| Sheriff — Law Enforcement | $242.2M | 25% |
| Sheriff — Corrections (Jail) | $164.3M | 17% |
| Prosecuting Attorney | $94.1M | 10% |
| Juvenile Court | $54.9M | 6% |
| Superior Court | $51.7M | 5% |
| Assigned Counsel (Indigent Defense) | $50.3M | 5% |

**Total law enforcement/justice: ~77% of General Fund.** Sheriff alone = 42% of GF.

### Budget Constraints
- Hiring freeze on General Fund positions (except public safety)
- Limited travel and training
- Reserves adequate through 2029, then projected shortfall
- TPCHD budget cut $24M → 22 staff layoffs
- Federal revenue uncertainty flagged by council

## Procurement System

Pierce County uses **OpenGov** for electronic procurement: https://procurement.opengov.com/portal/piercecountywa

### Key Procurement Facts:
- All RFPs and IFBs advertised in **The News Tribune** (legal newspaper of record)
- **Small Works Roster** for contracts under $350K
- **Procurement & Contract Services Division** under Finance & Enterprise Services
- Primary contact: pcpurchasing@co.pierce.wa.us / 253-798-7456

### Human Services Procurement (major contract areas)
- Affordable housing capital (Maureen Howard + 1406 Funds) — Q2-Q3 2026
- Behavioral health — Residential facility, opioid settlement funds
- Homeless programs — Shelters, rapid rehousing, coordinated entry
- Veterans housing
- Youth violence prevention

### Notable Sole Source Awards (TPCHD):
- **Tacoma Parks Foundation** — sole source (June 2025)
- **Dave Purchase Project** — sole source (Oct 2024)
