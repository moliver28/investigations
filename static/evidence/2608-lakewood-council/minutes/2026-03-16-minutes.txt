ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION
Update and Request for Direction regarding H-Barn Restoration
Project.
Roxanne Miles, Parks, Recreation & Community Services Director shared
that as a result of increased cost estimates for the H-Barn restoration
project, prior to proceeding to 60% design, options are being considered
to maximize efficiency, function and keep the essence of the H-Barn and
silos for the community and future users. Discussion ensued whether to
design around a prefabricated building, toward renovation of the barns
using salvageable elements of the structures or using the barn as a
passive space. After discussion a meeting between Partners for Parks,
Councilmembers Brandstetter, Pearson and Talbo and the Parks and
Recreation Advisory Board will be held to discuss the concept and
business plan prior to proceeding.
Review of the FY 2026 Annual Action Plan for Community
Development Block Grant (CDBG) and HOME Programs.
Jeff Gumm, Program Manager shared that the 2026 Annual Action Plan
is a framework for how the city will utilize its CDBG and HOME funds. He
reviewed the eligible activities to serve community interests and funding
allocations for 2026 for CDBG and HOME programs.
He then reviewed the public participation process for the Annual Action
Plan and shared that next steps include a public hearing by the City
Council on April 20th, a review by the Planning Commission with final
approval by the City Council on May 4th and submission to HUD by May
15th.
