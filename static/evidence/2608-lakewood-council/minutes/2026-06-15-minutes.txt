ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION
Review of facility use agreement with West Coast Baseball League
Franchise.
Roxanne Miles, Parks, Recreation and Community Services Director was
joined by Tony Bonacci Southwest Washington Baseball Group and
Duncan Stevenson, Pierce College. Miles shared that the City continues
discussions regarding establishing a partnership with West Coast
Baseball League to operate a collegiate league to hold 35-45 games at
Fort Steilacoom Park. She shared that the capital investment totals $3.2
Million which would be completed in two phases. She shared that the
next steps include approval of the facility use agreement and
submitting a capital funding request to the Lodging Tax Advisory
Committee for review. Discussion ensued.

Review of Resolution establishing an encroachment evaluation
process for lake terminating rights-of-way.
Jeff Rimack, Director, Planning and Public Works shared that the City
has continued to evaluate options for a framework for managing streetend right-of-way sites that may provide public water access and address
encroachments. He shared that peer jurisdictions have been reviewed
for best practices and next steps include the adoption of a Resolution
establishing the framework and development of administrative tools.
Discussion ensued.
Review of HOME Funding for Tacoma-Pierce County Habitat for
Humanity – American Lake Townhomes Down Payment Assistance at
15206 Portland Ave SW, 15210 Portland Ave SW, and 15211 88th Ave Ct.
SW, Lakewood.
Jeff Gumm, Program Manager shared that the City has received a
request to commit $180,000 in HOME Investment Partnership Program
funds to Tacoma-Pierce County Habitat for Humanity to be used as
down payment assistance for low-income homebuyers. The funding will
assist three properties located at 15206 Portland Ave SW, 15210 Portland
Ave SW and 15211 88th Ave Ct. SW and will be provided as a zero interest
