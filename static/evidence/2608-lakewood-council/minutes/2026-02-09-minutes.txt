ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION:
Review of 4th Quarter (2025) Police Report.
Chief Patrick Smith shared that in 4th quarter 2025 overall arrests
decreased by 2%, property and society crimes decreased by 26%, pursuits
decreased by 27%, eludes decreased by 45%, motor vehicle thefts
decreased 53% and traffic collisions decreased by 10%. He then reviewed
the Lakewood Police Department’s 2026 goals and objectives. Discussion
ensued.
Review of grant from the Washington State Department of Commerce
for the Natural Environment and Climate Change (NECC) Program.
Planning Manager Tiffany Speir shared that the City has been awarded a
grant from the Washington State Department of Commerce totaling
$355,000 to complete climate change resiliency work through 2027. The
grant will allow for an expanded scope of work on the NECC Program.
Discussion ensued.
Review of the Engineering Development Regulations including the
Engineering Standards Manual, Lakewood Municipal Code Titles 12, 13
and Chapter 18A.20.
Planning and Public Works Assistant Director Angie Silva who was joined by
Director Jeff Rimack shared that a public hearing on this topic is scheduled
