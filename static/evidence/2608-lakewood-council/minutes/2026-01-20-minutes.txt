ITEMS FOR DISCUSION
Review of Lakewood Municipal Code amendments related to the
allowed locations of alcohol production and sales.
Planning Manager Tiffany Speir provided an overview of recommended
amendments to the Lakewood Municipal Code related to alcohol sales
and production which are in compliance with current state law and
regulatory approaches. She shared that next steps will include a public
hearing scheduled for the Council meeting of February 2nd followed by
adoption on February 19th. Discussion ensued.

Review of 2024 Limited Tax General Obligation (LTGO) Bond Refund.
Deputy City Manager Tho Kraus shared that the bonds were issued in
2024 and the City has the opportunity refinance them to reduce debt
service costs and save $153,000 over the life. It is recommended that the
City Council authorize refinancing of the 2024 Limited Tax General
Obligation (LTGO) Bonds. Discussion ensued.
Review of 3rd Quarter (2025) Financial Report.
Deputy City Manager Tho Kraus provided an overview of activity in all
funds through September 30, 2025. Discussion ensued.
Review of 112th Street – Farwest Drive to Holden Road Project.
Capital Projects Manager Troy Potswinski shared that the 112th Street
from Farwest Drive to Holden Road is a Safe Routes to School project
which includes bike lanes, street lightening, sidewalks and minor traffic
improvements near Lake Louise Elementary School. He shared that the
project is currently advertising for bids with a contract anticipated to
come forward for City Council approval on February 17th. Discussion
ensued.
**********
Discussion then ensued regarding traffic impacts from the 112th Street
between Gravelly Lake Drive and Bridgeport Way. Councilmember Talbo
requested that the City Council consider road and transportation
projects from a safety perspective and in support of ranking
Comprehensive Transportation Improvement Plan (TIP) projects.
Councilmember Pearson spoke about considering 108th Street for a site
for future automated traffic safety cameras.
REPORTS BY THE CITY MANAGER
City Manager Russell confirmed that City Councilmembers are all
available for a Retreat to be held on Saturday, April 18th.
He shared that the City is adding 30th Anniversary component to each of
the events this year with a kickoff at the May 29th Streets Festival and a
larger event at SummerFEST. After discussion, the City Council
recommended considering a physical and digital time capsule with past
and current relics, tree planting, purchasing art for display at city hall,
inviting prior elected officials as well as those involved in the cityhood
campaign and the city’s Sister City representatives.
He then requested that the City Council identify their availability on
February 18th, 19th or 23rd to travel to Olympia to meet with State
Legislators.

CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
Councilmember Lauricella requested that the city review data regarding
potential revenues related to the sales of cannabis in the city.
Councilmember Talbo shared that she supports an update on data
related to sale of cannabis in the city.
Councilmember Brandstetter shared that he will attend the South
Sound 911 Board meeting where they will be selecting a new Board
Chair. He requested donations from Councilmembers towards the
Lakewood Lions Youth scholarship. He spoke about considering
community impact related to cannabis sales and he spoke about bills
under review by the State Legislature.
Deputy Mayor Belle thanked those who provided public comments this
evening and encouraged residents to reach out to the City to provide
accurate information. She is also in support of data regarding cannabis
sales and shared that she will attend the Association of Washington
Cities (AWC) City Action Days this week.
Mayor Bocchi shared that he signed on to a letter of opposition
regarding House Bill 2489, Unhoused Bill of Rights.
