PUBLIC COMMENTS
The City Council received written comments in advance of the meeting
from Walli Roarke.

Tichomir Dunlop, Lakewood resident, requested the City support the

lights out initiative, now through the end of May, to protect birds during
migration.

Garry Oak trees to be cut down and in support of tree preservation.

access to Gravelly Lake and in opposition to increasing public access to
Gravelly Lake.
