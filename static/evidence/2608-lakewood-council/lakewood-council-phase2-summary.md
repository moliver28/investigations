## 🔴 Critical Conflicts (4 members)

- **Issue:** Dual employment with Pierce County
- **Role:** Senior Legislative Analyst - Budget, Pierce County Council
- **Conflict:** Makes city budget decisions while employed by county; county and city have intergovernmental funding relationships
- **Recusal Required:** All votes on county-city intergovernmental agreements, funding transfers, joint projects

### 2. Patti Belle (Deputy Mayor) - HIGH CONFLICT
- **Issue:** Dual employment with competing municipality
- **Role:** Communications Manager, City of Kent Mayor's Office
- **Conflict:** Kent is WA's 6th largest city; potential competitor for state/federal grants, regional resources
- **Recusal Required:** All votes involving Kent, regional agreements, intercity funding

### 3. Philip Lindholm (Councilmember) - HIGH CONFLICT
- **Issue:** Real estate business interests
- **Role:** CEO/Owner, Concord Counsel (real estate brokerage, mediation, tribal law)
- **License:** Washington real estate broker (active)
- **Conflict:** Makes land use, zoning, development decisions that affect his business
- **Campaign:** Self-funded 2025 campaign (PDC records confirm)
- **Recusal Required:** All development permits, zoning changes, planning decisions, tribal land issues

### 4. Ryan Pearson (Councilmember) - HIGH CONFLICT
- **Issue:** County employment reviewing developments
- **Role:** Engineering/Technical Review Manager, Pierce County
- **Work:** Commercial/industrial/residential subdivision projects, development regulations
- **Conflict:** County employee approving subdivisions that affect Lakewood; infrastructure planning crosses city boundaries
- **Recusal Required:** All development approvals, subdivision reviews, infrastructure projects affecting county jurisdiction
