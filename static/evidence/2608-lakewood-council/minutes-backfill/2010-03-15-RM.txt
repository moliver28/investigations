REPORT S BY T HE CIT Y MANAGER

City Manager Neiditz announced that on March 18, 2010, from 6:00 p.m. -8:00 p.m., a neighborhood forum will be held on Wards Lake
Park. He also announced that the League of Women Voters invited the City to attend THE NEWS TRIBUNE forum on March 18, 2010 to
discuss the Open Meetings Act. With regard to the Colonial Center Theatre, a Request for Proposal was distributed this past week and a
site tour is scheduled for interested consultants to visit with the City and property owners on March 18, 2010. Proposals are due on April
12, 2010.

City Manager Neiditz reported that the City has petitioned the Utilities of Transportation Commission to hold public hearings about the Pt.
Defiance Bypass Rail project in Lakewood and there is a pre-hearing meeting scheduled for tomorrow, March 16, 2010. He added that
the Clover Park School District has been informed of the public hearings and the City of Dupont did adopt a resolution opposing the use of
the Barksdale crossing in DuPont.

City Manager Neiditz introduced Lt. Steve Mauer who is leading the Community Service Resource Team (CSRT). All the neighborhood
association presidents will be meeting with Steve Mauer to discuss the efforts of the CSRT team. Councilmember Brandstetter spoke
about the service boundaries of neighborhood association districts and asked if there is flexibility for redistricting the boundaries.
Councilmember Neary asked how the CSRT program will be rolled out and what is the focus and priorities.

PROCLAMAT IONS AND PRESENT AT IONS
Pierce T ransit Syst em Re-Design Updat e.

Mr. Lind Simonsen, Pierce Transit Community Relations, spoke about significant cutbacks that Pierce Transit will be facing which will
impact Lakewood citizens. He then provided an overview of designing a transit system that the community values and uses. He also
reviewed Transit’s sales tax revenue since 2007 and a projected $68 million loss by 2012. He reviewed new funding concepts and
Transit’s proposed sales tax increase to help restore services. He announced that a Lakewood Public Meeting will be held on March 23,
2010 from 6:00 p.m. - 7:30 pm to invite public input about Transit’s redesign.

Discussion ensued on the proposed cuts and comparison of revenues from previous years; service levels; and what information will be
presented at the March 23 Lakewood informational meeting.

BRIEFING BY T HE CIT Y MANAGER
City Manager Neiditz reported that the annual employee recognition event has been merged with the monthly employee recognition
event into a quarterly recognition event. He announced that the first quarter event was held last week in celebration of the City’s
birthday and recognition of employees for outstanding service in the City’s guiding principle for service. The two employee recipients for
the first quarter were Faye Landskov and Kim Dodds.

City Manager Neiditz recognized Tes Ongoco for receiving her Certification as a Certified Municipal Clerk from the International Institute of
Municipal Clerks.

With regard to the University Place Court contract, City Manager Neiditz reported that meetings are being held with attorneys from both
cities to work on the Municipal Court contract and get ready for the transition.

City Manager Neiditz announced that Judge Heller has appointed Deana Wright as Court Administrator.

City Manager Neiditz reported that a national hotel firm is working with City staff for locating along the I-5 corridor.
