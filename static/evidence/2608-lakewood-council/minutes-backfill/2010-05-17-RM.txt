Update on long term financial plan.
Assistant City Manager Halladay presented a PowerPoint presentation of a long term financial plan for the City’s finances. He
explained that the goal is to identify structure deficiencies and long term trends. He reviewed proposed revenue and
expenditure projections to 2012. He then reviewed three scenarios representing a speedy economic recovery, a path of least
resistance and no growth. He then reviewed the Street Funds and the vehicle/equipment and facility depreciation funds.

Councilmember Neary suggested that there be more explanation of the three scenarios and that this information be shared
with the Citizens Advisory Boards and Committees.

City Manager Neiditz distributed copies of the narrative to accompany the PowerPoint presentation.

Update on e-verify.

In response to comments about e-verify, City Manager Neiditz presented some background information about the City’s e-verify
policy and that, for the most part, it has been working successfully. He reported that there had been some challenges with the
contracts with the Tacoma Visitor and Convention Bureau (VCB), Lakewold Gardens and the Lakewood Historical Society. He
added that Pierce County’s e-verify policy is less restrictive than the City and the federal government may have some
exceptions.

Mayor Richardson noted that commercial products purchased off the shelf is not subject to e-verify. Deputy Mayor Anderson
commented on Arizona’s e-verify program which relates to employers. Councilmember Brandstetter questioned how the VCB
could be exempt from e-verify and considered to be a “quasi-government” non-profit, when their funding sources are from
public agencies (cities, towns and county), and non-profit human services agencies who could also be receiving their funding
from public agencies (cities, towns and county) are subject to e-verify.

Mayor Richardson noted that the intent of the Council was to apply e-verify to all contractors and subcontractors.
