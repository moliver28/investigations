REPORTS BY THE CITY MANAGER
Interim City Manager Tho Kraus reported that Sister City Danzhou, China
has requested to visit on September 23rd and the discussion should
include tours and a discussion with the Tacoma Pierce County Economic
Development Board and Port of Tacoma. Sister City Gimhae, South
Korea delegates will visit the City on October 30th for one night for eight
delegates and they have invited the City to the Gimhae Ceramic Festival
in the beginning of November.
She then shared that the City Clerk has been monitoring neighborhood
association and Desserts on the Driveway events and recommended
that Councilmembers be assigned to attend each meeting. After
discussion, the City Clerk will notice future meetings indicating that
there may be a quorum.
