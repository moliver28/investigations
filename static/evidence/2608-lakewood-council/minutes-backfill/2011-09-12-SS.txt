ITEMS FOR DISCUSSION:
Joint meeting with the Lakewood Arts Commission
Arts Commission Chair Shirley Petersen reviewed the significant accomplishments of the Arts Commission and presented a
slide show of their activities. She then reviewed next year’s 2011-2012 goals for promoting the visual, performing and literary
arts, encouraging the creative contribution of local artists and creating a City public art policy.
Discussion ensued on reader board signage; increasing attendance at the Martin Luther King (MLK) event; developing an
attractive website in promoting events and tourism; exploring reader boards at City gateways; exploring a privately funded sign
with the City; selling the MLK event as an entertainment event; developing cooperative partnerships with the educational
community; seeking private funding for art; providing for electricity at the north gateway entry; creating an art foundation; and
developing a policy for an arts program.
BRIEFING BY THE CITY MANAGER
City Manager Neiditz announced that the September 19, 2011 Council meeting is cancelled. He indicated that in follow-up to
the Council’s June retreat, a strategic planning meeting is being scheduled.
City Manager Neiditz indicated that emergency management continues to be a high priority of the CSRT program. He noted
that a grant was received by West Pierce Fire to hire an emergency management coordinator who will be partnering with
Lakewood.
City Manager Neiditz announced that the Marcus Walker Way street sign to commemorate Marcus Walker will be sited next
month.
He indicated that he was asked to find an appropriate feature to recognize Dave Sclair for his contributions in Lakewood.
He reported that the Life guard program ended on Labor Day weekend; and in light of the extended summer weather, the
program could be extended for a longer period of time in future years.
He then reported that in response to a BCRA offer, Pierce County Library is interested in doing a master planning process with
the City.
He then provided an update on the theatre project.

Lastly, he announced that Communications Director Jeff Brewster has received orders that he will be deployed overseas for 11
months starting the end of this month. Mr. Mike Savage will be Acting Communications Director for the next 11 months.

CITY COUNCIL COMMENTS
Councilmember Brandstetter asked about the traffic circulation pattern in Tillicum and requested that the Council be briefed on
ways to prohibit the cut-through traffic pattern.
Councilmember Whalen asked if the landlords will be involved in the strategic brainstorming session of the theatre project.
Councilmember Thomas reported that there is a drug house across the street from the Harrison Preparatory School that
involved a shooting and drug trafficking and asked if Police could look into this. Councilmember Thomas thanked those who
supported her sorority funding event.
Councilmember Whalen commented on the Lakewood Salutes event over the weekend. He also commented on the 911 event
on Sunday.
Councilmember Moss commented on the 911 celebration at the Fire Department and the military march support event.
The Council asked how does the City communicate to the public on the citizen’s comments received by the Community
Development Department about the right of way permit application for Camp Murray gate relocation. Council requested that
the Notice asking for public comment be posted on the City’s website.
