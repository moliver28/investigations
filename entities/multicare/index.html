## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Inclusion rule — power and decision-making.** MultiCare Health System is on this map because it is the largest private employer in Pierce County. It has 22,789 employees, runs 9 hospitals across Washington, and takes in more than $5 billion a year. - MultiCare runs Tacoma General, Good Samaritan, Allenmore, Auburn, Covington, Capital Medical Center, and Mary Bridge Children's Hospital. It also runs Deaconess Hospital and Valley Hospital in Spokane. - Its board connects to Tacoma Community College (TCC), the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber of Commerce, the Washington State Hospital Association (WSHA), and Pacific Lutheran University (PLU). All four are on this map. - MultiCare's choices about what services to offer, where to expand, and whom to hire shape Pierce County's healthcare and its job market. - **Adjacent but excluded:** MultiCare Foundations (EIN 91-1514257) is the giving arm. Mary Bridge Children's Foundation (EIN 94-303-0039) raises money for one hospital. Rockwood Clinic in Spokane and Indigo Urgent Care are also MultiCare, but outside Pierce County or not power nodes.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Market power:** MultiCare is the largest community-based, locally governed health system in Washington. In 2024 it had $5.01 billion in revenue and 22,789 employees. It operates 9 hospitals with 1,895 beds. In 2024 it logged 84,327 admissions, 431,429 patient days, 75,990 surgeries, 9,704 births, 480,752 outpatient visits, and 612,092 ER visits. That gives it the dominant share of the hospital market in Pierce County. - **Employment power:** With 22,789 employees, MultiCare is the largest private employer in Pierce County. That comes from the IRS Form 990 and from MultiCare's own claims. - **Tax-exempt power:** As a 501(c)(3), MultiCare does not pay property tax or sales tax. Donors can write off gifts. That is a big public subsidy. - **Philanthropy and influence:** MultiCare's community fund gave 66 grants in 2024, totaling $10.8 million. This builds goodwill and softens public scrutiny. - **Lobbying and policy:** MultiCare belongs to WSHA (Washington State Hospital Association). CEO Bill Robertson was WSHA Board Chair from 2020 to 2021. MultiCare also lobbies the Pierce County Council on health policy. - **Key facts:** - Tax status: 501(c)(3) public charity, tax-exempt since May 1991. - Board size: 10 unpaid directors. Board seats overlap with TCC, the Chamber, WSHA, and PLU. - Government funding share: About 95.7% of revenue comes from Medicare and Medicaid (the federal health programs for seniors and low-income people). That is $4.795 billion of $5.01 billion.

MultiCare got to this position through decades of acquisition (Auburn, Covington, Capital Medical Center, Deaconess, Valley) and organic growth, expanding from a Tacoma-centric hospital system to a statewide empire under Robertson's leadership (2014-2026). - **Conflicts-of-interest flags (defense counter-argument):** The defense would argue MultiCare's power is benign — it is a nonprofit providing essential healthcare services, its tax exemption is legally granted by IRS, and its community grants demonstrate genuine philanthropic intent.
