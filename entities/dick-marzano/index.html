## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Dick Marzano is one of five elected Port of Tacoma commissioners. In 2026 he became commission president. That makes him co-chair of the Northwest Seaport Alliance (NWSA) Managing Members. This group controls marine cargo terminals across Puget Sound. - He has held this seat since 1995. That is 31 years. He is the longest-serving port commissioner in Pierce County. - People looked at but left out: other commissioners John McCarthy, Deanna Keller, JT Wilcox, and Kristin Ang. Also staff at the Economic Development Board (EDB), current leaders of International Longshore and Warehouse Union (ILWU) Local 23, and Washington State Department of Transportation (WSDOT) Gateway Program staff.
