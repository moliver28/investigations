## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why we added Phil Fortunato:** He holds office. He is the sitting State Senator for Washington's 31st Legislative District. The district covers southeast King County and northeast Pierce County. It includes Auburn, Bonney Lake, Buckley, Carbonado, Edgewood, Milton, South Prairie, Sumner, and Wilkeson. The district covers a large slice of Pierce County. He sits on four Senate committees: Law and Justice, Transportation, State Government/Tribal Affairs/Elections, and Business/Trade/Economic Development. His district overlaps with many Pierce County cities and towns. - **Other people we did not add:** Drew Stokesbary (R-31, House Position 1) and Josh Penner (R-31, House Position 2) are his district-mates. They are added in this investigation separately. Pam Roach, a former D31 Senator who came before Fortunato, is excluded because she no longer holds office.

## 1. Who they are - **Legal name:** Philip D. Fortunato (Source 1). - **Common name:** Phil Fortunato. - **Birth:** Born 1953 or 1954 (Source 1). Birthplace not verified. - **Home:** Auburn, Washington (Source 1, 2, 5). - **Family:** Wife Suzanne Fortunato (married 42 or more years per Source 5). Five children. Three of the children served in the U.S. Marine Corps (Source 1, 5). - **Education:** Rutgers University, New Brunswick (Source 1). - **Job:** Owner and founder of Eco-3. Eco-3 is an erosion and stormwater management company based in Kent, WA. He has been a contractor for 30 or more years. He started his first business at age 18 (Source 2, 4, 5). He is a recognized expert on the federal Clean Water Act, which controls water pollution. He was the first person in Washington certified as a CESCL instructor. CESCL stands for Certified Erosion and Sediment Control Lead. The Washington State Department of Ecology certifies them (Source 4). - **Group memberships:** Knights of Columbus, Buckley Eagles, Sons of Italy, Washington Arms Collectors, the National Rifle Association (NRA), Renton Fish and Game Club, and Black Diamond Gun Club (Source 5). He is a past president and founding director of the Pacific Northwest chapter of the International Erosion Control Association, or IECA (Source 4).

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Elected office:** Washington State Senator, 31st Legislative District, since January 7, 2017. Current term ends January 11, 2027. He is running for re-election in the November 2026 general election. He advanced from the August 4, 2026 primary with 53.9% of the vote against Tamara Stramel (D), who got 46.0% (Source 2). Before that, he served in the Washington House of Representatives, 47th District, from January 11, 1999 to January 8, 2001 (Source 1). - **Committees (2025–2026 session):** Law and Justice; Transportation; State Government, Tribal Affairs and Elections; Business, Trade and Economic Development. He also serves on joint committees: Joint Committee on Veterans' and Military Affairs; Horse Racing Commission; Capitol Campus Design Advisory Committee; Election Administration and Certification Board; Puget Sound Partnership Ecosystem Coordination Board; Financial Education Public-Private Partnership; and School Safety Summit (Source 3). - **Past statewide campaigns:** He ran for Governor of Washington in 2020. He finished 6th in the primary with 4.0% (99,265 votes) (Source 1, 2). He ran for Insurance Commissioner in 2024. He lost the general election to Patricia Kuderer (D), 43.3% to 56.6% (Source 2). - **Budget power:** Fortunato is in the minority party. He does not control a budget on his own. He takes part in budget talks through his committee seats. - **Jurisdiction:** 31st Legislative District — Auburn, Bonney Lake, Buckley, Carbonado, Edgewood, Milton, South Prairie, Sumner, Wilkeson (Source 5).

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Fortunato's power comes from his long tenure as State Senator for the 31st District and his wide committee portfolio (Law and Justice, Transportation, State Government/Elections, Business/Economic Development). He does well at the district level (consistently 55 to 60% in his district) but does not win statewide races. He lost the Insurance Commissioner race by 13 points and placed 6th in the gubernatorial primary. His business, Eco-3, sits at the meeting point of his private commercial work and his legislative role overseeing construction, environmental rules, and economic development. - **Conflicts of interest:** Eco-3, Fortunato's erosion control company, works in a field directly regulated by the state he helps govern. His seats on the Business, Trade and Economic Development committee and the Transportation committee overlap with his business in construction-related environmental compliance. His CESCL training certification from the Washington State Department of Ecology is a state-recognized business relationship. His NRA and gun club memberships signal an ideological match that may shape his work on the Law and Justice committee. - **The other view:** Fortunato is a back-bench minority party senator with limited real power. The Republican caucus holds only 19 of 49 Senate seats. His repeated statewide runs (Governor, Insurance Commissioner) have failed. His influence is confined to his conservative district. His wide committee list may reflect a lack of seniority-based specialization rather than strong influence. His company is a small enterprise, not a major contractor. - **Confidence:** High — many primary sources (the WA Legislature, the PDC, the WA Department of Ecology, the Senate Republican Caucus, and certified WA Secretary of State results) confirm the key claims. The SRCC bio note that he was "elected to the House but appointed to the Senate before sworn in" is a small biographical wrinkle, but it stays consistent across sources.
