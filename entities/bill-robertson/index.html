## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Bill Robertson is on this map because he runs MultiCare Health System. MultiCare is the largest private employer in Pierce County. It is also the largest not-for-profit, locally owned health system in Washington State. He has been President and CEO since May 1, 2014. - Under his leadership, MultiCare grew from a Tacoma-focused hospital group into the largest community-owned nonprofit health system in the region. - He also chaired the Washington State Hospital Association (WSHA) board from 2020 to 2021. WSHA lobbies in Olympia on behalf of member hospitals. - He plans to retire on December 31, 2026. Florence Chang will take over as President and CEO on January 1, 2027.

## 1. Who they are - **Name:** William G. "Bill" Robertson. - **Education:** B.S. in Accounting from Southern Adventist University. MBA from Texas Christian University. He is also a Certified Public Accountant (CPA). - **Home:** Tacoma, WA area since May 2014. He and his wife moved to the South Sound when he joined MultiCare. - **Career:** He was CEO of Shawnee Mission Medical Center near Kansas City, KS. Then he was President and CEO of Adventist HealthCare Inc. in Gaithersburg, MD. He became President and CEO of MultiCare Health System on May 1, 2014. He took over from Diane Cecchettini. In 2022 his title became CEO. Florence Chang was named President, then President and CEO starting January 1, 2027. - **Family:** His wife was named in a 2014 announcement.
