## §0 why this entity is on the map Littmann is in the network because she is the chief executive of the Greater Tacoma Community Foundation (GTCF). She has held the job since July 2015. The CEO role puts her in charge of a $207M-asset foundation that has given out $218M in grants over its life. The foundation touches almost every nonprofit and agency in Pierce County. Littmann also sits on the Pacific Lutheran University (PLU) Board of Regents. That seat ties her into PLU's governance.

## §1 Identity **FACT** Kathi Littmann, Ed.D., is the President & CEO of the Greater Tacoma Community Foundation (GTCF), based in Gig Harbor, WA. She has been in the role since July 2015. In June 2026 she said she plans to step down by the end of 2027. **Source:** https://www.gtcf.org/news-reports/gtcf-marks-45-years-and-looks-to-its-next-chapter (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** Under Littmann's tenure, GTCF assets have grown to more than $207 million. Total grants over the foundation's life have passed $218 million. **Source:** https://www.gtcf.org/news-reports/gtcf-marks-45-years-and-looks-to-its-next-chapter (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** She is a member of the Pacific Lutheran University Board of Regents. She was nominated in 2020 for a 2020–2023 term. Her name appears in PLU's Form 990 (the tax filing) as a Regent. The seat is unpaid. **Source:** https://www.plu.edu/strategic-planning/2020/05/16/spring-meeting-of-the-board-of-regents (primary; reliability 0.9); PLU Form 990 via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer: https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/910565571 (primary; reliability 0.9).

**FACT** Her career path includes Senior Program Officer at the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation (Feb 2013–Jun 2015). She also founded the consulting firm 2RDS in 2002. Earlier work spans education, construction, and national philanthropy. **Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/kathil (secondary; reliability 0.7); https://www.gtcf.org/news-reports/gtcf-marks-45-years-and-looks-to-its-next-chapter (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** She won the University of Washington Tacoma (UWT) Milgard School of Business 2019 Nonprofit Leader of the Year award. She also won the Philanthropy Northwest Ambassador of the Year award in 2019. **Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/kathil (secondary; reliability 0.7).

## §2 Power basis **FACT** As GTCF President & CEO, Littmann sets the agenda for one of Pierce County's biggest philanthropic groups. GTCF runs more than $207M in assets and has given out $218M+ in total grants. The CEO shapes strategy, equity plans, and key funding partnerships. **Source:** https://www.gtcf.org/news-reports/gtcf-marks-45-years-and-looks-to-its-next-chapter (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** Her PLU regent seat gives her a voice in how PLU is run. PLU is a private university that trains many of Pierce County's professionals. PLU also has a big endowment and holds real estate in Parkland. **Source:** https://www.plu.edu/strategic-planning/2020/05/16/spring-meeting-of-the-board-of-regents (primary; reliability 0.9).
