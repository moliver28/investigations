## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why it is on the map.** Clover Park School District (CPSD) is one of Pierce County's largest K-12 public school districts. It serves about 12,395 students across 33 schools. Its schools sit in Lakewood, Tillicum, Woodbrook, and parts of unincorporated Pierce County (a county not part of any city). It also borders Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM). The district runs on a $262 million budget for the 2024-25 school year (FY2024-25). It has the legal power to levy property taxes. It is a Purple Star Award District, which means it serves many military-connected students whose families move with JBLM's deployment cycle. It has won statewide awards for its board and its superintendent. - **Who is left out.** Tacoma Public Schools, Bethel, Puyallup, University Place, Sumner-Bonney Lake, and Peninsula are separate districts and are mapped on their own pages. JBLM itself is mapped separately. The city of Lakewood is mapped on its own. Madigan Army Medical Center is part of JBLM and is mapped under military and defense.

## 1. Who they are - **Legal name:** Clover Park School District (CPSD). - **Type:** A public K-12 school district under Washington State law. It is a government body, not a nonprofit. The IRS does not treat it as a 501(c)(3) (federal tax-exempt charity). - **Governing law:** RCW 28A (Washington's Revised Code of Washington for common schools) and the Pierce County Charter. - **EIN (tax ID number):** Not verified. School districts are government bodies and do not file IRS Form 990. - **Location:** Head office in Lakewood, Washington. Schools spread across Lakewood, Tillicum, Woodbrook, parts of unincorporated Pierce County, and a small part of Tacoma near JBLM. - **Schools:** 33 schools (2023-24). - **Students:** 12,395 (2023-24). - **How it is run:** A 5-member elected Board of Directors governs it. No single person owns it. - **Subsidiaries:** None found.

The Board won WSSDA 2024 Board of Distinction and 2019 Board of the Year for large districts. The 5-member Board is elected by district on a nonpartisan ballot.
