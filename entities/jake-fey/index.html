## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why he is here:** Jake Fey is a state House member for the 27th Legislative District. He took office on January 14, 2013. He is the Chair of the House Transportation Committee. That is one of the most powerful seats in the state for transportation money, and that money shapes Pierce County roads and buses. He led the $16.9 billion Move Ahead Washington package through the legislature. - **Who is NOT on this page:** State Senator Emily Randall (D-26) chairs the Senate Transportation Committee. She is on a different page. Fey's former staff at the WSU Energy Program are not on this page. His wife Janine Fey is a private citizen, not a public figure. ## 1.
