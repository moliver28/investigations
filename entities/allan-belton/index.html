## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Allan Belton is the 14th President of Pacific Lutheran University (PLU), a private Lutheran school in Parkland, Pierce County. He runs a school that brings in millions each year. He hires and fires top staff. He sets the school's direction. - He is on the map because he holds this job. The PLU Board of Regents picked him. The board can also fire him. Board members are the power above the president. - PLU is one of the largest private employers in the Parkland and Tacoma area. The school serves about 3,000 students each year.

## 1. Who they are - **Legal name:** Allan Belton. - **Age:** 52 when appointed, per the News Tribune in 2019. - **Residence:** Gig Harbor, WA (per his PLU bio). - **Family:** Married to Melinda Krotz Belton (PLU Class of 1991). They have three children. - **Education:** - B.A. in Business Administration from Washington State University. - M.B.A. (Master of Business Administration) from Washington State University. - **Career path:** 25 years at Bank of America Merrill Lynch (last role: Managing Director and Chief Operations Officer for Global Treasury Management) → PLU Chief Financial Officer (joined 2015) → PLU Senior Vice President and Chief Administrative Officer → PLU Acting President (around 2017) → **PLU 14th President** (April 3, 2019 to now, per PLU website in Fall 2025). - **Sanctions or criminal record:** None found.
