## §0 why this entity is on the map Strozier is on this map because he is the elected President of the Tacoma Public Schools (TPS) Board of Directors. He holds Position 3 on the five-member board.

The board runs Tacoma Public Schools. TPS is the second-largest school district in Pierce County. About 28,272 students attended TPS during the 2024 school year.

Strozier worked for TPS from 2008 to 2017. Those nine years inside the district give him strong ties to TPS staff. He now also holds an elected board seat.

## §1 Identity **FACT** Korey Strozier is the President of the Tacoma Public Schools (TPS) Board of Directors. He represents Position 3. His current term ends in November 2029. **Source:** https://www.tacomaschools.org/about/school-board/board-members (primary; reliability 0.95); corroborated by Ballotpedia: https://ballotpedia.org/Tacoma_Public_Schools,_Washington,_elections (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** Strozier joined the board by appointment in November 2020, during the pandemic. Voters then elected him in November 2021. They elected him again in November 2023. The board picked him as President for the 2024–25 and 2025–26 school years. **Source:** https://www.tacomaschools.org/about/school-board/board-members (primary; reliability 0.95); https://tacomaweekly.com/tacoma-public-schools-has-much-to-celebrate-p8958-117.htm (secondary; reliability 0.85); https://www.cwu.edu/about/media-resources/news/2025/04/cwu-alum-elected-president-of-tacoma-public-schools-board-of-directors (secondary; reliability 0.85).

**FACT** Strozier is a TPS graduate of Lincoln High School. He earned an associate's degree at Pierce College. He earned a bachelor's at Central Washington University. He holds a master's in leadership from City University. He is now working on a doctorate in educational leadership. **Source:** https://www.tacomaschools.org/about/school-board/board-members (primary; reliability 0.95); https://www.cwu.edu/about/media-resources/news/2025/04/cwu-alum-elected-president-of-tacoma-public-schools-board-of-directors (secondary; reliability 0.85).

**FACT** From 2008 to 2017, Strozier worked for Tacoma Public Schools. He held hourly, classified, and certificated roles. His jobs included coach, education support specialist, case manager, graduation specialist, and athletic director. **Source:** https://www.tacomaschools.org/about/school-board/board-members (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Outside the board, he is a founding coach with Success Cohorts. That program helps early-career professionals build leadership skills. **Source:** https://www.cwu.edu/about/media-resources/news/2025/04/cwu-alum-elected-president-of-tacoma-public-schools-board-of-directors (secondary; reliability 0.85).

## §2 Power basis **FACT** As Board President, Strozier sets the meeting agenda and runs each meeting. He is the public face of the five-member elected board. The board oversees the superintendent, sets policy, and approves the district budget. The district serves about 28,272 students across 69 schools. **Source:** https://www.tacomaschools.org/about/school-board/board-members (primary; reliability 0.95); https://ballotpedia.org/Tacoma_Public_Schools,_Washington,_elections (primary; reliability 0.9)

**FACT** Voters elect Strozier to the at-large Position 3 seat. His current term ends in November 2029. **Source:** https://ballotpedia.org/Tacoma_Public_Schools,_Washington,_elections (primary; reliability 0.9).

## §7 who they are connected to Strozier is a recent insider turned board leader. His nine years as TPS staff (2008–2017) gave him strong ties inside the district. His current board role gives him standing outside it.

As Board President, he works with the Joint Municipal Action Committee (JMAC). JMAC pulls together Tacoma Public Schools, Tacoma City Council, Pierce County Council, Metro Parks Tacoma, the Puyallup Tribe, Port of Tacoma, Pierce Transit, and the Health Department.
