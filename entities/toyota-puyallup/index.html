## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Toyota of Puyallup is on the map for two reasons. First, the graph already lists it as a donor to Kelly Chambers (weight 3). Second, it sits on Puyallup's River Rd corridor. That corridor is inside the same land-use and downtown-redevelopment zone that Jim Kastama and the Puyallup City Council control. The dealership calls itself the "#1 Volume Toyota dealership in the Puget Sound." That gives it name recognition in any commercial-corridor policy fight. - A few related names sit outside this map. - Glenn Sawyer, the founder, has passed away. He is Kerry's father. He is listed as an open question. - Any sister stores in Tacoma or Federal Way are unverified. They are also open questions.

## 1. Who they are - **Trade name:** Toyota of Puyallup. - **Entity form:** It is a privately-held auto dealership in Washington state. The corporate parent is the Sawyer/Odeman family. The exact legal entity name, likely an LLC, is not listed on the dealer website. It is unverified at the state level. - **Operating address:** 1400 River Rd, Puyallup, WA 98371. This comes from the Toyota of Puyallup website and DealerRater. - **Founded:** 1970. The Facebook page says "Her father was awarded the Toyota franchise in 1970" (Source 2). A 2023 PRWeb release says "Toyota of Puyallup was founded by my father, Glenn Sawyer, in 1970" (Source 3). - **Owner / leadership:** Kerry Sawyer Odeman is the President & General Manager. She calls herself "a second generation auto dealer, serving the South Sound for more than 48 years" (Facebook post, 2017). She is the listed owner on the Our Story page. - **Awards:** A Facebook post says "The only dealership in the state of Washington to receive Toyota's prestigious 'President's Award' for twenty-six [years]." The same 26-time award claim appears on other dealer pages. - **Governing law:** Washington state law on Motor Vehicle Dealers (state law RCW 46.70) covers this dealership. It also has a franchise agreement with Toyota Motor Sales, U.S.A.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Market position:** The dealership's homepage says it is the "#1 Volume Toyota Dealership in the Puget Sound" (Source 1). The Sawyer family has run it since 1970. It is now second-generation. Winning the President's Award for many years shows strong sales volume. - **Political influence:** Federal records show political donations. See Sources. The dealership gave $1,000 to Kelly Chambers (R) on July 12, 2020. It also gave to Hans Zeiger (R-WA) in 2015 and 2016, to Rob McKenna (R) in 2011, and to Reagan Dunn (R) in 2011 (Source 4). - **Civic / community influence:** The dealership's Community Involvement page lists ongoing charity work. It has ties to the Washington Premier Football Club (Source 3, a 2023 Kerry Odeman quote). - **Government contracts / grants:** No public records show direct government contracts. This is normal for a retail car dealer. - **Jobs / land:** Employee count is unverified. The River Rd store is large and likely adds to local jobs and the tax base. But no primary source confirms exact numbers.

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Toyota of Puyallup is a single-store, family-run Toyota franchise. It is a high-volume store and it is a known Republican donor in Pierce County politics. The graph edge to Kelly Chambers (weight 3) is confirmed by a verified $1,000 federal donation on July 12, 2020. The same store gave federal money to Hans Zeiger (R, 25th LD), Rob McKenna (R, former WA AG), and Reagan Dunn (R, King County). Its standalone political weight is low (composite 0.001). But the pattern is clear: this is one of the two main River Rd dealers backing the 25th LD and Pierce County Republican donor pool. The story: Glenn Sawyer got the franchise in 1970. His family ran the store for decades. His daughter Kerry Sawyer Odeman took over and grew it to "#1 volume" status. It has won the President's Award 26 times. It has kept giving to federal candidates. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** No direct city-contract conflict shows up in public records. The store only gives to Republican candidates in the records reviewed. That is consistent, but not a legal problem. - **Analysis of alternatives:** The other reading is that Toyota of Puyallup is just a successful family business. Its political donations are routine civic work. A single $1,000 federal donation is well within normal small-business political activity. That reading is fair. The argument for including it is the pattern. The same store gave to the entire 25th LD Republican group: Zeiger, Chambers, and McKenna-era Pierce Republicans. That fits a dealer-class donor group, not isolated civic donations. - **Confidence:** High — corporate-identity facts and the political-contribution claim to Kelly Chambers are corroborated by primary-source FEC filings via OpenSecrets, with corporate self-disclosure as secondary corroboration; the cross-corroboration across 5 independent sources is sufficient for a tier3 deep profile.
