## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why she is on the map:** Rosie Ayala is included for three reasons. First, she holds office as an elected Pierce County Councilmember for District 4. She votes on the county budget, county laws, and land-use decisions. Second, she makes decisions on several boards, like Pierce Transit, the Greater Tacoma Community Foundation (GTCF), the Puget Sound Regional Council (PSRC), and the Flood Control Zone District. Third, she is well-connected. She is the first Black, Indigenous, or Person of Color (BIPOC) woman to serve on the Pierce County Council. Her district covers downtown Tacoma, Hilltop, South Tacoma, and nearby areas. - **Who is NOT on the map:** Other Pierce County Councilmembers are profiled separately.
