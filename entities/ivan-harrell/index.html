## §0 why this entity is on the map Harrell is in the network because he is the 11th President of Tacoma Community College (TCC). TCC is one of the largest public community colleges in Pierce County. The president runs TCC's strategy, budget, and outside advocacy. Harrell also sits on the American Association of Community Colleges (AACC) board. That gives him a say in national community-college policy.

## §1 Identity **FACT** Ivan L. Harrell II, Ph.D., is the 11th President of Tacoma Community College. He has held the role since May 2018. **Source:** https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/leadership/collegeadministration (primary; reliability 0.95); corroborated by Phi Theta Kappa news: https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/newsroom/_summer2025_spring2026/president-harrell-ptk-award (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** He was 41 when he became TCC president (per the Community College Daily profile). He grew up in Oberlin, Ohio. He was the first in his family to go to college. **Source:** https://www.ccdaily.com/2025/08/younger-presidents-who-have-grown-into-the-roles (secondary; reliability 0.85); https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/leadership/collegeadministration (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** He holds a Ph.D. from Florida State University. His dissertation title was "Using Student Characteristics to Predict the Persistence of Community College Students Enrolled in Online Courses." He also has an M.Ed. from Vanderbilt University and a B.A. from Wittenberg University. **Source:** https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/leadership/collegeadministration (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Before TCC, Harrell worked at several schools. He was at Tallahassee Community College. He was at J. Sargeant Reynolds Community College as Coordinator for Student Affairs. He was Dean of Student Affairs at Anne Arundel Community College. He was VP of Success at Lone Star College. He was EVP of Academic & Student Affairs at Georgia Piedmont Technical College. **Source:** https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/leadership/collegeadministration (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** He has been appointed to the American Association of Community Colleges (AACC) Board of Directors. **Source:** TCC Facebook announcement: https://www.facebook.com/TacomaCC/posts/we-are-so-proud-to-announce-that-tcc-president-dr-ivan-harrell-has-been-appointe/1310409867759923 (secondary; reliability 0.8).

**FACT** In 2026 he received Phi Theta Kappa's Shirley B. Gordon Award of Distinction. That is PTK's top honor for community college presidents. **Source:** https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/newsroom/_summer2025_spring2026/president-harrell-ptk-award (primary; reliability 0.95).

## §2 Power basis **FACT** As TCC President since May 2018, Harrell is the chief executive of a public community college. He runs TCC's strategy, spending, hiring of senior staff, academic programs, and outside advocacy. The Board of Trustees oversees the college. **Source:** https://www.tacomacc.edu/about/leadership/collegeadministration (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** His AACC Board of Directors seat gives him a voice in national community-college policy. Those debates affect federal financial aid and accreditation. They also affect workforce training money. **Source:** https://www.facebook.com/TacomaCC/posts/we-are-so-proud-to-announce-that-tcc-president-dr-ivan-harrell-has-been-appointe/1310409867759923 (secondary; reliability 0.8).
