## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Inclusion rule — from holding office + reputational:** JT Wilcox is on this map for two reasons. First, his reputation. He was the senior leader of the House Republican Caucus (Minority Leader, 2018-2023). In those years, House Republicans shaped budgets, natural-resource policy, and labor law. Those touched Pierce County employers. Second, he holds office. As of Jan. 1, 2026, he is a Port of Tacoma Commissioner (Position #4, term 2026-2029). It is a statutory post under state law (RCW) 53.12. He has direct power over the Port's $300M+ annual operating budget, industrial leases, and maritime commerce in Commencement Bay. - **Adjacent but EXCLUDED candidates:** His predecessor as Minority Leader, Dan Kristiansen, sits outside the Pierce-area map focus. Matt Marshall, who took his 2nd LD House seat, is excluded as a junior legislator without committee-leadership status.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Power currently held (as of 2026):** Port of Tacoma Commissioner, Position #4. Elected 2025. Took office Jan. 1, 2026. Current term: Jan. 1, 2026 – Dec. 31, 2029. Current Commission Office: First Assistant Secretary. RCW 53.12 governs port commissioners. It is a five-person board with six-year staggered terms. The board has statutory power over industrial development, leasing, taxation (via levy lid lifts), and environmental cleanup on Port-owned tideflats. - **Past power:** WA House of Representatives, 2nd District, 2011-2025 (14 years). Minority Leader (Republican) 2018-2023. Committee seats: House Appropriations, House Finance, House Rules (per Wikipedia). He did not run for re-election in 2024. He left office Jan. 13, 2025. - **Inherited / familial / economic base:** Wilcox Family Farms is a five-generation egg producer (since 1909). It is based in Yelm / Pierce County. Wilcox spent 23 years at the farm. He was COO then CFO. He now runs Wilcox Strategies, a government-relations and external-affairs consulting firm. - **Domain questions:** - *Government contracts/grants:* Wilcox Farms has received USDA Farm Service Agency support and WSDA programs. Exact totals are not in the record. As a Port commissioner he now votes on awarding port contracts. - *Land/real-estate holdings:* Port of Tacoma tideflats — the Commissioner has power over industrial leases on Port-owned land. Wilcox Family Farms owns its own farmland in Yelm and nearby Pierce County. - *Jobs controlled:* The Port of Tacoma is the center of marine cargo and warehousing for the South Sound. The commissioner role covers thousands of direct and indirect jobs (longshore, truck, distribution).
