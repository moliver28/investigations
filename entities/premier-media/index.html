## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Inclusion rule:** Reputational + economic. Premier Media Group (PMG) is the top regional business-magazine publisher in the Puget Sound. It runs the Eastside 425 and 425 Business titles. It also runs South Sound and South Sound Business, plus West Sound and two travel titles. - PMG controls a large share of the regional magazine-ad channel. Pierce County businesses use it to reach top decision-makers. PMG owner Josh Dunn also sits on the boards of UW Milgard School of Business and the Association of Washington Business. That gives the company policy and education influence. - **Excluded but related:** The News Tribune (McClatchy), Seattle Times, KING5 and TV stations, Sound Publishing, Gannett / USA Today network, Everett Herald, and individual magazines.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Market consolidation:** PMG is the regional consolidator in business-and-lifestyle magazine publishing. It bought Business Examiner Media in 2021. It bought Northwest Travel & Life in 2022 (per PitchBook). It bought Master Plan Media (Dan Redwine's social-media agency). - **Editorial reach:** Eastside + South Sound + West Sound + travel titles cover the central Puget Sound metro. The reach runs from Bellevue and Tacoma out to Olympia and the Washington coast. - **Industry access:** Josh Dunn sits on two boards. One is the UW Milgard School of Business. The other is the Association of Washington Business (AWB). These seats give PMG a voice in business-education policy. - **Adjacent revenue:** Hallmark events (such as South Sound Business' "Women to Watch") combine editorial coverage with paid-event revenue.

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Premier Media Group is the top privately-held business-and-lifestyle magazine publisher in the central Puget Sound. It owns both the Eastside and South Sound flagship titles. Its deals — Business Examiner Media (2021), Northwest Travel & Life (2022), Master Plan Media — show an active roll-up strategy. PMG does not control daily news in Pierce County. But its grip on monthly business-magazine shelf space gives the Dunns real agenda-setting power over regional business talk. Josh Dunn's board seats at UW Milgard and AWB add policy access. - **Conflicts-of-interest flags:** PMG titles often cover UW Milgard, AWB, the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber, and regional business-award sponsors. PMG leaders sit on boards of many of those same groups. Defense: this is normal in regional trade magazines. - **Analysis of alternatives :** A friendly reading: PMG is a normal regional publisher serving advertisers and readers. A critical reading: PMG acts as a private editorial gatekeeper for the regional business world. Its owner sits on UW and AWB boards. That gives it policy access most regional publishers lack. - **Confidence:** High — multiple PMG-controlled primary sources, Chamber directory listing, and trade-press coverage corroborate the corporate structure, ownership, and portfolio.
