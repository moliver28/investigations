## §0 why this entity is on the map Erika Tucci runs one of the largest private foundations in Pierce County, the Ben B. Cheney Foundation. She also chairs a governor's commission. Both roles give her real power over civic agenda-setting and grantmaking in the South Sound. She used to work at Tacoma Public Utilities. That gives her a bridge into the public-sector utilities world.

## §1 Identity **FACT** Erika Tucci is the Executive Director of the Ben B. Cheney Foundation. She was appointed in November 2023. She took over from Brad Cheney, who served more than 20 years. She is the first woman to lead the foundation. **Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95)

**FACT** Before becoming Executive Director, Tucci served as the Foundation's Senior Program Officer. She has been in that role since 2019. **Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** Tucci previously led community affairs at Tacoma Public Utilities. There, she started a leadership program that encouraged staff to volunteer. **Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** During the pandemic, Tucci started the Pierce County Daily Report. It gave policymakers, nonprofit leaders, and funders coordinated information about human services. **Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95).

**FACT** She chairs the Governor's Serve Washington Commission. **Source:** https://www.benbcheneyfoundation.org/board-of-directors-announces-leadership-transition (primary; reliability 0.95).

## §2 Power basis **FACT** Tucci directs grantmaking at the Cheney Foundation. The Foundation works in the same communities where Cheney Lumber Company once operated. Its EIN (Employer Identification Number, the IRS tax ID) is 91-6053760. It is located at 3110 Ruston Way, Suite A, Tacoma, WA 98402-5308. **Source:** https://fconline.foundationcenter.org/fdo-grantmaker-profile?key=CHEN001 (primary; reliability 0.85)

**FACT** She served as Vice President and President of the Board at CLR (Comprehensive Life Resources). CLR is a mental-health agency with five facilities in Pierce County. It has about $30 million in revenue. She led the board through a CEO transition. **Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/erikatucci (secondary; reliability 0.7).

**FACT** The Cheney Foundation is a member of the Tacoma-Pierce County Chamber (Foundation listing, 3110 Ruston Way). **Source:** https://business.tacomachamber.org/list/member/ben-b-cheney-foundation-12500 (primary; reliability 0.9).
