## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - Chris Gildon is on the map because he holds office and makes decisions. He is the sitting State Senator for the 25th Legislative District. That district covers Puyallup, South Hill, Summit, and Waller in Pierce County. As Senate Republican lead on the Ways and Means Committee (the operating budget committee), he plays a big role in shaping the state's operating budget. That budget directly affects Pierce County funding. He also co-chairs the bipartisan Pierce County legislative delegation. That delegation includes members from both parties and both chambers. - **Adjacent figures not profiled here.** Cyndy Jacobsen (R-25) is the current House member who succeeded Gildon. She is not at this tier. Joyce McDonald (R-25) was the House member Gildon succeeded. She is a former officeholder.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Power held — elected office:** Washington State Senator for the 25th Legislative District. He has served since January 11, 2021. His current term ends January 8, 2029. Before that, he served in the Washington House of Representatives. He represented the 25th District, Position 2, from January 14, 2019 to January 11, 2021 (Source 1, 2). - **Committee authority:** He is the Senate Republican Leader on the Ways and Means Committee. That makes him the lead Republican voice on the state's operating budget. It is one of the most powerful committee assignments in the Senate (Source 2, 5). He also sits on the Housing Committee. - **Additional roles:** Member, Economic and Revenue Forecast Council. Member, Legislative Ethics Board. Co-chair of the Washington State Institute for Public Policy (WSIPP) Board of Directors. Co-chair of the bipartisan Pierce County legislative delegation. Member, Joint Committee on Veterans' and Military Affairs. Member, Legislative Committee on Economic Development and International Relations. Member, Washington State Criminal Sentencing Task Force. Member, Legislative-Executive WorkFirst Poverty Reduction Oversight Task Force (Source 2, 5). - **Budget controlled:** As Senate Republican Operating Budget Leader, he shapes Republican budget proposals. He negotiates with the Democratic majority on the state operating budget, which is about $70 billion for two years. - **Jurisdiction governed:** 25th Legislative District — Puyallup, South Hill, Summit, Waller (Pierce County).

## 1. Who they are - **Verdict:** Gildon's power comes from his role as Senate Republican Operating Budget Leader. It is the top budget job for the minority party in the Washington Senate. The Republican caucus is in the minority, with 19 of 49 seats. Even so, his budget role, his co-chair role on the Pierce County delegation, and his many board seats (WSIPP, Economic and Revenue Forecast Council, Legislative Ethics Board) give him real influence over state spending that affects Pierce County. His military record and real-estate work add weight on veterans' affairs and housing policy. - **Conflict flags:** As a commercial real-estate broker on the Senate Housing Committee, his private work could overlap with his legislative role on housing. The 2020 PDC complaint (over-limit contribution) was cleared without a violation finding, but it shows campaign-finance issues. Recent corporate PAC gifts (T-Mobile, WA Food Industry) while serving as budget leader raise standard access-influence questions. - **Counter-reading:** Gildon's real power is limited by the Republican minority. The Democratic majority controls the budget process. His role is advisory and oppositional rather than final. His committee leadership is "Republican Leader," not chair. The Pierce County delegation co-chair role is mostly coordinative. - **Confidence:** High. Multiple primary sources confirm all key claims. These include the WA Legislature, the Senate Republican Caucus, PDC, and WA Secretary of State certified results. Electoral, financial, and biographical data are well-documented.
