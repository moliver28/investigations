## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - T'wina Nobles is on this map because she is the elected State Senator for the 28th Legislative District in Washington. The district includes Fircrest, University Place, Lakewood, Steilacoom, DuPont, Elk Plain, Camp Murray, and Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM). - She is the Chair of the Higher Education and Workforce Development Committee. She is Vice Chair of the Early Learning and K-12 Education Committee. Those roles give her real power over education policy and state funding for Pierce County schools and colleges. - She is also the only Black member of the Washington State Senate. That gives her a strong voice as a trailblazer and coalition-builder.

## 3. Where their power comes from - **Elected office:** She is a Washington State Senator for the 28th LD. Her term is four years and ends January 8, 2029. She has said she will run for re-election in 2028. - **Committee leadership:** She is Chair of the Higher Education and Workforce Development Committee. That controls bills on higher education, workforce training, and college funding. This matters for Pierce College, Bates Technical College, Clover Park Technical College, and UW Tacoma. She is Vice Chair of the Early Learning and K-12 Education Committee. That shapes education policy for all Pierce County school districts. She also sits on the Transportation Committee. - **Legislative agenda:** Her main bills help foster care children, give mental health sentencing alternatives, and expand college scholarships. For the 2025-26 session, she is focused on housing stability, environmental justice, transportation access, broadband, restoring financial aid cuts for private college students, requiring medication abortion access at public colleges, and requiring diverse history lessons in public schools. - **Budget power:** As committee chair, she shapes state funding for higher education and K-12 programs that flow to Pierce County. Specific dollar amounts were not confirmed in this pass. - **District:** The 28th LD covers Fircrest, University Place, Lakewood, Steilacoom, DuPont, Elk Plain, Camp Murray, JBLM, parts of Tacoma, plus Ketron, Anderson, and McNeil Islands. - **Cross-domain roles:** State senator and committee chair. Career educator and school board member. Former Tacoma Urban League CEO. Founder of Ladies First. Foster care advocate. Member of the Legislative Black Caucus.
