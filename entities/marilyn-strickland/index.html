## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why included:** holds office. Marilyn Strickland is the U.S. Representative for WA-10. Her district covers parts of Pierce and Thurston Counties, including east Tacoma and Olympia. She is a federal officeholder. She has pull over federal money, infrastructure, and military policy. That policy hits Pierce County because JBLM (Joint Base Lewis-McChord, the major Army base on the Pierce-Thurston line) sits there. (Sources 1, 7) - **Who was left out and why:** Denny Heck is her predecessor and now serves as Washington State Treasurer. Victoria Woodards is the current Tacoma Mayor. Strickland once held that mayoral seat. Both have their own profiles and are not the subject here. ## 1. Who they are - **Legal name:** Marilyn Strickland. Korean name: 김순자. - **Date of birth / birthplace:** September 25, 1962, Seoul, South Korea. (Sources 1, 7) - **Current home:** Tacoma, Washington. (Source 3) - **Education:** Mount Tahoma High School, class of 1980. B.A. in Sociology from the University of Washington in 1984. M.B.A. from Clark Atlanta University in 1992. (Sources 1, 7) - **Family / spouse:** Married. She lives in Tacoma with her husband. His name is not in the official bios. (Source 8) - **Career before Congress:** Marketing executive at Starbucks. Helped launch Tacoma's Click! broadband (a city fiber network). Tacoma City Council from 2008 to 2009. Mayor of Tacoma from 2010 to 2017. President and CEO of the Seattle Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce from 2018 to January 31, 2020. (Sources 1, 5, 6) - **Firsts:** First African-American to represent Washington State at the federal level. One of the first three Korean-American women in Congress. (Source 5) ## 3. Where their power comes from - **Elected office:** U.S. Representative for WA-10 since January 3, 2021. That covers the 117th through 119th Congress. She was re-elected in 2022 and 2024. (Sources 1, 7) - **Committee seats:** House Transportation and Infrastructure, and House Armed Services, both for the 117th through 119th Congress. Her subcommittees: Coast Guard and Maritime Transportation. Highways and Transit. Military Personnel. Readiness. (Source 4) - **Caucus leadership:** Secretary of the Congressional Black Caucus. Whip of the New Democrat Coalition. Chair of the New Democrat Coalition National Security Task Force. Executive Board Member of the Congressional Asian Pacific American Caucus. Defense — JBLM is in her district. Healthcare — she voted for the IRA, the Inflation Reduction Act. Business — she is the former Chamber CEO. ## 3.
