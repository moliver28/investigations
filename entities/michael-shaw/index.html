## 0. Why this person or group is on the map - **Why he is here:** Michael Shaw is Pierce County's paid lobbyist in Olympia. Since 1999 he has spoken for the county at the Washington State Legislature. He also runs Shaw — Government Relations, his own firm. It has many public and private clients. That mix puts him at the center of political power in Pierce County and across the state. - **Who is left out:** David Foster, King County's lobbyist, is not on this map. He works for King County, not Pierce County. Other Shaw clients like the Seattle Aquarium and Pacific Science Center are also outside Pierce County.

His clients on the left (like Seattle) give him cross-party access. He has won real money: $9 million for transit, parts of the 2015 transportation package, and $3.2 million for a Pierce County crisis center. His past job as Senate Republican Caucus Attorney (1993–1996) while now lobbying Republican senators is a "revolving door" concern. The FollowTheMoney record for "SHAW, MIKE" with a DC address is unverified. It may be a different person. - **Other reading:** A skeptic would call Shaw a mid-tier contract lobbyist. His Republican staff role ended over 30 years ago.
